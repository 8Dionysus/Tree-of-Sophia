# Access contracts

These files define the portable consumer seam. They do not redefine the
meaning of the ToS projections they carry.

`runtime-manifest.v1.json` names component ownership and profiles;
`runtime-data.v1.json` is the exact standalone data allowlist; and
`web-actions.v1.json` is the read-only page-action ABI shared by the site and
future WebMCP adapter.
