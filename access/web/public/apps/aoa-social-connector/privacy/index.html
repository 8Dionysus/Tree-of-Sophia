Dionysus AoA Social Connector

Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: September 4, 2026

The current preview is owner-operated and limited to accounts explicitly authorized by the developer.

1. Who operates the service

Dionysus operates AoA Social Connector as part of the Agents of Abyss project. Tree of Sophia hosts this public service information. Questions and privacy requests can be submitted through the public support channel. Do not include passwords, access tokens, or private account data in a public issue.

2. Information the connector processes

Only after the operator authorizes access through a platform's official flow, the connector may process:

  • basic account and profile identifiers made available by the authorized API;
  • the operator's boards, Pins, posts, videos, media metadata, and related public or permissioned fields;
  • performance or engagement metrics when the operator grants the relevant permission;
  • drafts, selections, tags, notes, and publication plans created by the operator; and
  • technical logs needed to diagnose errors and protect the connector.

The website itself does not request social-network passwords or access tokens.

3. How information is used

The connector uses authorized data only to:

  • display and organize the operator's own social content;
  • support research, filtering, editorial selection, and draft preparation;
  • perform an operator-requested API action after any required explicit confirmation; and
  • maintain security, reliability, and compliance with platform rules.

The connector does not sell personal data, build advertising profiles, broker data, or use authorized social data to train a general-purpose model.

4. Authorization and credentials

Access is granted through each platform's official authorization mechanism. Where a platform provides revocation controls, the operator may revoke access there at any time. Credentials are stored outside public source repositories and are not intentionally exposed through this website.

5. Sharing and service providers

Authorized social data is not sold or shared for independent marketing. Limited infrastructure providers may process data solely to host or secure the service. Data may also be disclosed when required by law or needed to protect the service, its operator, or others from abuse.

6. Retention and deletion

Authorized data is retained only as long as needed for the operator's active workflow, security, or legal obligations. Local cached data and tokens can be deleted by the operator, and platform authorization can be revoked through the relevant platform. A deletion request may be submitted through the support channel above; identity or account control may need to be verified before action.

7. Security

Reasonable technical controls are used to restrict access to credentials and private working data. No internet-connected system is perfectly secure, so the connector limits its scope and avoids collecting data not needed for its stated purpose.

8. Children

The connector is not directed to children and is not intended to collect children's personal information.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated as supported platforms or product behavior change. The effective date above will be revised, and material changes will be reflected on this page before broader use.

10. Contact

Use the AoA Pinterest Connector support channel for privacy, access, or deletion requests. Do not post secrets or private account content publicly.
