# ToS Charter

## Purpose

Tree of Sophia (ToS) is a source-first living knowledge architecture for
philosophy and world thought. Its research purpose is to measure philosophy:
distinguish its objects, trace relations and transformations, and compare
thought across scales while retaining sources, context and competing readings.

This repository is the canonical public statement of that architecture.
Counts, identity, historical coverage and depth of understanding have distinct
units and evidence. Its representations and methods of understanding are also
subjects of inquiry. A model of thinking remains a research goal; possible
insight into consciousness requires its own evidence beyond accumulating records.

## Mission

ToS exists to support long-horizon cultivation of thought through:

- source-linked textual grounding
- concept and lineage tracing
- context-rich interpretation
- reviewed synthesis by humans and authorized agents
- provenance-aware growth
- durable architectural clarity across time and traditions

## Growth posture

ToS grows by populating a world-philosophy foundation of people, works,
witnesses and their contexts, with evidence-bearing relations. Mathematical
preparation develops alongside that foundation; deeper philological work
builds on its readiness. [ROADMAP](ROADMAP.md) owns the current sequence.

*Thus Spoke Zarathustra* remains the first golden growth kernel: a dense cycle
from source through interpretation and review into authored knowledge. It
teaches a transferable method, while each work and tradition supplies its own
concepts. Ready material can advance without waiting for unrelated gaps.

## What this repository owns

This repository owns ToS-level truth about:

- what ToS is
- what kinds of nodes and relations belong in ToS
- what source-first discipline should remain stable
- how raw material, derived extraction, and reviewed synthesis stay distinct
- how ToS relates to AoA, `aoa-kag`, and `abyss-stack`

## Related owners

The AoA constitution belongs to `Agents-of-Abyss`; ecosystem deployment belongs to
`abyss-stack`; derived knowledge infrastructure belongs to `aoa-kag`; typed
routing belongs to `aoa-sdk`. ToS supplies source-traceable knowledge to those
systems through its declared handoffs. These are independent consumers, not
prerequisites for ToS operation, export, validation or release. ToS does not
install or invoke their checkouts, skills, builders or validators.

## Core principles

- sources before abstraction
- lineages before isolated fragments
- source-visible review and explicit authority before scoped admission
- explicit provenance over floating interpretation
- plural scope without silent flattening
- growth through explicit structure
- stable evidence beneath versioned interpretation
- gold examples that preserve judgment, refusal, and uncertainty

[Knowledge Assessment](ToS/doctrine/KNOWLEDGE_ASSESSMENT.md) owns the concrete
human/agent competence, authority and admission rules. A judgment is assessed
through its evidence, competence and use scope. Rights, consent, publication
and canon keep their separate owner decisions.

## Role in the broader ecosystem

Treat `Tree-of-Sophia` as:

- the source-authored knowledge architecture
- the public conceptual root of ToS
- the place where source-first meaning and structural discipline are named

Treat related repositories as follows:

- `Agents-of-Abyss` is the operational federation around ToS
- `aoa-kag` is the derived knowledge substrate built from authoritative sources
- `abyss-stack` owns ecosystem deployment and can consume independently released ToS artifacts

## Long-term direction

If ToS matures successfully, it should remain source-first even as derivative and operational systems become more capable.

ToS cultivates a living, traceable architecture of thought whose source-owned
meaning remains available through each operational and derived system.
