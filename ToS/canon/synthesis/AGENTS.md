# AGENTS.md

This card applies to authored `synthesis` nodes under `ToS/canon/synthesis/`.

## Role

Reviewed interpretive syntheses and bounded commentary-like readings.

## Editing posture

Keep synthesis route-local, source-linked and explicitly interpretive;
principle and source canon retain their own routes.
The parent canon card owns object/source-anchor and review rules. Its worked
mirror for this class is `ToS/public-compatibility/synthesis_node.example.json`.
