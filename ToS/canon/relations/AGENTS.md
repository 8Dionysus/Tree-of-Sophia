# AGENTS.md

This card applies to canonical relation packs under `ToS/canon/relations/`.

## Role

Reviewed route-local relation layers are carried as deterministic `edges.csv`
packs. Node payloads stay in their owning canon classes.

## Editing posture

Use canonical `tos.*` IDs in `from_id` and `to_id` and predicates from the
current registries. Unresolved relation residue remains in candidate intake
until review; a local alias cannot replace a registered predicate.

## Validation

Select `canon_contracts` from [ToS/VALIDATION.md](../../VALIDATION.md).
Relation-weaving and export owners retain their affected procedures.
