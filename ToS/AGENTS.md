# AGENTS.md

This card applies to the authored `ToS/` source home.

## Role

`ToS/` owns philosophical meaning and its source-to-review history. Root guides
orient the repository; `docs/decisions/` explains durable choices;
`mechanics/` owns repeatable operations.

## Read First

For source-home structure, use `ToS/source_home.manifest.json`. Knowledge law
lives in `ToS/doctrine/KNOWLEDGE_MODEL.md` and
`ToS/doctrine/NODE_CONTRACT.md`; read the selected contract and nearest branch
card. Open `ToS/README.md` when human navigation is relevant.

## Boundary Routes

Classify a kernel or corpus artifact before editing it. Its identity, content,
claim, assessment and publication state retain their separate owners. A gold
slice includes inspectable accepted, rejected and unresolved readings and their
grounds. Use `doctrine/CORPUS_FOUNDATION.md` for corpus identity and address law;
processing tools and storage remain explicitly selected operations. Every
derived layer cites its exact input digest and transformation event.

Move through the relevant source and review owners before promoting canon or
rebuilding a stronger public representation. An unrepresentable source returns
to doctrine/contracts with its uncertainty intact.

## Validation

Select the owning source-home, philosophy, canon, contract, lived-witness or
export route from [ToS/VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md). Structural checks do not
replace the source-visible assessment or actual owner decision.
