# Work boundaries in the Mysl 1996 volume 2 witness

This directory records the source-visible work sequence of the exact local PDF
item without copying its text layer into Git.

`work-boundary-map.json` separates the seven contained works, their Russian
translation expressions, and the non-work notes section. `anchors.jsonl`
addresses the seven title pages, the notes boundary, and the two table-of-
contents pages in the fixity-bound PDF. The title-page positions agree with the
work order and start pages in the table of contents; the table of contents also
supplies the displayed translator credits.

All anchors, membership claims, responsibility claims, and inferred end pages
remain model-made and unreviewed. They establish a proposed bibliographic
structure for the local witness only. They do not establish accepted source
text, translation quality, textual equivalence to another edition, semantic
units, rights clearance, or canon.
