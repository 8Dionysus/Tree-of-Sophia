# Access Request Letter Template

This is a draft template. Do not send it without a real human reviewing the
recipient, scope, promises, legal terms, and final wording.

**Subject:** Research access request for `[exact work / edition / resource]`

Dear `[institution or rights team]`,

I am writing on behalf of Tree of Sophia, a source-first research project for
studying philosophical works through verifiable bibliography, textual
witnesses, translation, semantic annotation, indexing, and graph projections.

We are researching:

- exact material: `[title, author, editor/translator, edition, date, IDs]`;
- requested portion: `[complete resource / articles / pages / metadata]`;
- preferred format: `[PDF / XML / TEI / images / API / other]`;
- research purpose: `[specific bounded question and experiment]`.

We request permission only for the checked purposes below:

- [ ] local research access;
- [ ] local OCR or transcription;
- [ ] local lexical or semantic indexing;
- [ ] local embeddings or other computational analysis;
- [ ] quotation of explicitly limited excerpts with citation;
- [ ] publication of metadata and provenance only;
- [ ] publication of named derived data: `[describe exactly]`;
- [ ] processing on a controlled research server;
- [ ] another purpose: `[state exactly]`.

The material would be stored in access-controlled local project storage. We
will not redistribute source files, full text, scans, or restricted derivatives
without explicit permission. Every use will retain provenance, citation,
version, access, and rights conditions. We can remove material and derived
outputs if permission expires or is withdrawn.

Could you please confirm:

1. whether you control or can grant the requested access and uses;
2. the exact permitted material and purposes;
3. citation, attribution, security, retention, publication, and takedown terms;
4. any time limit, fee, institutional agreement, or reporting requirement;
5. the appropriate contact for future rights or takedown questions?

We are happy to narrow the request or provide a fuller research description.

Sincerely,

`[real human name and contact — private correspondence only, never committed]`
