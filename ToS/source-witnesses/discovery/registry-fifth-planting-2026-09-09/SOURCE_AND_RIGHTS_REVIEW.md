The opening begins with the invocation to Venus and supplies a Latin edition/body identity; this establishes a readable exact digital witness, not a reconstructed print bibliography. Keep the unknown print source visible in every version route and rights uncertainty. A29's source owner must obtain explicit exemplar evidence before assigning a historical critical edition.
- **Academica / Lucullus:** the provider has separate CTS entries drawn from the same Plasberg volume. This pass selects the supplied `Academica` file only. The surviving/redaction/part relationship with `Lucullus` needs its own identity review; it is not silently turned into either two unrelated complete works or one undifferentiated file. Supplied completeness never restores lost ancient books.
- **De re publica:** preserve the supplied edition's gaps, reconstruction and apparatus. No claim to the intact ancient six-book work follows from having the full XML file.
- **De divinatione:** select Mueller's P5 `perseus-lat1` edition. The other `perseus-lat2` candidate uses TEI P4; its exact metadata is retained for separate version and legacy-adapter review. It is not described as corrupt merely because the P5 parser does not accept its envelope.
- **De legibus:** the CTS record describes a 1959 Plinval edition, but the retained [tracking record](evidence/phi0474.phi044.perseus-lat1.tracking.json) says `not migrated`, and the pinned tree has no corresponding XML. Metadata-only evidence is not a retained text. The owner should follow another exact source route and assess its rights before acquisition.
- **Seneca Dialogi and Naturales quaestiones:** the corresponding `phi012` and `phi016` directories/files are absent from this complete pinned tree. No global absence or unavailability of the works is asserted; the next source-owner step is another reviewed edition/provider.
- **Seneca letters:** retain metadata, defer the full epistolary corpus until collection and constituent letter identity are prepared under the existing source route. **Apocolocyntosis:** retain metadata, defer until the A29 owner supplies the specific satire/political-reception source need.

These seven deferrals retain owner and next condition in the selection record. The current source witness intake does not generate new abstract canon nodes or adopt the reported schools, dialogue speakers or polemical positions as author doctrine.
