# Aristotle, On the Soul: Wallace 1882

- Digital source: https://aristotle.johnhboyer.com/DA/
- Historical scan: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Aristotle%27s_psychology_(IA_b24866660).pdf
- Exact captures and scan digests: source metadata snapshot.
- All 30 chapters are present in the three book pages. Reading text is extracted from the Aristotle Reader Wallace fields, with chapter and paragraph boundaries reassembled from site segments. The site gives approximate alignment to Greek; no sentence or Bekker-line alignment is asserted here. The digital text contains apparent OCR and punctuation errors. Openings and sample passages were checked against the 1882 scan; no full scan collation or critical-edition review is claimed. The scan and exact captured HTML are retained locally; the HTML also contains a different Greek text and English translation, which are not included in this reading.

- External mixed-content HTML captures remain outside the public payload. Separate events bind their digests to the retained Wallace-only chapter extraction, and bind the latter to the packaged reading; both scripts are retained. Reassembly reproduced the reading JSON byte for byte.
