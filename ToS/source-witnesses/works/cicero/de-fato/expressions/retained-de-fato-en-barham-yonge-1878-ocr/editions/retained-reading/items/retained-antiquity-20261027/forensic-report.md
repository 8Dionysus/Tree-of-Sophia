# Cicero, On Fate: Barham / Yonge 1878

- Source: https://archive.org/details/treatisesofcicer00ciceuoft
- Scan: https://archive.org/download/treatisesofcicer00ciceuoft/treatisesofcicer00ciceuoft.pdf
- OCR: https://archive.org/download/treatisesofcicer00ciceuoft/treatisesofcicer00ciceuoft_djvu.txt
- Exact digests, source chain, extraction script digest and spot-review locations are in source metadata.
- English translation of the surviving continuous treatise, chapters I–XX, from the 1878 edition. The ancient beginning and ending are lost and an internal gap is noted in the edition. This version does not include separately transmitted fragments or testimonia represented in some modern editions. It is an OCR-derived reading: running headers and two footnotes were separated, selected obvious OCR slips and line-break word splits were corrected, and source footnotes were retained; other OCR mistakes, broken hyphenation, archaic forms and mistranscribed Greek may remain. The opening, a middle sample, and ending were checked against scan PDF pages 274, 281, 285, 288 and 292; the full text has not been collated. Chapter numbering is that of the 1878 edition and does not assert alignment to modern numbered sections.

- The unchanged PDF OCR layout for pages 274–292 and the exact correction script are separate retained payload files with independent digests. Separate extraction and correction provenance events bind the predecessor and corrected JSON.
