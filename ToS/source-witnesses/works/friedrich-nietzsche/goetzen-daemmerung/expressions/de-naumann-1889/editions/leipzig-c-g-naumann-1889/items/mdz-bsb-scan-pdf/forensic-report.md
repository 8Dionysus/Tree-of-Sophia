## Scholarly production history

Sommer's official HAdW commentary records that Nietzsche sent the manuscript to
Naumann on 7 September 1888, continued title and content changes, and received
four completed copies on 24 November after printing ended on 13 November. The
1889-dated work became commercially available at the end of January 1889.
Köselitz's 1893 second edition introduced heading and textual changes.

That history makes this exact 1889 witness materially stronger for
author-proximate work than the later 1906 collected edition. It does not prove
that every character in the scan equals a critical text.
