## Editorial boundary

The digital object contains two materially different authored layers:

1. Nietzsche's work through printed page 130.
2. Richter's separately titled editor's afterword from printed page 133
   through page 154.

The title leaf and blank transition make this boundary source-visible. The
resource inventory describes physical PDF pages only; any later transcription,
index, graph, or semantic projection must preserve the change of responsibility
instead of flattening both layers into Nietzsche's voice.

Established critical scholarship further shows that the 1908 edition largely
follows the Köselitz editorial copy and is not the strongest reconstruction of
Nietzsche's intended text. The item is therefore historical soil and an exact
edition witness, not an author-final or critical-text authority.
