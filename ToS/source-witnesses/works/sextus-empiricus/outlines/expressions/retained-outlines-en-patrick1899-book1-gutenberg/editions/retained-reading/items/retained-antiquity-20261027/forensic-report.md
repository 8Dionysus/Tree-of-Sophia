# Sextus Empiricus: Patrick 1899, Book I only

- Source: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/17556
- Scan: https://archive.org/details/cu31924029002561
- Exact digests and scan spot reviews are in source metadata.
- Only Book I is included: Patrick translated the first of the three books. This does not supply Books II–III. Chapters I–XXXIV cover the complete Book I in the 1899 edition. Project Gutenberg’s ASCII Greek transliterations are retained in [Greek: ...] brackets, not replaced with reconstructed Greek. Its 241 marginal section numerals were removed from the reading; chapter structure does not claim section-level alignment. The Gutenberg text retains its original spelling and may contain digitization errors. The opening and ending were checked against scan PDF pages 115 and 175, and the spurious inline 6 in chapter II was removed after comparison with PDF page 116; there has been no full collation.

- An uncorrected Book I substring and the exact correction script are separately retained with digests. Separate extraction and correction events bind the predecessor and reading JSON.
