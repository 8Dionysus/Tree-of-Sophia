# AGENTS.md

This file applies to Deep Research packet families under
`ToS/research-packets/deep-research/`.

Deep Research output is a research scaffold. It can suggest branches, names,
questions, and leads. Primary source authority routes to source witnesses;
philosophical authority routes to reviewed branch, doctrine, and canon
surfaces.

Select the relevant `philosophy_topology` or `source_home` route in
[`ToS/VALIDATION.md`](../../VALIDATION.md) after the packet surface is known.
