## Published source and bounded observation

Andreas Urs Sommer, *Kommentar zu Nietzsches Jenseits von Gut und Böse*,
Historischer und kritischer Kommentar zu Friedrich Nietzsches Werken 5/1,
Berlin/Boston: De Gruyter, 2016, pp. 5–6. The consulted carrier is the
Heidelberger Akademie der Wissenschaften / Heidelberg University Library
[page 5](https://digi.hadw-bw.de/view/nietzsche_kom5_1/0025) and
[page 6](https://digi.hadw-bw.de/view/nietzsche_kom5_1/0026),
DOI [10.11588/diglit.69929](https://doi.org/10.11588/diglit.69929).

Sommer reports that Nietzsche accepted Naumann's offer on 3 June 1886 and
ordered work to begin immediately. His cited letter locator is
KSB 7 / KGB III/3, no. 705, p. 193. The preceding account places the decision
in Nietzsche's plan to finance publication himself after unsuccessful
publisher negotiations. Page 6 separately reports typesetting, correction
and printing during June–July, a distribution request on 2 August and a
letter reporting receipt of copies on 4 August. These are distinct actions,
not interchangeable dates of public release.
