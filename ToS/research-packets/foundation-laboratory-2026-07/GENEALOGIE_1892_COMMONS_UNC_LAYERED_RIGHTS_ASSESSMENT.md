# Genealogie 1892 Wikimedia Commons and UNC Layered Rights Assessment

Status: model-authored rights research; exact acquired Commons revision and
current provider state reconciled; no legal advice, human rights review,
source-text acceptance, operator transfer approval, or publication decision

Research snapshot: 2026-08-02

## Question

What may Tree of Sophia say about the exact Wikimedia Commons PDF derived from
the Internet Archive scan of the University of North Carolina copy of the 1892
second Naumann edition of *Zur Genealogie der Moral*?

The file is not one legally homogeneous object. It contains or represents at
least:

1. Nietzsche's underlying Work;
2. the historical 1892 second-Edition presentation;
3. faithful images of historical book pages;
4. a physical binding and marbled covers whose date and maker are unresolved;
5. UNC holding furniture, including a bookplate or card, marks, barcode, due
   slip, and stamps;
6. automatically recognized historical Nietzsche text embedded in the PDF;
7. OCR position, page-layout, and PDF derivative structure;
8. the Commons structured file metadata;
9. the Commons unstructured description and revision prose;
10. the Internet Archive lineage metadata and current derivative package;
11. the operator's local custody and the future site's independent admission
    policy.

## Result

The historical Work and 1892 Edition presentation are
`public_domain_reviewed` for the bounded German and United States routes. The
faithful scan of the historical pages receives the same result. Current German
book-scan guidance and United States human-authorship doctrine converge on the
absence of a new copyright in a merely faithful mechanical reproduction, and
the exact Commons file revision is marked public domain and
`Copyrighted=False`.

The automatically recognized historical Nietzsche text is also
`public_domain_reviewed` as a mechanical rendering of public-domain material.
This is a rights result only. It does not establish OCR accuracy, German
correctness, reading order, textual authority, or acceptance into ToS.

The exact PDF cannot receive a positive aggregate result. Source-visible pages
contain a physical binding and marbled covers of unresolved date and origin,
as well as modern and mixed-date UNC holding furniture. The PDF adds OCR
coordinates, layout, wrapper, and derivative-package structure for which no
exact operative license was found. Internet Archive exposes no rights or
license value for the current source Item. Commons separately applies CC0 to
structured file-namespace data and CC BY-SA 4.0 to unstructured page prose;
those provider metadata licenses do not clear the book scan's other layers.

The Commons description currently invokes only `PD-US-expired`. The template
itself warns that a non-United-States work also needs a source-country basis;
the exact page does not presently encode a German tag. ToS does not treat this
incomplete provider expression as either a complete worldwide grant or a
reason to ignore the independent German term analysis.

The aggregate record therefore becomes `conflicting_evidence`, not
`public_domain_reviewed`, `licensed`, or `in_copyright`. The operator-held PDF
remains `local_only`, `not_authorized`, and `local_research_only`. A future
public candidate must be independently reacquired from an exact then-current
upstream route and pass fresh fixity, layer, rights, human-review, and operator
approval gates. It must not be copied from the local ToS payload.

## Classical and official documentation

### Exact provider surface

The current [Commons file
page](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Zur_Genealogie_der_Moral_-_eine_Streitschrift_(IA_zurgenealogieder00niet).pdf)
and API are the exact object surfaces. The page links the Internet Archive
lineage and displays a public-domain result. Its current wikitext uses
[`PD-US-expired`](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Template:PD-US-expired),
whose own documentation says that a foreign work must also be free in its
source country or carry an applicable source-country license. The absence of a
German tag is recorded as incomplete provider expression, not silently filled
by ToS.

Commons' current [PD-scan
guidance](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:When_to_use_the_PD-scan_tag)
distinguishes faithful mechanical scanning and automatic enhancement from
creative selective enhancement. That supports only the faithful historical
page layer, not binding design, holding furniture, OCR arrangement, package,
or metadata prose.

The current Commons file-page footer separates its provider contributions.
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) governs
structured file-namespace data, while [CC BY-SA
4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) governs unstructured
text under the stated page terms. These licenses are recorded in distinct
metadata layers; neither is treated as a license for the exact PDF package.
Wikimedia's current [Terms of
Use](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Terms_of_Use) also leave
reuse responsibility with the reuser and provide no exact-object warranty.

### Germany

The current German Copyright Act supplies the bounded legal route:

- [UrhG §64](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__64.html) provides the
  ordinary life-plus-seventy term. Nietzsche died in 1900, so that route ended
  at 1970-12-31.
- [§66](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__66.html) supplies the
  anonymous and pseudonymous publication route. Even a conservative 1892
  historical-presentation calculation ended at 1962-12-31.
- [§70](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__70.html) protects qualifying
  scientific editions for twenty-five years, while
  [§72](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__72.html) addresses
  photographs. Neither establishes a live term for the source-visible 1892
  historical presentation.
- [§68](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__68.html) concerns a narrow
  class of reproductions of public-domain visual works and is not expanded into
  a blanket rule for every book, package, OCR, or metadata layer.
- [§87a](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__87a.html) keeps database
  investment separate from copyright in individual facts and old text.

Deutsche Digitale Bibliothek's current [license and rights-statement
guidance](https://pro.deutsche-digitale-bibliothek.de/daten-liefern/teilnahmekriterien/rechtliches/lizenzen-und-rechtehinweise-der-lizenzkorb-der-deutschen-digitalen-bibliothek)
allows Public Domain Mark where the source is public domain and the digital
object is a reproduction. Its [digital-images
guidance](https://pro.deutsche-digitale-bibliothek.de/daten-liefern/teilnahmekriterien/rechtliches/das-urheberrecht-digitalen-abbildungen)
explains that a pure reproduction of a two-dimensional source generally
creates no new copyright. ToS applies this only to faithful historical pages.

### United States

The United States Copyright Office's current
[Circular 38B](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ38b.pdf) and
[17 U.S.C. §104A](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html#104a) provide
the foreign-work restoration route. A foreign work could be restored only if,
among cumulative requirements, it was not public domain through expiration of
term in its eligible source country on the restoration date. Nietzsche's Work
and even the conservative 1892 presentation route were already public domain
through expiration in Germany by 1 January 1996, so they fail that cumulative
restoration condition. ToS does not rely only on the domestic pre-1931
shortcut for this German publication.

The current [Circular
15A](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ15a.pdf) provides the general duration
table. The Copyright Office's [Compendium, Third
Edition](https://www.copyright.gov/comp3/) requires human authorship and
excludes mere scanning or digitization from copyrightable authorship. This
supports the faithful-scan and automatic historical-text layers but does not
clear modern furniture, creative enhancement, prose, arrangement, marks, or a
database or package as a whole.

## Established scholarship, cases, and institutional practice

The established sources support separation rather than blanket permission:

- [*Bridgeman Art Library v.
  Corel*](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/district-courts/FSupp2/36/191/2413183/)
  found no United States originality in exact photographic copies of
  public-domain two-dimensional works. It is a district-court decision, not a
  worldwide rule.
- [*Authors Guild v.
  Google*](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/ca2/13-4829/13-4829-2015-10-16.html)
  upheld bounded scanning, indexing, search, and snippets as fair use. It is
  not a full-file redistribution license for ToS.
- Europeana's updated 2025 [Public Domain
  Charter](https://pro.europeana.eu/post/the-europeana-public-domain-charter)
  argues that digitization should not recreate control over public-domain
  material and that rights should be stated accurately. It is policy, not the
  exact file's contract.
- Deutsche Digitale Bibliothek and Wikimedia Commons supply mature
  institutional practice for faithful reproduction, source-country checks,
  and layer-aware rights statements. Their guidance does not replace exact
  provenance or jurisdictional review.

## Server consequence

The server-import plan remains `rights-unknown`, `metadata-only`,
`blocked-rights`, and operator-unapproved. Positive DE/US findings for the
historical and faithful mechanical layers do not authorize payload transfer,
server OCR, transcription, page images, snippets, embeddings, alignments,
translations, source-bearing annotations, or content-derived search.

Only public-safe ToS bibliographic, provenance, source-route, and layer-status
metadata plus source-attributed licensed Commons metadata may remain
conditional for lexical, search, and graph projections. No provider row,
source text, page image, OCR coordinate, or local payload is included.

A future exact public candidate must be reacquired from the then-current
Commons route, compared by new fixity, and inspected again by layer. The
current Internet Archive revision is not an automatic substitute. A later
ToS-built package might omit unresolved binding or holding furniture and use
only independently cleared historical pages, but that would be a new Item
with new provenance and a separate publication decision.

## What this does not prove

This assessment does not prove:

- German orthographic, grammatical, textual, OCR, or reading-order correctness;
- that the second edition is author-final, critical, or preferable to the 1887
  first print, manuscripts, proofs, correction sheets, or a critical edition;
- that Commons' provider statement is a worldwide warranty;
- that `PD-US-expired` alone is sufficient for a German work;
- that the current Internet Archive PDF is the exact acquired Commons file;
- that UNC granted a file-, furniture-, mark-, or package-specific license;
- that every binding, mark, coordinate, layout, metadata, or provider layer is
  public domain;
- that public-domain historical text is an accepted ToS transcription;
- that local possession authorizes publication or server transfer;
- that a green validator can replace source-visible or human review.

The narrow durable result is a reproducible, eleven-layer rights model whose
positive findings are retained without converting unresolved mixed layers into
permission.
