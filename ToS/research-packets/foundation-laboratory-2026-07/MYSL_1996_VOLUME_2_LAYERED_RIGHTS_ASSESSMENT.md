## Question

What may Tree of Sophia say about the exact operator-provided PDF of Friedrich
Nietzsche's *Сочинения в двух томах*, volume 2, published by `Мысль` in Moscow
in 1996?

The answer cannot be inherited from Nietzsche's death, a historical
translator's death, the Edition year, a public download route, or a single
publisher notice. The exact object contains at least eleven analytically
distinct layers:

1. the seven underlying Nietzsche Works;
2. the historical Antonovsky translation substrates represented by
   *Zarathustra* and *Ecce Homo*;
3. the historical Polilov translation substrates represented by *Beyond Good
   and Evil*, *The Case of Wagner*, and *Twilight of the Idols*;
4. the historical Flerova translation substrate represented by
   *The Antichrist*;
5. Karen Svasyan's Russian translation of *On the Genealogy of Morality*;
6. Svasyan's compilation, Edition-wide editing, and notes;
7. the publisher's claimed Edition-level contribution or arrangement;
8. the Nietzsche portrait and Edition presentation;
9. the 2011 digital page scan;
10. the ABBYY embedded text layer; and
11. the exact mixed PDF aggregate.

## Result

The seven underlying Nietzsche Works are `public_domain_reviewed` in the
bounded Russian and United States routes already established by the
source-witness foundation. Nietzsche died in 1900. The Russian historical
endpoint was 1950; for the United States foreign-work route, the German
source-country life-plus-seventy endpoint was 1970, before possible 1996
restoration. This positive result does not select or clear a modern critical
German text, Russian Expression, Edition, scan, or PDF.

Antonovsky's historical translation contributions are separately
`public_domain_reviewed`. He died in 1913 and the former Russian fifty-year
term ended in 1963, before the 1993 transition and possible United States
restoration. The exact 1996 volume visibly credits him, but no collation proves
that the represented *Zarathustra* or *Ecce Homo* text equals an 1898, 1900,
1903, 1907, 1911, 1913, or other historical Expression. The positive result is
therefore a substrate result, not clearance of the exact 1996 text.

The Polilov route can now be bounded more strongly than the earlier
`copyright_not_evaluated` record. GND `1012315509` establishes a Nietzsche
translator `Polilov, N.` and connects him to *Beyond Good and Evil*. RSL and
RuNEB bridge `Н. Н. Полилов` to `Николай Николаевич Полилов` in an explicit
translator role. Current historical-reference surfaces independently agree on
1864–1907. The former Russian fifty-year term therefore ended in 1957, before
the transition and United States restoration boundaries. His historical
translation substrates are `public_domain_reviewed` for RU/US, but the GND
record itself supplies no life dates, the current conclusion remains
model-assessed, and no collation proves exact 1996 continuity. Nothing in this
result clears later editing or the operator PDF.

V. A. Flerova remains initials-only. The RSL record for the 1907
*Антихристианин* confirms the historical translation attribution but supplies
no full identity or death date. General-web candidates that expand her to Vera
Aleksandrovna Flerova, born in 1913, are chronologically impossible and remain
rejected. Any United States restored publication-based term for the 1907
historical publication would have ended no later than 2002, but the Russian
life-term operand cannot be calculated. The layer therefore remains
`copyright_undetermined`, not public domain by guesswork.

The source-visible title page identifies Karen Svasyan as compiler, editor,
author of notes, and translator. GND `120452367` currently records
`Swassjan, Karen (1948-2024)`. His qualifying translation, compilation,
editing, and notes contributions remain `in_copyright` in RU/US under the
ordinary life-plus-seventy route, with a model-calculated endpoint of
2094-12-31. That endpoint is contributor-specific: it does not decide whether
every correction, fact, selection, or typographic choice is independently
protected, and it does not prove contractual ownership.

The source visibly states `© Издательство «Мысль». 1996`. This is evidence of
a claimed recent contribution, not evidence that the publisher owns every
Work, translation, editorial, visual, scan, and OCR layer. No contract,
transfer instrument, exact contribution statement, or reuse license was
found. The publisher layer remains `copyright_undetermined` and blocks
clearance without becoming a blanket ownership claim.

PDF page 2 visibly contains a Nietzsche portrait, and the catalog describes
one portrait leaf. The exact photograph source, photographer, first
publication, credit, Edition contribution, and rights chain are absent. The
portrait and presentation layer remains `copyright_undetermined`; Nietzsche's
identity and death date cannot determine a photographer's term.

The PDF metadata names `ABBYY FineReader 10`, later `iTextSharp 5.0.6`, and a
2011 digital creation/modification date. The scan and embedded text are kept as
separate `copyright_undetermined` layers. Current Russian and United States
guidance supports only the bounded proposition that a purely automatic
technical operation would add no authored contribution. It does not prove
that this production was wholly automatic, establish its creator or license,
or clear the active Svasyan material it reproduces. The embedded text is also
visibly errorful and remains unaccepted OCR regardless of copyright status.

The exact 24,126,572-byte PDF aggregate is `in_copyright` because it reproduces
the active Svasyan translation, compilation, editing, and notes contributions.
Unresolved publisher, portrait, presentation, scan, OCR, acquisition, and
permission layers add further closed boundaries. Even a future finding that
the scan or OCR itself is non-original would not remove rights in the content
reproduced by the file.

The top-level record therefore becomes `in_copyright`, not
`copyright_not_evaluated`, while staying `local_only`, redistribution
`not_authorized`, derivatives `local_research_only`, and human review
`unreviewed`. This is a better-supported closed boundary, not a transfer or
publication permission.

## Classical and official documentation

### Russia

The current consolidated Russian Civil Code surface was checked on
2026-08-11. It identifies the text as amended through 23 July 2025, with the
relevant changes effective 4 January 2026. No later demonstrated amendment
changing this layer analysis was found.

- [Article 1260](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_64629/26eaf5de7ca59025f4388fe2980d3dd03dd5e775/)
  protects qualifying translation and compilation contributions independently
  from the pre-existing material and limits the compiler's contribution to
  selection or arrangement rather than the underlying works.
- [Article 1281](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_64629/675b81f0bc9b8ecd68ea332ecfd9d1562541fb98/)
  supplies the ordinary life-plus-seventy term and preserves the transition
  rule where the former fifty-year term had not expired by 1 January 1993.
- [Article 1282](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_64629/366db9d9c7215d66ada95405f1654967f2ae5e4b/)
  permits use after expiry while preserving authorship, name, and integrity.
- [Federal Law 231-FZ, Article 6](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/6787)
  is the transition source used for the historical Antonovsky and Polilov
  operands.
- [Supreme Court Plenum No. 10 of 23 April 2019](https://www.vsrf.ru/files/27771/)
  separates human creative work from a purely technical result. It prevents
  ToS both from inventing scan/OCR authorship and from presuming that an
  undocumented digital process was purely automatic.

The current record therefore calculates only contributors whose identity and
term operands are sufficiently bounded. It leaves Flerova, the publisher
scope, portrait, presentation, scan, and OCR unresolved.

### United States

The current Copyright Office [Title 17
edition](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/) includes amendments enacted
through 18 December 2025. In [Chapter
1](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html), §103 limits derivative and
compilation protection to the new material and does not enlarge protection in
the pre-existing material. Section 104 covers qualifying treaty-party works,
while §104A supplies the source-country restoration route for historical
foreign works. [Chapter
3](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap3.html) supplies the ordinary
post-1977 author term used for Svasyan's qualifying recent contributions.

The Copyright Office's [Limitation of Claim
guidance](https://www.copyright.gov/eco/help-limitation.html) treats
translations and sufficiently original editorial revisions as possible new
contributions while excluding pre-existing material from the new claim. The
current [Compendium, Third
Edition](https://www.copyright.gov/comp3/docs/compendium.pdf) supports the same
human-authorship boundary for digital production. Neither source grants a
license for this Edition or file.

## Established scholarship and practice

Thomas Margoni's established study, [*The digitisation of cultural heritage:
originality, derivative works and (non) original
photographs*](https://www.ivir.nl/publicaties/download/1507.pdf), explains why
digitisation requires separate analysis of the source object's rights and any
new originality in the digital representation. That method directly supports
the ToS separation of the 1996 contributions, page scan, ABBYY text, and PDF
aggregate.

The study and current official authorship guidance support a narrow negative:
a demonstrably automatic faithful process does not become a new authored work
merely because software produced a file. They do not prove the facts of this
undocumented 2011 workflow, grant reuse permission, validate the OCR, or clear
the content copied into it.

## Consequence for ToS

This Item demonstrates that one aggregate can contain public-domain historical
substrates and still remain closed because the exact Expressions, Edition,
notes, visuals, scan, OCR, and package do not share one rights status. ToS may
use the registered file for bounded local research, OCR comparison, structure
recovery, translation comparison, and provenance-aware experiments under the
operator's local policy. It may track public-safe bibliographic facts, exact
fixity, contributor roles, source-return addresses, and layer-status metadata.

It may not transfer or publish the local PDF, page images, OCR, source text,
snippets, embeddings, alignments, translations, or source-bearing annotations.
A future website route requires a separately reacquired exact public object,
current fixity, current exact-scope license or permission, human rights review,
content-quality and source-admission review, and explicit operator approval.

No rights validator or green test can replace those judgments. The record and
tests prove only that the closed boundary is consistently represented.
