## Result first

The current [RuNEB record](https://rusneb.ru/catalog/000199_000009_003693382/)
identifies an exact printed Edition:

- *Так говорил Заратустра*;
- Friedrich Nietzsche;
- Saint Petersburg;
- the literal typography string `тип. Ф. Вайсберга и П. Гершунина`;
- year 1907;
- RuNEB code `000199_000009_003693382`;
- the Russian State Library as source institution.

Antonovsky's exact 1911 preface calls the 1907 state the third edition, and
established Blok scholarship independently cites `3-е изд. СПб., 1907`.
Together these sources close one provisional Edition identity for the existing
Antonovsky 1907 Expression.

They do **not** close the source-witness route. The live RuNEB card says the
edition was removed or replaced and tells the reader to search for a current
version. The formerly corresponding RSL route currently returns 404, and no
record-specific reader or downloadable file was found. No ToS Item, File,
payload, anchor, source text, responsibility claim, provision-activity claim,
rights clearance, collation, equivalence, semantic object, or canon object is
created.

The typography string is preserved literally as catalog evidence. It is not
converted into a publisher, printer Organization, or provision claim. A
current replacement record or source-visible title/provision page is required
before making any such assertion.

## 1. Classical and official documentation

### 1.1 Exact RuNEB record

The current RuNEB card joins title, Nietzsche authorship, Saint Petersburg,
the literal typography string, year 1907, source library, and NÉB code. RuNEB
describes that code class as a digitized-document identifier, but the record's
current visible state is removed-or-replaced. Identifier class therefore does
not prove that a digital object remains available.

Only the verified RuNEB code is promoted as an external identifier. A
predictable RSL number derived from the shared numeric suffix is not promoted:
the tested RSL URL is a current 404 and no live RSL record independently
resolved it.

### 1.2 A removed catalog card is not an Item

The record provides no current read link, download link, shelfmark, source
pages, or file fixity. A direct RuNEB PDF endpoint returned no file. No
technical bypass, authenticated-session reuse, or bulk acquisition was
attempted.

An Item in the current corpus law requires custody of an exact payload plus
manifest, fixity, provenance, rights, and resource inventory. Creating an
empty Item from a removed catalog card would make the identity ladder appear
complete while removing its evidence floor. This pass therefore stops at
Edition.

### 1.3 Current lawful route

The RuNEB [user agreement](https://rusneb.ru/user-agreement/) and
[FAQ](https://rusneb.ru/faq/) distinguish catalog access from record-specific
content availability, limit downloading to open objects, and preserve
participant-library control over holdings. They support no record-specific
redistribution conclusion for this Edition.

The prepared [RuNEB feedback route](https://rusneb.ru/feedback/) asks which
record replaced the removed card, what current RSL holding or shelfmark
applies, whether an institutional digital object exists, and what lawful
research-copy terms apply. It is a draft only. No form was submitted and no
message, order, payment, permission, or external upload occurred.

The Pushkin House manuscript-department route remains a secondary way to
locate the copy reported in Alexander Blok's library. Its current official
notice closes the reading room from 1 July through 1 September 2026 and states
that applications stopped being accepted on 22 June. That route remains
draft-not-sent and cannot substitute for the originating RSL/RuNEB record.

## 2. Established scholarship

Antonovsky's exact 1911 preface names the 1900, 1903, and 1907 predecessors,
calls the 1907 state the third edition, and reports that each later predecessor
was a new reworking of the one before it. This source supports the already
materialized 1907→1903 derivation claim but does not prove textual equivalence
or supply the missing 1907 pages.

The 1991 Blok study cites:
`Ницше Ф. Так говорил Заратустра: Книга для всех и ни для кого / Пер. с нем.
Ю. М. Антоновского. 3-е изд. СПб., 1907. С. 353—359`, identifies the cited
book with Blok's library, and points to the 1986 printed library description.
This independently corroborates edition number and date, but it exposes
neither a current shelfmark nor an inspectable digital Item.

Pertsev's 2015 peer-reviewed
[study](https://doi.org/10.17072/2078-7898/2015-3-24-31) remains the
methodological control: translator, title, and edition sequence cannot replace
comparison of exact witnesses when philosophical meaning, style, rhythm, and
translation strategy are at issue.

## 3. Fresh and current evidence

Ermakova's 2025 direct
[comparison](https://doi.org/10.17588/2076-9210.2025.2.161-169) of Ivanov,
Antonovsky, Borzakovsky, and Golosovker strengthens the need to preserve exact
translation witnesses and their stylistic and rhythmic differences. It does
not resolve the 1907 source object or change its bibliographic identity.

No inspectable 2026 publication found in the ordered search changes this
historical endpoint. A current commercial publication is not evidence for the
1907 Edition. Freshness affects the live catalog, institutional closure, and
access routes, all of which must be refreshed before contact or acquisition.

Exact Internet Archive and Open Library searches returned no matching 1907
Russian Antonovsky digital Item. Google Books returned no usable exact
record; HathiTrust blocked the attempted public request. These negatives are
time-sensitive availability observations, not proof that no digital copy
exists.

## 4. General web last

Only after official, source, established, and fresh routes, an
[auction record](https://www.antiqueauction.ru/lot/niczshe-f-tak-govoril-zaratustra-kniga-dlya-vseh-i-ni-dlya-kogo-per-s-nem-yu-m-antonovskogo-3-e-izd)
corroborated `3-е изд.`, Saint Petersburg, the same typography string, 1907,
and described one sold physical copy as `VIII, 363 с.; 21 × 15 см`. This is
secondary physical-copy evidence. Its extent, dimensions, binding, damage,
restoration, and sale state are not promoted into the institutional Edition
record and create no ToS holding or Item.

## Rights and publication boundary

Many historical works may be lawfully open or redistributable, but that status
must be established separately for every source object and layer. Historical
age, a catalog code, generic RuNEB language, or prior digitization does not
settle the current record-specific rights and access state.

Any future reproduction received from an institution stays local,
access-controlled, and gitignored unless separate evidence permits a broader
use. Public ToS may expose permitted bibliographic metadata and provenance
without publishing source bytes. Original work, translation, editorial
matter, scan, OCR, and derived publication remain separate rights questions.
