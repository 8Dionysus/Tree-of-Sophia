## Exact backlog pressure

This packet addresses line 23 of the A02 branch-local source backlog:
`Paul Delnero, Sumerian Extract Tablets and Scribal Education`, whose compact
contribution is `переход от упражнения к литературному корпусу`.

The source matters because a one-column extract tablet is simultaneously a
physical artifact, a modern typological class, a bounded copied passage, and
possible evidence of a stage or method of training. None of those identities
entails the others without evidence. Shape alone does not prove that a tablet
was a school exercise; an extract does not automatically expose which larger
composition state supplied it; and the completed artifact does not directly
record whether the pupil copied from a visible model, dictation, or memory.

The bounded source planting therefore separates:

- Delnero's 2010 scholarly article Work;
- each physical tablet and its material and archaeological provenance;
- modern format labels such as `Type III` and the ancient term `imgida`;
- the actual passage and its source-anchored boundaries;
- the mapping from the passage to a composition and textual state;
- the proposed pedagogical sequence or copying process;
- the errors and variants used to test that proposal;
- later historical, semantic, and philosophical interpretation.

### Classical source and terminology line

Delnero begins from a history in which school reconstruction relied heavily on
Sumerian Eduba literary compositions. These accounts are now treated as
idealized representations that cannot alone reconstruct daily educational
practice. Material form, archaeological context, correlations among exercise
contents, and textual errors provide separate evidence.

The 2023 ISAC catalog essay preserves both terms without collapsing them:
Babylonian `imgida`, “long tablet,” and scholarly `Type III` refer to the
one-column extract-tablet format in the checked context. This does not license
the modern type as an ancient universal class, nor does the term determine the
artifact's pedagogical purpose by itself.

## 2. Established and field-shaping scholarship

The 2010 article is the direct established study of the role of Sumerian
literary extract tablets in scribal education. In relation to Veldhuis 1997,
Tinney 1999, and Robson 2001/2002, it supports a staged interpretation while
keeping the argument evidential:

- teacher-student tablets visibly preserve a model and a pupil copy in an
  earlier learning setting;
- advanced literary extracts normally do not preserve a model extract on the
  same tablet;
- a shorter passage can divide a longer composition into portions suitable for
  progressive learning and memorization;
- extract and full-composition tablet frequencies, catch lines, repeated
  groupings, handwriting, and diagnostic errors can jointly support a proposed
  sequence;
- the absence of a model on the same artifact is not, by itself, proof of
  memorization or proof that no external model existed.

Delnero's 2012 “Memorization and the Transmission of Sumerian Literary
Compositions” and 2012 monograph refine the causal test: omissions,
anticipations, preservations, borrowings, and other transfer errors may be more
diagnostic of memory than simple format or disagreement. The proposed process
must fit the actual error pattern and its alternatives.

For ToS the important direction is:

```text
tablet form + passage boundaries + hand + provenance + error pattern
  -> bounded copying-process hypothesis
  -> bounded pedagogical-stage hypothesis
```

No arrow in that chain is automatic, and the resulting hypothesis is not an
immutable ancient institution or curriculum entity.

## 3. Fresh current relevance check

### ISAC 2023: exact object-visible return

The official open-access volume *Back to School in Babylonia* (2023) returns
the argument to visible catalog objects:

- official route <https://isac.uchicago.edu/research/publications/ISACMP/isacmp1>.

Paul Delnero's chapter “The Decad and Scribal Errors” places extract tablets
at the beginning of the advanced phase, contrasts them with teacher-student
tablets, and explains how short extracts may precede a whole composition. It
also makes the strongest evidence causal: different copying situations should
produce different error signatures, and memory-transfer errors require exact
source examination.

The volume's catalog essay “Learning to Fly Solo: Extract Tablets” makes the
material skill explicit. A pupil had to size and shape the tablet for the width
and length of the intended passage; one checked object has unused space. The
artifact is therefore evidence not only for textual contents but for material
planning. The essay also notes that a thirty-line extract from *Dumuzi's
Dream* does not align with a narrative unit and is interpreted as a division
for easier memorization. That remains an argued relation, not a boundary
intrinsic to the ancient composition.

### Delnero 2024: corpus-relative error and stability

Delnero's open-access 2024 chapter “Errors or Innovation?” supplies the newest
direct methodological control found:

- chapter DOI <https://doi.org/10.1515/9783111054360-001>.

It compares literary, cultic, and pedagogical corpora and shows that textual
stability, correction, accepted variation, and possible innovation differ with
text type and transmission practice. For extract-tablet research this blocks a
global rule such as `school tablet = error-prone`, `variant = pupil failure`, or
`stable sequence = centralized curriculum`. Each corpus and artifact must be
tested in context.

### 2026 relevance without false supersession

The June 2026 UCL Press volume *Scribal Worlds* contains Gabriella Spada's
study of model contracts, standardisation, and variants and Eleanor Robson's
return to House F locality and temporality:

- <https://uclpress.co.uk/book/scribal-worlds/>
- DOI <https://doi.org/10.14324/111.9781806550821>.

These are current controls on how exercise forms, local communities, and
standardization should be interpreted. They do not, on the checked evidence,
supersede Delnero's exact 2010 extract-tablet argument or identify every Type
III tablet's function.

No direct 2025-2026 study was found that overturns the bounded progression or
the need for artifact- and error-level evidence. New OCR, sign recognition,
and alignment systems remain laboratory challengers, not historical or
pedagogical authorities.
