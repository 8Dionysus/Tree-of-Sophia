## Result first

The official Russian National Library (RNL) General Alphabetical Catalogue
(GAK) now supplies independent institutional evidence for the exact 1907
Edition. Divider `67133`, card `82`, lists three Antonovsky editions in one
bibliographic sequence:

- the 1903 second corrected edition, with extent
  `[2], III, [3], 432, [2], V с.`;
- the 1907 third edition, Saint Petersburg, typography of F. Vaisberg and
  P. Gershunin, with extent `VIII, 363 с.`;
- the 1911 fourth edition, Prometey, with extent `VIII, 292 с.`.

This closes two previously weak points for the 1907 **Edition**: an
independent institutional catalogue trace and an institutional extent. The
same `VIII, 363 с.` value had previously appeared only in marketplace
metadata and was deliberately withheld. It may now be retained as
institutionally reported bibliographic evidence.

The card does **not** close the source-witness route. A handwritten and partly
crossed-out shelfmark near the 1907 entry is not reliably legible. It is not
transcribed, normalized, guessed, or promoted. The historical card does not
prove a currently available copy, a current holding coordinate, or a digital
object. No ToS Item, File, payload, page, OCR, transcription, accepted text,
publisher claim, provision-activity claim, rights conclusion, semantic claim,
human task, or canon object is created.

## 1. Classical and official documentation

RNL's [electronic catalogue guide](https://nlr.ru/nlr_visit/RA1812/elektronnyie-katalogi-rnb)
states that the General Alphabetical Catalogue covers Russian books from 1725
through 1998 and describes its card-based search. The
[RNL FAQ](https://nlr.ru/nlr_visit/RA1804/faq) says that a negative electronic
catalogue search may be referred to bibliographers through the official
reference route. It also describes remote copy ordering in general terms.
Those general statements are process evidence, not record-specific access or
rights permission.

### 1.1 Reproducible GAK route

The exact route was found without a guessed shelfmark and without general web
search:

1. open the official GAK author search for `Ницше` at
   `https://nlr.ru/e-case3/sc2.php/web_gak/ss?text=Ницше`;
2. select the divider `Ницше Фридрих 1844 - 1900 Философ`;
3. preserve divider identifier `67133` and the returned set of 96 cards;
4. inspect the ordered cards at
   `https://nlr.ru/e-case3/sc2.php/web_gak/lc/67133/1`;

### 1.2 What the card proves and does not prove

The printed 1907 line independently confirms Edition statement, place, year,
typography string, and extent. It also proves that RNL historically catalogued
the Edition within this author-card sequence. The handwritten holding notation
is overwritten or crossed out and is too ambiguous for reliable
transcription. Therefore:

- `RNL GAK divider 67133` and `RNL GAK card 67133/82` are safe external
  bibliographic identifiers;
- `VIII, 363 с.` is safe as institutionally reported extent;
- no RNL call number, holding object, current availability, or Item is safe;
- the typography string remains evidence of manufacture wording, not a
  normalized publisher or printer Organization claim.

## 2. Exact source and established work

This pass inherits rather than rewrites the already inspected evidence spine:

- Antonovsky's exact 1911 preface calls the 1907 predecessor the third
  edition and reports it as a new reworking of the 1903 predecessor;
- the established 1991 Blok study independently cites the 1907 third edition
  and reports a copy in Blok's library;
- Pertsev's 2015 translation study remains the methodological control that an
  edition label cannot replace exact-witness comparison.

The RNL card corroborates the bibliographic endpoint. It does not strengthen
the existing unreviewed 1907->1903 relation into collation, equivalence, or
accepted text.
