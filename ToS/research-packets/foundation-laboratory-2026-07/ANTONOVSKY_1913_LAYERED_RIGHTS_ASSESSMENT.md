## Question and exact scope

What can ToS say now about the rights of the exact 402-page Wikimedia Commons
PDF of the 1913 Saint Petersburg Antonovsky edition, and which conclusions
apply separately to Nietzsche's work, Antonovsky's translation and preface,
the historical edition presentation, the Penza-library page reproduction, and
the embedded ABBYY text?

This Item is deliberately a positive-route contrast to the fail-closed Nani
and Reader Items. The exact Commons object, not merely a similar old book, is
currently declared `Public domain`; the live API still reports the same file
identity, byte size, SHA-1, and public-domain status as the tracked snapshot.
Current Russian term and public-domain rules support the historical work,
translation, preface, and 1913 presentation. The United States conclusion
cannot rest on the domestic pre-1931 shorthand alone because this is a foreign
publication. Its independent basis is 17 U.S.C. §104A: the relevant historical
layers had already expired in their source-country routes before the possible
restoration date and therefore fail the restored-work condition. Current
United States official guidance separately treats mere scanning or
digitization of a literary work as noncopyrightable, while the exact Commons
page remains positive provider evidence for the complete digital object.

That positive rights result does **not** authorize ToS to publish the acquired
local bytes. Rights status, source-text quality, ToS acceptance, and operator
transfer approval are different gates. The operator has directed that local
source files remain local, even where an independent public source permits
redistribution. The aggregate rights record can therefore be
`public_domain_reviewed` for the reviewed RU/US route while remaining
`local_only`, redistribution `not_authorized`, derivatives
`local_research_only`, and server import blocked pending human review and an
explicit operator decision.

### 1.2 Translation is an independent layer under Russian law

Current [Civil Code Article 1260](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_64629/26eaf5de7ca59025f4388fe2980d3dd03dd5e775/)
recognizes a translator's copyright in the translation while preserving the
rights of the author of the translated work. Nietzsche's work and
Antonovsky's Russian expression therefore require separate term reasoning.
The translation cannot inherit public-domain status merely from Nietzsche.

Current [Article 1281](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_64629/675b81f0bc9b8ecd68ea332ecfd9d1562541fb98/)
sets the ordinary life-plus-seventy term and special rules for anonymous or
pseudonymous works, posthumous publication, rehabilitation, and authors who
worked during or participated in the Great Patriotic War. Current [Article
1282](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_64629/366db9d9c7215d66ada95405f1654967f2ae5e4b/)
provides that, after the exclusive right expires, anyone may use a
public-domain work without consent or remuneration while authorship, the
author's name, and integrity remain protected.

[Federal Law No. 231-FZ, Article 6](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_64630/46b4b351a6eb6bf3c553d41eb663011c2cb38810/)
limits retroactive application of the seventy-year terms to cases in which the
earlier fifty-year term had not expired by 1 January 1993. Nietzsche died in
1900. The verified Antonovsky authority route identifies him as 1857-1913.
Their ordinary fifty-year terms had expired by the end of 1950 and 1963
respectively, before the transition date. No evidence of a rehabilitation or
wartime extension affecting this calculation was found. The exact current
Commons public-domain declaration independently corroborates the conclusion.

For the historical edition presentation, even a conservative anonymous-work
calculation from the verified 1913 publication date places the end of a
seventy-year term no later than the end of 1983. This does not identify every
designer or compositor and does not authorize a later digital addition; it
supports the historical 1913 presentation layer in the reviewed
jurisdictions.

### 1.3 United States foreign-work restoration route

The 1913 Saint Petersburg publication is not a United States publication, so
its historical layers cannot be classified merely by applying the current
domestic pre-1931 line. Under
[17 U.S.C. §104A](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html#104a), a
restored work must still be protected in its source country through an
unexpired term. Section 104A(h)(8)(C)(i) defines the source country of a
published work as the eligible country in which it was first published. The
Copyright Office's current [Circular 38B](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ38b.pdf)
and [restored-copyright overview](https://www.copyright.gov/gatt.html) confirm
the source-country boundary and the 1996 restoration route.

Nietzsche's underlying German Work is independently outside restoration: the
official German [UrhG §64](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__64.html)
uses a life-plus-seventy term, which ended no later than the close of 1970 for
Nietzsche's 1900 death. The Russian Antonovsky translation and preface ended
at the close of 1963 under the transition calculation above, and even the
conservative 1913 presentation calculation ended at the close of 1983. Every
historical layer was therefore public domain in its relevant source-country
route before the possible 1996 restoration date and fails §104A's
source-country-protection condition. This is the positive United States basis
for the historical layers; age or the Commons template alone is not.

The rights record's single `term.ends_on` value for the original Work must
therefore track Germany's 1970 endpoint when it represents the §104A
source-country operand. The Russian 1950 endpoint remains a separate result
for the reviewed Russian jurisdiction; it is not the first-publication
source-country endpoint of Nietzsche's German Work. This data-model correction
does not change any layer status, aggregate decision, or transfer gate.

### 1.4 Creative threshold for scan and embedded text

The official [Supreme Court Plenum Resolution No. 10 of 23 April
2019](https://www.vsrf.ru/files/27771/) distinguishes results containing human
creative contribution from results produced by technical means without such
contribution. The latter are not copyright objects. That rule does not make
every digitization automatically free: the exact production and additions
still matter.

For this exact object, ToS has more than appearance alone:

- Commons applies an exact-file public-domain declaration;
- the holding library supplied the object through an open-library campaign;
- the PDF consists of page reproductions and an ABBYY FineReader 11 text layer;
- no new commentary, annotations, or separately credited creative digital
  edition was found;
- the embedded text contains conspicuous OCR errors, which supports mechanical
  generation but does not prove that no human correction occurred.

The United States Copyright Office's current [Compendium, Third Edition](https://www.copyright.gov/comp3/)
section 313.4(A) lists photocopying, scanning, and digitizing a literary work
among examples of mere copies that lack human authorship. The exact Commons
page also independently marks the complete file public domain. For the recent
digital layers, that exact provider statement and the originality threshold
are the relevant evidence; the historical foreign-work term analysis remains
the separate §104A route above.

Taken together, the evidence supports a reviewed public-domain posture for the
exact scan and embedded mechanical text in RU and US, with an explicit
uncertainty note about undocumented correction choices. It does not make the
ABBYY layer accepted text. Textual quality and authority remain unresolved.

## 2. Established scholarship and practice

Thomas Margoni's established study [“The digitisation of cultural heritage:
originality, derivative works and (non) original photographs”](https://www.ivir.nl/publications/the-digitisation-of-cultural-heritage-originality-derivative-works-and-non-original-photographs/)
shows why digitization rights depend on originality, the kind of source, and
the governing national law. A scan cannot be classified solely because it is
digital, but a faithful technical reproduction also does not automatically
create a new copyright.

WIPO's established overview [“Copyright and Museums in the Digital
Age”](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/wipo-magazine/articles/copyright-and-museums-in-the-digital-age-39611)
likewise distinguishes original digital reproductions from simple
reproductions lacking originality. WIPO Working Paper 75, [“The Role of
Copyright in Access to Digital Collections of Cultural Heritage
Institutions”](https://www.wipo.int/publications/en/details.jsp?id=4674),
documents how copyright status affects the availability and downstream reuse
of digital surrogates.

The peer-reviewed study [“Digitization of Libraries, Archives, and Museums in
Russia”](https://ital.corejournals.org/index.php/ital/article/view/13783)
supplies institutional context for Russian digitization practice. These works
support the layer-specific method; they do not outrank the exact object,
current statutes, official guidance, or provider declaration.

## Consequence for ToS

This slice proves that “unknown” must not become the permanent default merely
because ToS is cautious. Positive exact-object evidence should be represented
as positive rights evidence. Caution belongs in the independent gates that
actually remain closed: jurisdiction scope, quality, textual authority, human
review, operator approval, payload transfer, and publication.

The source contract must therefore answer at least four independent questions:

1. What legal layer and jurisdiction was assessed?
2. What exact object and provider statement support that result?
3. Is the text or image accepted as a faithful scholarly source?
4. Has the operator authorized this particular local payload or derivative to
   move to a server or public site?

For this Item the answers are: positive RU/US public-domain rights evidence;
exact Commons object and live declaration; no accepted OCR/transcription; and
no operator transfer approval. A future public route may independently
reacquire or generate material from a currently permitted source, but it must
receive its own fixity, provenance, quality, rights, and operator-admission
record. It may not silently inherit the local file merely because the upstream
route is public.
