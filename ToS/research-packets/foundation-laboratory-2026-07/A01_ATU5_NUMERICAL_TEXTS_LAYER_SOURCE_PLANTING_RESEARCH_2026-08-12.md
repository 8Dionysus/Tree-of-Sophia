# A01: ATU 5 Numerical-Texts-Layer Source Planting Research

Date: 2026-08-12
Authoring posture: model-authored, review-ready, no human or Assyriological
judgment claimed
Scope: bibliographic identity, the historically recoverable numerical-only
subset, provenance, current corpus/encoding controls, rights, access route, and
future laboratory method; no source text or image copied into Git

## Result

The A01 backlog label `ATU 5 numerical texts layer [53]` names a real and
important source boundary, but not a separate book. The bibliographic Work is
Robert K. Englund's 1994 *Archaic Administrative Texts from Uruk: The Early
Campaigns*, published simultaneously as *Archaische Texte aus Uruk* 5 and
*Ausgrabungen der Deutschen Forschungsgemeinschaft in Uruk-Warka* 15. The
volume reports `with a contribution by Rainer M. Boehmer`; ToS preserves that
wording as a bounded contribution claim and does not flatten it into
co-authorship.

Hans J. Nissen's 2024 retrospective supplies the exact historical boundary:
Falkenstein omitted from ATU 1 the tablets containing only numerical signs,
and those tablets were later published by Englund in ATU 5. This supports a
source planting for the numerical-only layer inside the ATU 5 Work. It does
not support a second Work identity, a complete tablet-membership list, or an
exact count of numerical-only documents.

The publisher describes about 820 archaic documents in the volume; the current
CDLI publication search reports 897 related artifacts. These are dated,
source-specific observations with potentially different granularity and
coverage. ToS preserves both rather than manufacturing a growth claim or
silently choosing one as timeless corpus size.

No lawful complete digital ATU 5 Item was found. The planting therefore
creates a Work, two unreviewed responsibility claims, a provisional Boehmer
Agent, the source planting, and one unsent tiered access-request draft. It does
not create an Expression, Edition, Item, tablet membership, numerical sign
inventory, transcription, translation, metrological interpretation, semantic
relation, graph fact, or canon state.

## 1. Official and classical documentation

### Exact Work and publication identity

Freie Universität Berlin's official
[ATU project page](https://www.geschkult.fu-berlin.de/en/e/vaa/forschung/projekte_abgeschlossen/archaische_texte/index.html)
lists Robert K. Englund, with R. M. Boehmer, *Archaic Administrative Texts
from Uruk: The Early Campaigns*, Berlin 1994, as ATU 5.

CDLI exposes the same book through two bibliographic axes. The
[ATU record](https://cdli.earth/publications/1139) identifies BibTeX key
`Englund1994ATU5`, *Archaische Texte aus Uruk* Band 5, Gebr. Mann, Berlin,
1994. A second
[CDLI record](https://cdli.earth/publications/1785923) identifies designation
ADFU 15 and reports Englund and Boehmer in its creator field. These are not two
Works. They are two series identifiers and two current catalog views of one
book.

The current [Gebr. Mann publisher record](https://www.reimer-mann-verlag.de/controller.php?cmd=detail&titelnummer=301745&verlag=3)
gives the exact title, `With a Contribution by Rainer M. Boehmer`, ATU 5,
ADFU 15, ISBN `978-3-7861-1745-2`, and 1994. Its description says the edition
contains copies, catalog, transliterations, and a sign glossary for about 820
archaic documents, almost all in the Berlin museums. The detail record reports
236 text pages, 143 plates with 967 illustrations, and ten additional plates
with 56 illustrations. Current library catalogs instead report 232 pages plus
143 and ten plates. ToS retains this extent discrepancy as source-specific
description, not an editorial correction.

The publisher's
[series page](https://www.reimer-mann-verlag.de/controller.php?cmd=reihen&reihe=Ausgrabungen+der+Deutschen+Forschungsgemeinschaft+in+Uruk-Warka&verlag=3)
marks the volume `Vergriffen` and describes it as the first modern series
volume devoted exclusively to administrative documents from writing's origin.
Out-of-print status is availability evidence, not a content license.

The DAI [Zenon record](https://zenon.dainst.org/Record/000223247/Access)
reports Englund as author, Boehmer as contributor, Berlin: Gebr. Mann, 1994,
232 pages, 143 and ten plates, 35 cm, a diskette, ATU 5 / ADFU 15, and ISBN
`3-7861-1745-4`. [CiNii](https://ci.nii.ac.jp/ncid/BB0633060X?l=en)
corroborates the responsibility statement and supplies NCID `BB0633060X`.
[Google Books](https://books.google.com/books/about/Archaic_Administrative_Texts_from_Uruk.html?id=daomnQEACAAJ)
exposes a contents map: introduction, Boehmer's seal-impressions contribution,
an excavation-report terminology glossary, concordance/transliterations, sign
glossary, text copies, and photographs. These records identify and describe a
publication; none supplies a lawful complete source Item.

### The numerical-only layer is a subset, not a second Work

Hans J. Nissen's 2024 open CDLJ article
[*Uruk and I*](https://cdli.earth/articles/cdlj/2024-1) is the decisive return
to publication history. On PDF page 13 he states that Falkenstein in ATU 1 had
omitted all tablets containing only numerical signs and that these were later
published by Englund in ATU 5. The A01 label therefore preserves a real
omission/recovery boundary in the publication lineage.

The safe identity rule is:

```text
ATU 5 / ADFU 15 bibliographic Work
  contains a historically identified numerical-only tablet subset
  != a second numerical-only Work
  != ATU 2's Die Zahlzeichensysteme analysis Work
  != one fixed current CDLI result set
  != a numerical sign identity, value, unit, commodity, or meaning
```

CDLI artifacts [P000753](https://cdli.earth/artifacts/753), P000754, and
P000755 provide exact examples whose publication trail names them as sealed
numerical tablets in ATU 5. P000753's current transliteration contains only
numeric-sign notation. These examples prove that the subset is source-visible;
they do not establish an exhaustive list, exact subset size, or invariant
classification. Any future member claim must bind an exact artifact, exact
ATU 5 catalog/plate coordinate, dated provider state, and reviewable evidence.

### Current digital coverage is an observation

The current [CDLI publication search](https://cdli.earth/search?publication_bibtexkey=Englund1994ATU5)
reports 897 related artifacts. The publisher's `ca. 820 documents` and CDLI's
897 results may differ because of fragments, joins, catalog granularity,
reclassification, or current database coverage. Without an exact source and
record-level comparison, none of those explanations is promoted. Both numbers
are preserved with date, scope, and origin.

CDLI's current [API documentation](https://cdli.earth/docs/api),
[search documentation](https://cdli.earth/docs/search), and
[artifact metadata fields](https://cdli.earth/docs/artifact-metadata-fields)
make a future reproducible record-level inventory possible. Such an inventory
would be a dated provider representation, not the ATU 5 Work or a final tablet
membership authority.

## 2. Established top scholarship

### Direct reviews define correction surfaces

The Tübingen
[KeiBi record](https://vergil.uni-tuebingen.de/keibi/Record/KEI00000292)
indexes four direct review routes:

- B. R. Foster, *Bibliotheca Orientalis* 53 (1996), 742-743;
- Marvin A. Powell, *Journal of the American Oriental Society* 117.3 (1997),
  608-609, DOI `10.2307/605288`;
- Jöran Friberg, *Journal of Cuneiform Studies* 51 (1999), 107-137;
- Josef Bauer, *Archiv für Orientforschung* 48/49 (2001/02), 168-173.

Friberg's review article,
[*Counting and Accounting in the Proto-Literate Middle East*](https://doi.org/10.2307/1359734),
is the largest direct analytical review route. Its body was not lawfully
available in this pass, so ToS records the bibliography and role but imports no
review assertion.

An accessible indexed excerpt of Powell's direct review maps the volume's
contents: catalog and findspots, excavation terminology glossary, concordance
of 601 ATU 1 texts, transliterations, sign glossary/drawings, text copies, seal
drawings, and photographs. This supports a future page/section request and the
understanding of ATU 5 as primary documentary material with preliminary
analysis. It does not replace inspection of the book.

### Numerical notation must remain contextual

The established numerical and metrological literature shows why a tablet
containing only numerical signs is not semantically self-interpreting. A sign
shape may participate in different numerical systems; represented quantity,
unit, counted object, administrative action, and interpretation require exact
context and scholarly argument. ATU 2's numerical-systems Work is therefore a
distinct analytical source that may later help interpret ATU 5 records, never
a substitute for their catalog and tablet evidence.

For ToS, the conservative sequence is:

```text
physical artifact and source-visible marks
  -> exact ATU 5 catalog / copy / plate / transliteration evidence
  -> dated sign and numerical-system proposals
  -> contextual metrological or administrative interpretations
  -> reviewed semantic claims
```

No later step may be inferred from the earlier step merely because a record is
called “numerical”.

## 3. Fresh current relevance check

### Nissen 2024 fixes the historical omission boundary

Nissen's 2024 retrospective is newer than the classic reviews but is anchored
in first-hand project and publication history. It is used narrowly: to prove
the ATU 1 omission and ATU 5 publication of numerical-only tablets. It is not
used to infer current corpus completeness, tablet readings, or semantics.

### Kelley, Cartolano, and Ferrara 2025: precursor relations remain contested

The 2025 *Antiquity* article
[*Seals and signs: tracing the origins of writing in ancient South-west Asia*](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/antiquity/article/seals-and-signs-tracing-the-origins-of-writing-in-ancient-southwest-asia/B3C2D400F3F80A7A0162D9035C9C2804)
is published under CC BY-NC-ND 4.0. It places numerical notations among the
pre-literate practices relevant to proto-cuneiform and tests relations between
seal motifs and signs. Its own contextual method and the continuing number of
unidentified or contested signs warn against deriving meaning from graphic
resemblance or numerical adjacency alone.

### PCSL and Unicode: numeric and ideographic coordinates stay separate

The current PCSL
[ACN concordance](https://oracc.museum.upenn.edu/pcsl/signlist/ACNConcordance/index.html),
[number list](https://oracc.museum.upenn.edu/pcsl/signlist/Numbers/index.html),
and [frequency surface](https://oracc.museum.upenn.edu/pcsl/signlist/Frequencies/index.html)
explicitly separate numeric and ideographic signs while exposing variant,
stacking, omission, and introduction choices. These are valuable current
working concordances and read models. They are not source truth, ATU 5 tablet
membership, or metrological meaning.

Unicode proposal
[`L2/25-211`](https://www.unicode.org/L2/L2025/25211-proto-cuneiform.pdf)
also separates numeric from ideographic signs and uses published and digital
data. The official
[Unicode 18.0 draft](https://www.unicode.org/versions/Unicode18.0.0/) plans a
September 2026 release with archaic numerals, while the
[allocation pipeline](https://www.unicode.org/alloc/Pipeline.html) retains the
larger Proto-Cuneiform block provisionally and UTC 185 provisionally assigned
the nonnumeric range. Those states are dated technical coordinates, not stable
historical sign identity.

Current [UTR #56](https://www.unicode.org/reports/tr56/tr56-4.html) warns that
cuneiform numerical transliteration is less standardized and that a Unicode
numeric value may express sign multiplicity rather than the value represented
in a particular context. Its numeral section is public-review material, not
accepted ATU 5 interpretation. This is precisely why ToS must keep glyph,
character, transliteration, numeric value, contextual quantity, and meaning on
separate evidence-bearing layers.

| FU Berlin, CDLI, publisher, Zenon, CiNii, Google Books metadata | public web metadata | public-safe identity and citation evidence; no full-book grant |

| CDLI metadata and transliterations | reusable under CDLI's stated academic-practice/attribution terms | exact layer and attribution required; does not license photographs, line art, or ATU 5 plates |
| CDLI artifact photographs | owned by holding institutions | no image acquisition or publication in this slice |
| ATU 5 line art and plates | publication authors/rights holders remain relevant | no copying, OCR, training, public derivative, or redistribution right inferred |
| 2025 *Antiquity* article | CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 at its own layer | cite and study; no rights transfer to source artifacts or ATU 5 |

CDLI's [terms of use](https://cdli.earth/terms-of-use) distinguish textual page
data and transliterations from artifact photographs and author-owned line art.
The future intake must therefore attach rights to each exact layer rather than
using one “open CDLI” flag.

The DAI Orient Department's
[library page](https://www.dainst.org/en/departments/orient-department/about-us/organisation/library)
states that the library is open to scholars, does not lend, and can provide
scans or copies on inquiry. The public-safe draft is deliberately tiered:

1. ask first for exact front matter, table of contents, catalog/plate ranges
   that identify numerical-only tablets, and current copying/research terms;
2. only if lawful, ask for a bounded packet containing the relevant catalog,
   concordance, transliteration, sign-glossary, and representative numerical
   plates, or a complete local research copy if the institution can supply it.

The draft asks separately about local OCR/transcription, indexing, embeddings,
quotation, and retention. It asks for no public source hosting, source
redistribution, derivative publication, or server processing. It is not sent.

## A/B/C laboratory route

| Condition | Material and method | State | Quality question | Cost/speed observation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | exact institutional metadata, Nissen 2024, direct-review trail, current CDLI/PCSL/Unicode documentation | executed, metadata only | Can Work identity, the numerical-only subset boundary, responsibility, current coverage, and false equivalences be resolved without source bytes? | seconds per source; negligible retained storage; cannot assess catalog, copy, plate, or transliteration fidelity |
| B | lawful local, fixity-bound ATU 5 Item or bounded numerical packet; native extraction, page rendering, OCR/layout challengers | prepared, not acquired | Can exact tablet membership, catalog/plate coordinates, mark order, line structure, and numerical notation be recovered with source return? | reproduction cost and terms unknown; each extractor must record elapsed time, resource use, and manual error sample |
| C | B plus exact CDLI artifact snapshots, ATU 1/2 controls, PCSL/ACN/Unicode coordinates, and competing numerical-system interpretations | prepared, not run | Which identities and memberships agree, split, merge, drift, or conflict, and which semantic inferences fail under cross-source comparison? | highest review cost; joins are cheap only after artifact, source, sign, value, and interpretation layers are correct |

Manual inspection is required for representative pages, disagreement cases,
and every promotion boundary. Automated schemas may prove field closure and
fixity; they cannot prove that a drawn sign, tablet membership, reading,
numeric value, commodity, administrative action, translation, or semantic
interpretation is correct.

## Rejected shortcuts

- Do not create a separate Work called “ATU 5 numerical texts layer”.
- Do not conflate the subset with the distinct ATU 2 numerical-systems Work.
- Do not infer an exact numerical-only count from the publisher's approximately
  820 documents, CDLI's 897 current results, or three representative tablets.
- Do not treat a current CDLI search result as immutable ATU 5 membership.
- Do not flatten `with a contribution by Rainer M. Boehmer` into co-authorship
  or infer its entire scope without inspecting the source.
- Do not infer source access or redistribution rights from out-of-print status,
  a library holding, metadata visibility, or a public database record.
- Do not equate a mark, transliterated sign, PCSL identity, Unicode character,
  numeric value, metrological system, quantity, object, or concept.
- Do not infer meaning from shape, frequency, co-occurrence, or numeric-only
  classification without exact contextual evidence and review.
- Do not ask a human to transcribe or review until a source-visible conflict or
  promotion question exists.

## Remaining gates

- Complete or bounded lawful ATU 5 source Item: absent.
- Exact source-visible front matter, contents, and numerical-tablet catalog
  ranges: absent.
- Expression, Edition, Item, and File identities: intentionally open.
- Exact numerical-only tablet membership and count: unresolved.
- Rights-holder and local-processing scope for pages, plates, line art, and
  diskette data: unresolved.
- Tablet, page, plate, catalog-entry, region, and sign anchors: absent.
- CDLI/ATU 5 membership reconciliation: not run.
- Sign identity, reading, numeric value, metrology, translation, etymology,
  concept, and administrative interpretation: absent.
- Assyriological and human source-visible review: not performed or scheduled.
- Graph and canon: unchanged.

The durable result is one direct bibliographic Work planting that preserves a
real publication-history recovery boundary without reifying it as a false
book, fixes the dual-series identity and responsibility wording, records
current corpus and encoding drift, and prepares the lawful source route needed
before exact numerical-tablet experiments can begin.
