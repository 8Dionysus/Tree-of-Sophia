### 1.2 Translation and historical authored layers under Russian law

Current [Civil Code Article 1260](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_64629/26eaf5de7ca59025f4388fe2980d3dd03dd5e775/)
treats a translation as its own copyright object and preserves the distinction
between the translator's rights and the rights in the original. Nietzsche's
German work and Antonovsky's Russian expression therefore require separate
term calculations.

Current [Article 1281](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_64629/675b81f0bc9b8ecd68ea332ecfd9d1562541fb98/)
sets the ordinary life-plus-seventy term and special rules for anonymous or
pseudonymous works, posthumous publication, rehabilitation, and wartime work.
Current [Article 1282](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_64629/366db9d9c7215d66ada95405f1654967f2ae5e4b/)
allows use without consent or remuneration after the exclusive right expires,
while authorship, the author's name, and integrity remain protected.

[Federal Law 231-FZ, Article 6](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/6787)
applies the later term only where the prior fifty-year term had not expired by
1 January 1993. Nietzsche died in 1900, so the ordinary earlier term ended at
the close of 1950. The verified Antonovsky authority route gives 1857-1913, so
the translation and his source-visible preface reached the end of the ordinary
earlier term at the close of 1963. Both dates precede the transition boundary.
No evidence of rehabilitation or another special extension affecting these
two calculations was found.

The exact 1911 title page and translator preface are published layers, not
later reconstructions. Even if an unidentified historical presentation
contribution were treated conservatively under a seventy-year
publication-based or pre-1993 legal-entity route, that term ended no later
than the close of 1981. This does not identify a designer or claim that every
typographic choice is independently copyrightable; it prevents an unknown
credit from silently extending the historical presentation forever.

### 1.3 Recent scan and embedded text under Russian law

The official [Supreme Court Plenum Resolution No. 10 of 23 April
2019](https://www.vsrf.ru/files/27771/) says that copyright requires human
creative work and that results produced through technical means without human
creative activity are not copyright objects. It also preserves a presumption
of creative work until evidence shows otherwise. A faithful technical scan is
therefore not automatically a new work, but ToS must still examine the exact
production and additions.

For this PDF, the forensic evidence shows page reproductions and a large
embedded text layer. It does not supply the capture workflow, operator credits,
manual correction history, selection rationale, or an exact provider
public-domain statement. The agreement establishes lawful bounded access, not
the creative character or reusable status of the digital package. The scan and
embedded text therefore remain `copyright_undetermined`; the absence of a
license is not rewritten as either a copyright claim or a public-domain grant.

### 1.4 United States foreign-work route

The 1911 Russian publication cannot be classified in the United States merely
by applying the current pre-1931 rule for works published in the United States.
Foreign works require the restoration test in
[17 U.S.C. §104A](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html).
An eligible restored work must not already have been in the public domain in
its source country through expiration of term. The Copyright Office's current
[restored-copyright overview](https://www.copyright.gov/gatt.html) describes
the same boundary and the 1 January 1996 restoration date for then-eligible
countries.

Nietzsche's German work had already completed its source-country author term,
and Antonovsky's Russian translation and preface had completed their Russian
term by the end of 1963. They were therefore already public domain in their
respective source-country routes before the possible 1996 restoration date and
do not satisfy the source-country-protection condition for restoration. This is
the positive United States basis for those historical layers; age alone is not.

For a recent digital reproduction, the Copyright Office's current
[Compendium, Third Edition](https://www.copyright.gov/comp3/docs/compendium.pdf)
section 313.4(A) treats photocopying, scanning, or digitizing a literary work
as mere copying without human authorship. That supports a faithful-scan route,
but the exact RSL/RuNEB workflow and embedded-text correction history remain
unknown. In the absence of an exact provider statement, the digital layers
stay unresolved rather than inheriting the historical text's result.

### 1.5 Jurisdiction and contract boundary

Berne Convention [Article 5(2)](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/text/283698)
makes the law of the country where protection is claimed relevant to the
extent of protection and means of redress. This assessment is limited to RU
and US. It does not claim global clearance for a future website.

Copyright status and provider contract are separate controls. A layer can be
public domain under the reviewed law while the operator-held file remains
excluded from transfer by ToS policy and while the provider supplies no exact
reuse license. Conversely, provider access terms do not create copyright in
facts or a purely mechanical copy. The rights record keeps both controls
visible without collapsing them.

## 2. Established scholarship and practice

Thomas Margoni's established study,
[“The digitisation of cultural heritage: originality, derivative works and (non) original photographs”](https://eprints.gla.ac.uk/149774/),
shows why digitization must be assessed through originality, the represented
source, and the governing national law rather than through the word “digital.”
Faithful reproduction does not automatically create a new right, while
creative additions and separate national protections can change the result.

WIPO's established overview,
[“Copyright and Museums in the Digital Age”](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/wipo-magazine/articles/copyright-and-museums-in-the-digital-age-39611),
and Working Paper 75,
[“The Role of Copyright in Access to Digital Collections of Cultural Heritage Institutions”](https://www.wipo.int/publications/en/details.jsp?id=4674),
reinforce the distinction between source-work status, digital reproduction,
institutional terms, and downstream access. They support the layer method but
do not grant rights in this exact PDF.

## Consequence for ToS

This Item demonstrates why “public-domain text” and “publishable PDF” must be
different fields. ToS may preserve the positive historical result and use the
local witness for bounded research without pretending that the provider's
exact scan package is licensed for republication.

A future public text route must receive its own identity, fixity, provenance,
quality, rights, and operator decision. It may be built or independently
reacquired from a route whose exact terms permit the intended use, or it may
proceed after written RSL/RuNEB clarification. It may not silently inherit the
local PDF, embedded text, page images, or provider package. Human review of
rights and source quality remains mandatory before any content-bearing server
route opens.
