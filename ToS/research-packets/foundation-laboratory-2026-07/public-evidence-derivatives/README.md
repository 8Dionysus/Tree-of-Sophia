# Public-Safe Laboratory Evidence Derivatives

This directory is an exact destination for reviewed, aggregate derivatives of
private laboratory evidence. It is not a staging area for raw artifacts.

The evidence-set-specific handoff state conforms to
`ToS/contracts/private-laboratory-evidence-handoff.schema.json`. Every actual
JSON derivative here conforms separately to
`ToS/contracts/public-laboratory-evidence-derivative.schema.json` and must
cross-close to that frozen handoff. Raw pages, text, unit-level judgments,
screenshots, local paths, identities, tokens, private locators, and other
reconstructive detail are prohibited. Counts below the frozen aggregation
floor are suppressed rather than exposed as small cells.

Creating a derivative does not publish it, and publication requires a separate
real-human rights and correlation-risk review. A derivative remains
non-authoritative research evidence: it cannot establish source text,
translation, semantics, rights, canon, or a method winner.

Active-goal authorization may open one bounded local read for derivative
preparation. It is not publication approval and cannot be recorded as human
rights or correlation review.
