## Exact backlog pressure

This packet addresses line 22 of the A02 branch-local source backlog:
`Paul Delnero, The Textual Criticism of Sumerian Literature`, whose compact
contribution is `как работать с копиями, extract tablets и текстовой
стратификацией`.

The source is foundational for ToS because the surviving copy is not a noisy
container hiding one self-evident text. A physical manuscript may preserve a
copying mistake, an accepted orthographic form, a local or temporal form, an
individual scribal choice, an interpretation, or evidence of a differently
transmitted text. A modern composite can help compare these witnesses, but it
cannot silently become the ancient original or overwrite the readings from
which it was argued.

The bounded source planting therefore separates:

- the 2012 modern scholarly Work and its bibliographic identity;
- the ancient composition as a Work-level hypothesis;
- each physical tablet and its material/provenance record;
- each manuscript reading anchored to artifact, side, column, and line;
- an observed difference between readings;
- a proposed classification or causal explanation of that difference;
- the editorial reconstruction and the evidence used to support it;
- translation and semantic interpretation built over a stated text state.

### Classical digital score distinction

The current Oracc score documentation keeps a crucial implementation
distinction visible:

- a `composite line` is reconstructed and does not belong to a physical
  source;
- `exemplar lines` represent the individual manuscript witnesses;
- matrix and synoptic scores are different editorial representations;
- parsed and unparsed states make different machine-processing claims.

See <https://oracc.museum.upenn.edu/doc/help/editinginatf/scores/index.html>.
This operational distinction is compatible with Delnero's source criticism,
but Oracc syntax does not itself decide why a reading differs, whether it is

## 2. Established and field-shaping scholarship

Delnero's 2012 monograph is the field-shaping source in this route. It develops
a procedure from detailed comparison of duplicates of the group of ten
compositions called the Decad. Its central pressure is causal rather than
merely formal: a difference should be assessed by the process that could have
produced it and by its distribution across witnesses, places, periods, scribes,
and textual contexts.

For ToS this yields the non-collapsing chain:

```text
physical artifact
  -> source-anchored reading
  -> observed variant relation
  -> argued variant classification and cause
  -> editorial reconstruction
  -> translation and semantic interpretation
```

The 2013 review by J. Cale Johnson identifies both the importance and the
methodological pressure of the book. The 2015 review by John MacGinnis stresses
that a standardized composite may correspond fully to no ancient tradition,
that intentional and unintentional differences must be distinguished, and
that classical or biblical reconstruction of a single hypothetical original
cannot simply be imported into Sumerian literature:

- Johnson review, DOI <https://doi.org/10.1017/S0041977X13000505>
- MacGinnis review, DOI <https://doi.org/10.1017/S1356186314000492>

The reviews are secondary evaluation, not substitutes for the monograph or
the manuscript evidence. They do, however, establish that the Work was treated
as a major methodological intervention rather than merely a bibliography item.

The bounded established conclusion is not that every difference is meaningful
or that no correction is possible. It is that normalization must be an explicit
editorial act supported by witness-level evidence and a proposed cause. A
mechanical mistake, a stable alternate spelling, a regional distribution, and
an interpretive change are not interchangeable labels.

## 3. Fresh current relevance check

### Delnero's 2024 direct return

The strongest current control found is Delnero's own open-access chapter,
`Errors or Innovation? Variants in Mesopotamian Literary, Cultic, and
Pedagogical Texts from the Early Second Millennium BCE`, pp. 1-35 in Glenn W.
Most (ed.), *Variants and Variance in Classical Textual Cultures* (De Gruyter,
2024):

- volume DOI <https://doi.org/10.1515/9783111054360>
- chapter DOI <https://doi.org/10.1515/9783111054360-001>
- Humboldt repository record <https://doi.org/10.18452/34942>.

The chapter does not discard the 2012 causal method. It makes several
boundaries more useful for ToS:

- textual stability is a continuum, not a binary property shared equally by
  every corpus or every period;
- the same broad types of scribal mistake can occur in literary, cultic, and
  pedagogical corpora, while erasure, acceptance, and creative reuse differ;
- Sumerian literary compositions are often comparatively stable in content
  while still preserving orthographic, grammatical, and occasional macro-level
  variation;
- cultic laments may be much less stable, partly because written copies can be
  related to particular performances rather than one definitive master copy;
- elementary pedagogical lists may be exceptionally stable in sequence and
  content without that fact generalizing to literature or ritual;
- even when a variant looks creative, assigning innovation to the scribe who
  made the surviving copy may remain speculative.

This is a direct semantic control for ToS. `Variant`, `error`, `innovation`,
`stable text`, `composition`, and `version` are evidence-bearing claims whose
meaning depends on corpus type, transmission practice, time, place, and
editorial procedure. They must not be reduced to string inequality or one
global confidence score.

The 2024 volume and Delnero chapter are licensed CC BY 4.0, with explicit
exclusions for third-party material not covered by the open-access
publication. This packet stores only metadata and links; a later payload route
must retain the exact license, attribution, chapter/volume boundary, and any
excluded figures or excerpts.

## Reconciliation decisions

1. Create one modern scholarly Work for the 2012 monograph, not an ancient
   composition, manuscript, composite edition, or universal textual state.
2. Create one provisional Agent coordinating the checked `Paul Delnero` and
   `Paul A. Delnero` forms without external equivalence claims.
3. Treat Delnero's categories as scholarly classifications supported by a
   method, not immutable properties inferred mechanically from different
   strings.
4. Keep artifact, reading, observed difference, variant claim, proposed cause,
   editorial reconstruction, translation, and semantic interpretation in
   separate layers with explicit provenance.
5. Keep the 2024 chapter as a separate contained Work and the newest direct
   current control; do not silently fold it into the 2012 monograph.
6. Preserve Oracc's composite/exemplar distinction as an implementation
   control without turning a digital score into source or semantic authority.
7. Admit only public bibliographic metadata and links in this slice. No book or
   chapter payload, manuscript, score, text, transliteration, translation,

## Laboratory route prepared, not executed

A later A/B/C experiment can compare:

- A — artifact-anchored manuscript readings and material/source provenance;
- B — a composite or score that keeps exemplar readings visible;
- C — candidate alignment, variant detection, and causal classification from
  deterministic software or local LLMs.

Manual review must inspect the actual readings and the evidence for the stated
cause. Evaluation should separately score witness preservation, alignment,
false normalization, omitted variants, causal overreach, uncertainty, speed,
and cost. A schema pass, edit distance, embedding similarity, majority reading,
or fluent model explanation cannot verify a mechanical error, local recension,
scribal intention, innovation, original text, or philosophical meaning.
