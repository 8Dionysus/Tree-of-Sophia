## Authority boundary

This ledger answers which parts of the current work may be retained as durable
foundation mechanics and which outputs remain rejected, blocked, or awaiting
real-human review. “Promoted” here never means philosophically true merely
because a schema, test, process, or model inspection is green.

The promotion authorities remain separate:

- source bytes, digests, manifests, and resolvable anchors can be established
  mechanically;
- bibliographic and rights assessments require their stated evidence and
  human review where still unreviewed;
- transcription, translation, sign, relation, concept, and interpretation
  require source-visible human decisions;
- graph and retrieval systems remain replaceable projections;
- the server, a validator, a model, or this ledger cannot promote a claim by
  itself.

## Current content and canon decision

No OCR transcription, German source unit, translation, etymological claim,
sign, relation, concept, interpretation, transfer claim, or graph edge from
this laboratory is promoted into ToS canon.

The only current promotions are architectural and methodological: stable
identity and fixity routes, provenance and rights boundaries, source-visible
review interfaces, independent experimental lanes, replaceable projections,
and fail-closed contracts. Those are the soil. They are not the fruit.

## Promotion-triggered human decisions still required

Prepared packet size does not create a human backlog. Human episodes open only
for a concrete task or promotion whose prerequisites exist. The remaining
triggered decisions are:

- source-visible review of only the OCR, structure, retrieval, or graph units
  selected by a future quality question; the remaining prepared rows stay
  unscheduled;
- for direct visual retrieval, only `tos-query-003`, `009`, `010`, `011`, and
  `020` are currently trigger-selected, and even those remain unscheduled
  until an adoption or source-return decision actually needs human judgment;
- German-competent source review for only the source units actually selected
  by a translation, morphology, lemma, or semantic task;
- human bibliographic and rights review of reference candidates before use;
- independent human/AI pre-draft philological lanes after source acceptance;
- real translation adjudication after comparator reveal;
- an unassisted operator baseline for materialized interpretive tasks before
  model suggestions, followed by real-human promotion only when a concrete
  sign, relation, or concept is proposed for a stronger layer;
- exceptional second-human or external-expert review only when competence,
  high-impact promotion, persistent ambiguity, or baseline-instability
  triggers it; otherwise the claim remains unresolved;
- 20 double-checked content-bearing out-of-kernel passages before transfer;
  the twenty prepared private pages are a trigger-only sampling frame and do
  not satisfy this gate or create a standing human backlog;
- human approval before sending any access request or moving any payload to a
  server.

Every future change to a decision above must cite the superseding receipt,
preserve the prior state, and state whether the change promotes content,
method, projection, or access. A later negative transfer result has equal
standing with a positive one.
