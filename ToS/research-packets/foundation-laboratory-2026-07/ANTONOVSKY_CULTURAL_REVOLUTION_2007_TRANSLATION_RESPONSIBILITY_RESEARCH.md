# Antonovsky Cultural Revolution 2007 Translation-Responsibility Research

Date of evidence refresh: 2026-08-01
Scope: the exact Russian Expression embodied in the 2007 *Cultural
Revolution* volume 4 and the boundary between translator credit, later
editorial intervention, and textual identity
Authority posture: model-made research; no human bibliographic, textual,
translation-quality, semantic, or rights review

## Question

Can the existing 2007 Russian *Also sprach Zarathustra* Expression gain one
source-returnable `translated_by` claim to the stable Antonovsky Agent while
preserving all of the following distinctions?

- Antonovsky's historical translation lineage is not one invariant text;
- the 2007 checked and newly edited text is not asserted equivalent to the
  1898, 1911, 1913, 1996, or later Antonovsky witnesses;
- general editing, scientific editing of the translation, commentary
  translation, and design are not flattened into inaccurate predicates;
- a bibliographic responsibility claim is not accepted source text,
  translation quality, semantics, rights clearance, or publication authority.

Research followed the project order: classical and official documentation,
then established scholarship, then current scholarship and publisher reuse,
and only then general-web results.

### Bibliographic model

The current official [IFLA Library Reference Model, 2024-07](https://repository.ifla.org/items/version/13)
defines a Work as realized through one or more Expressions and each Expression
as realizing exactly one Work. It also treats all Expressions as distinct
realizations even when users regard one set of characteristics as
representative or canonical. This supports a separate 2007 Expression rather
than a timeless `Antonovsky text`.

The Library of Congress [BIBFRAME initiative](https://www.loc.gov/bibframe/)
remains an active linked-data bibliographic framework; its main surface was
last edited 2026-06-29. Its older 2014 draft documents are used only for two
durable modelling cautions: translations are represented as derivative
resources, and agent association is expressed through an explicit role. The
draft [authority guidance](https://www.loc.gov/bibframe/docs/bibframe-authorities.html)
also says that specific roles are effectively unbounded and should use a
controlled role vocabulary or an explicit relator when no exact property is
known. The drafts are not treated as a current ToS predicate contract.

## 2. Established scholarship and contemporary reception

Alexander Pertsev's peer-reviewed article
[“Для чего переводить Ницше заново?”](https://doi.org/10.17072/2078-7898/2015-3-24-31)
(2015) treats translation strategy, changing language, philosophical sense,
style, rhythm, and reader effect as inseparable pressures. It is an argued
scholarly position rather than a quality verdict for ToS, but it establishes
why edition-specific translation witnesses must remain comparable and
contestable instead of being silently normalized into one text.

Mikhail Boiko's contemporary review
[“Сверхчеловек и Заратустра”](https://www.ng.ru/koncep/2007-09-13/10_zaratustra.html)
(13 September 2007) identifies this exact volume and describes its base as
Antonovsky's historical translation, newly edited with selective attention to
other Russian translations. The review reports Oznobkina's rationale and the
aim of improving readability. This independently supports an editorially
intervened 2007 witness, but the local source remains authoritative for the
exact printed responsibility lines.

The existing ToS translation-reference packet already records the governing
result: “Antonovsky translation” denotes a lineage, not a stable string, and
the 1911, 1913, 1996, and scientifically edited 2007 witnesses stay separate
until bounded comparison proves a narrower relation.

## 3. Fresh and current material

L. L. Ermakova's 2025 article
[“Перевод Вяч. Иванова из книги Ф. Ницше «Так говорил Заратустра»”](https://publications.hse.ru/en/articles/1061726305)
(DOI `10.17588/2076-9210.2025.2.161-169`) compares Ivanov's incomplete 1919
translation with Russian witnesses including Antonovsky. Its attention to
variants, rhetorical figures, rhythm, archaism, and translation strategy is a
current confirmation that translation comparison needs exact Expressions and
variant-preserving evidence. It does not identify the 2007 Edition and is not
direct claim evidence.

A 2026 Tomsk State University qualification-work search result concerns the
translatability of Nietzschean concepts and names Antonovsky among comparison
witnesses. The repository blocked direct inspection during this run. The lead
is therefore rejected as evidence: recency and topical overlap do not replace
inspectability or scholarly authority.

The current official publisher page for a
[2026 Azbuka edition](https://azbooka.ru/books/tak-govoril-zaratustra)
continues to assign the translation to Antonovsky, but expands the name as
`Юрий Антоновский`. The already reconciled GND and Russian national-library
authority evidence identifies `Юлий Михайлович Антоновский`. The 2026 card is
therefore a useful freshness and authority-collision control, not evidence for
the 2007 text, the person's name, or Expression equivalence.

## 4. General web last

General-web searches for the exact ISBN, responsibility block, and a reusable
license found unverified library mirrors, download sites, retail records, and
copies of the publisher description. Some reproduce the 2007 responsibility
block accurately, but none supplied stronger object identity or a verifiable
license for redistribution of the edited 2007 volume. They are rejected as
claim and rights authority.

Open availability elsewhere is not treated as permission. The local
`rights.json` remains `copyright_not_evaluated`, `local_only`, redistribution
`not_authorized`, and derivatives `local_research_only`.

## Admission decision

Admit exactly:

- one proposed whole-page anchor for PDF page 3, where the Edition credits
  Antonovsky's translation;
- one proposed whole-page anchor for PDF page 4, preserving the fuller
  responsibility and revision context without converting every role into a
  ToS predicate;
- one model-made, `public_metadata_only`, `unreviewed` bibliographic claim
  from the exact 2007 Russian Expression via `translated_by` to the existing
  stable Antonovsky Agent;
- digest-bound discovery and annotation events.

Defer the other visible roles. The current responsibility contract has an
exact Expression-level `translated_by` predicate, but no exact representation
for general editor, scientific editor of a translation, or translator of a
separate commentary. `edited_by` would erase material distinctions, while
attaching commentary translation to the main Zarathustra Expression would be
false. These statements remain source-returnable research evidence and
explicit contract pressure.

Do not admit:

- OCR, transcription, source text, page images, or payload bytes;
- equivalence among any Antonovsky Expressions or Editions;
- a claim that Antonovsky alone produced every reading in the 2007 text;
- accepted translation, translation quality, etymology, semantics, concepts,
  signs, canon promotion, or human review;
- any new publication or derivative-use permission.

The claim records what the exact Edition attributes. It does not convert a
historical lineage into an invariant text or a bibliographic credit into
textual acceptance.
