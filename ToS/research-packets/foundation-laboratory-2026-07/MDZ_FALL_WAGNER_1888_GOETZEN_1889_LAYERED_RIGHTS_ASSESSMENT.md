# MDZ 1888/1889 Layered Rights Assessment

Status: model-authored, source-specific research; not legal advice, human
rights review, source-text acceptance, transfer authorization, or publication
approval

Research snapshot: 2026-08-02

## Result

The two exact Münchener DigitalisierungsZentrum objects are useful precisely
because their outcomes differ while their historical soil is similar.

- *Der Fall Wagner*, MDZ `bsb11827837`, is governed at the exact digital-object
  layer by `No Copyright - Non-Commercial Use Only 1.0` (`NoC-NC 1.0`). That
  statement grants non-commercial reuse permission for the Item but is a
  descriptive Rights Statement, not a standard public license.
- *Götzen-Dämmerung*, MDZ `bsb00069119`, is declared by MDZ under
  `CC BY-NC-SA 4.0`. That is an operative public license for the exact
  digital-object layer, subject to attribution, non-commercial use, and
  ShareAlike for shared adapted material.
- In both cases Nietzsche's historical Work and the source-visible 1888/1889
  Edition presentation are public domain in the reviewed German and United
  States routes. A faithful, non-creative scan of those historical printed
  pages has an independent positive DE/US basis.
- BSB/BDR title metadata is a separate CC0 layer. Neither the Rights Statement
  nor the Creative Commons license is projected from the digital object onto
  unrelated metadata, later editorial work, a future ToS transcription, or an
  AI+human critical edition.
- Neither exact PDF contains embedded book OCR. The small extractable text on
  PDF page 1 belongs to the provider-generated cover. No OCR layer is invented.
- The operator-held PDFs remain local and are never uploaded to the future
  site. A future public object must be independently reacquired from an exact,
  current upstream route and pass fixity, layer-specific rights, quality,
  human-review, and operator-approval gates.

The aggregate ToS rights outcomes are therefore positive but deliberately
non-transferable: `permission_granted` for the *Fall Wagner* Item and
`licensed` for the *Götzen-Dämmerung* Item, while both records retain
`local_only`, `not_authorized`, and `local_research_only` at the operator-held
payload boundary.

### NoC-NC is permission, not a license

The official [NoC-NC 1.0
statement](https://rightsstatements.org/vocab/NoC-NC/1.0/) permits copying,
modifying, distributing, displaying, and performing the Item for
non-commercial purposes without additional permission. It also warns that
other rights and provider conditions can apply and gives no warranty. Its
current response was 12,551 bytes with SHA-256
`cd50d39e3860ebff2a915435842d6def89733a7e0d77425098f2bf54a19dcb25`.

RightsStatements.org's [documentation](https://rightsstatements.org/en/documentation/)
and [white paper](https://rightsstatements.org/files/160208recommendations_for_standardized_international_rights_statements_v1.1.pdf)
classify the vocabulary as standardized descriptive statements rather than
licenses. The white paper describes `NoC-NC` for public-domain Items whose
commercial reuse is contractually restricted, including public-private
digitization partnerships. ToS therefore records `permission_granted` with a
contract/mixed basis, not `licensed`, for `bsb11827837`.

### CC BY-NC-SA is an operative scoped license

The official [CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 legal
code](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc-sa/4.0/legalcode) grants a
worldwide, royalty-free, non-exclusive, irrevocable license for the rights the
licensor has authority to license. It permits reproduction, sharing, and
adaptation for non-commercial purposes. Sharing requires attribution, a
license link, source identification where supplied, and notice of
modifications. Shared adapted material must use the same or a compatible
license, and additional effective restrictions may not be imposed. Technical
modifications alone do not necessarily create adapted material. The current
legal-code response was 53,058 bytes with SHA-256
`b0eb998818431962b58eab36c2d8c475ab0300945be2af833492b5bc45895c27`.

The license cannot grant rights MDZ does not control and provides no warranty.
Those ceilings do not erase the concrete exact-object declaration; they
prevent ToS from treating it as a universal conclusion about every possible
layer or jurisdiction.

### IIIF scope is the resource content

The exact MDZ manifests use [IIIF Presentation API
2.1.1](https://iiif.io/api/presentation/2.1/). Its `license` property links to
the license or rights statement under which the resource may be used, and its
`attribution` property must be available to the client. The latest stable
[IIIF Presentation API 3.0](https://iiif.io/api/presentation/3.0/) renames this
property `rights` and explains that it applies to the content represented by
the resource, such as manifest JSON or pixels, as an informative
machine-readable statement. The exact MDZ download gates reinforce that the
URI governs the delivered digital object, not merely an abstract Work or all
third-party data reachable from the page.

### Historical Work, Edition, and faithful scan

German [UrhG §64](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__64.html) applies
the ordinary life-plus-seventy term. Nietzsche died in 1900, so the reviewed
Work-level term ended on 1970-12-31. Even a conservative anonymous-publication
boundary for unidentified historical presentation contributions under
[§66](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__66.html) ended on 1958-12-31
for the 1888 Edition and 1959-12-31 for the 1889 Edition. No anonymous status
or separate creative contributor is asserted; these outer dates show that no
reviewed historical-presentation term remains active.

Current German institutional [guidance on digital
reproductions](https://pro.deutsche-digitale-bibliothek.de/daten-liefern/teilnahmekriterien/rechtliches/das-urheberrecht-digitalen-abbildungen)
states that a faithful flat reproduction normally creates no new copyright
and specifically distinguishes non-creative book scanning from protected
creative capture. [UrhG §68](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__68.html)
is a narrow rule for reproductions of public-domain visual works, not a
blanket book rule. [UrhG §87a](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__87a.html)
keeps database rights separate.

The United States Copyright Office's April 2026 [Circular
15A](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ15a.pdf) places publications from
before 1931 in the public domain. The Office's [Compendium chapter
300](https://www.copyright.gov/comp3/chap300/ch300-copyrightable-authorship.pdf)
explains that mere photocopying, scanning, or digitizing of a literary work
does not add copyrightable authorship. These sources support the historical
Work, Edition-presentation, and faithful-scan layers in DE/US; they do not
override MDZ's separately applicable license or contractual reuse terms.

### Metadata is separately CC0

BSB's [dataset page](https://www.bsb-muenchen.de/bsblab/datensets/)
states that datasets are CC0 unless otherwise noted. The [Bavarian Digital
Repository documentation](https://oai.bsb-muenchen.de/doc/en/bavarian-digital-repository)
states that each BDR record is published under CC0, and the
[BSB OAI terms](https://www.bsb-muenchen.de/bsblab/datenschnittstellen/bsb-oai/)
independently state that BSB title data is available under CC0. The current exact
MDZ search surface for `bsb00069119` also displays the digital-object
`CC BY-NC-SA` label separately from `Metadata: CC0`. ToS limits its metadata
layer to BSB/BDR bibliographic and identifier data and does not project CC0 to
the page images, generated PDF, provider cover, or descriptive prose from
another surface.

## Established scholarship, cases, and institutional practice

The RightsStatements.org white paper and [Europeana rights-labelling
guidance](https://pro.europeana.eu/page/available-rights-statements) distinguish
`NoC-NC` from Creative Commons licenses. The former communicates a
non-commercial constraint on an otherwise public-domain Item; the latter is a
license whose exact conditions travel with the licensed material.

Andrea Wallace and Ellen Euler's 2020 study, [*Revisiting Access to Cultural
Heritage in the Public Domain*](https://doi.org/10.5334/jcms.195), separates
intellectual-property status from contract controls, explains the narrow
visual-art reach of EU Article 14, and emphasizes accurate machine-readable
rights and provenance. It does not extend Article 14 to ordinary textbooks or
other non-visual Works.

The German Federal Court of Justice's 2018 *Museumsfotos* decisions preserve
the historical lesson that copyright and museum-contract restrictions can be
distinct. Later §68 changes some visual-work outcomes but does not erase the
need to model contracts separately. The United States district-court decision
in *Bridgeman Art Library v. Corel* treats faithful two-dimensional
reproduction as lacking originality; it is bounded United States authority,
not a worldwide rule. *Authors Guild v. Google* supports scanning, search, and
snippets as fair use in its facts; it is not a license to redistribute full
source files.

Harvard Library's open-access practice likewise excludes objects accompanied
by license terms and keeps public-private restrictions visible. Creative
Commons recommends leaving faithful reproductions of public-domain material
in the public domain rather than applying a CC license merely to demand
credit. ToS follows the underlying mechanism: rights status and attribution
provenance are separate fields, and institutional credit is retained even
where a public-domain result would not itself require attribution.

## Fresh and currently relevant checks

The Court of Justice of the European Union's judgment of 19 March 2026 in
[C-649/23, ECLI:EU:C:2026:213](https://curia.europa.eu/juris/liste.jsf?num=C-649/23)
holds that a critical edition of a public-domain Work can receive copyright
when its precisely identifiable text reflects free and creative choices. The
judgment also states that such protection does not withdraw the pre-existing
Work from the public domain or confer exclusive rights over it. This is a
direct design requirement for ToS: original Work, historical Edition,
faithful scan, mechanical transcription, creative critical reconstruction,
translation, and future AI+human edition must remain separately identified
and assessed.

Europeana's 2025 [Public Domain
Charter](https://pro.europeana.eu/post/europeana-public-domain-charter) was
still current when rechecked on 2026-08-02 and had been updated on 2026-04-24.
It describes the public domain as permanent, recommends that contracts not
re-enclose it, and calls for high-quality reusable reproductions with metadata
and paradata. It is policy guidance rather than an override of the exact MDZ
conditions.

Pinar Oruc's 2025 article, [*Cultural Heritage as AI Training
Data*](https://doi.org/10.1017/lst.2025.7), shows why legal openness does not
exhaust ethical, contextual, and cultural-heritage questions. For ToS this
supports provenance-rich access decisions and purpose-specific gates rather
than a single Boolean `open` field.

The 2026-08-02 exact manifest checks found both rights declarations, canvas
counts, and institutional attributions unchanged. The current BSB/BDR policy
still supplies a distinct CC0 route for bibliographic metadata. No fresh
source supported collapsing the two objects, replacing one declaration with
the other, or treating local custody as public-transfer authority.

## ToS consequences

1. Keep the five layers as first-class rights assessments. Do not store one
   Item-level label as if it governed everything below or above it.
2. Preserve the exact local files, fixity, source-visible pagination, and
   provider identifiers in ignored custody. Record only metadata and derived
   evidence in Git.
3. Keep both server-import plans `metadata-only`, `blocked-rights`, and
   operator-unapproved. A positive upstream route is not authorization to
   upload the operator-held local copy.
4. If public payload work is later selected, reacquire the exact current
   object from MDZ, bind the new bytes to the then-current declaration and
   conditions, prepare the full attribution package, and obtain human and
   operator approval.
5. For `NoC-NC`, explicitly preserve Bamberg Staatsbibliothek, call number
   `30.1972`, page or folio, URN, MDZ/BSB route, and Google digitization credit
   where required. Any commercial scenario requires a separate conditions
   decision or provider contact.
6. For `CC BY-NC-SA`, preserve BSB, call number `Ph.pr. 881 kc`, page or
   folio, URN, source URL, license link, and modification notice. Apply
   ShareAlike to shared adapted material and add no effective restriction.
7. Keep historical spelling, source correctness, accepted German, critical
   status, and rights as separate gates. A rights-positive scan may be wrong
   for the question; a useful local source may still be non-transferable.
8. Treat any future creative critical or AI+human edition as a new layer under
   the 2026 CJEU boundary. Its rights cannot be back-projected onto Nietzsche's
   public-domain Work, and the Work's public-domain status cannot be used to
   erase new creative responsibility.

## Explicit exclusions

This assessment does not establish:

- legal advice or a worldwide rights conclusion;
- human rights review or provider authority beyond the current exact
  declarations;
- accepted German, OCR, transcription, collation, critical text, translation,
  semantic sign, concept, relation, or canon;
- identity between either first publication and a manuscript, proof,
  later edition, critical edition, mirror, or future reacquisition;
- permission to publish either operator-held local PDF;
- permission to train, serve, or redistribute content-bearing derivatives;
- satisfaction of non-copyright, ethical, quality, correlation-risk,
  attribution, takedown, or operator gates; or
- an active human backlog.
