# Nani 1899 Parallel Fragment Source Witness

Status: exact institutional Item acquired locally; bibliographic claims are
model-made and unreviewed; text, translation quality, rights clearance, and
semantics are not accepted

Research date: 2026-08-01

## Question and boundary

Can ToS admit the exact 1899 Saint Petersburg edition of nine Zarathustra
fragments translated by `С. П. Нани`, together with its parallel German text,
as controlled laboratory soil while preserving the difference between source
bytes, bibliographic statements, textual acceptance, translation comparison,
and publication permission?

The answer for this slice is yes, narrowly. The exact institutional PDF can be
held as a local-only Item and its source-visible responsibility and manufacture
statements can be materialized. This does not accept either language's text,
identify S. P. Nani beyond the printed initials, prove which German textual
state was used, establish translation quality or equivalence, or authorize
redistribution.

## 1. Classical and official documentation

### Exact national-catalog and holding records

- [RuNEB record 000199_000009_003689091](https://rusneb.ru/catalog/000199_000009_003689091/)
  identifies *Так говорил Заратустра : [Кн. для всех и никого]*, `Девять
  отрывков в пер. [и с предисл.] С. П. Нани`, Saint Petersburg, the printing
  house of M. M. Stasyulevich, 1899, `XIV, [2], 105 с.; 24`, Russian and German,
  held by the Russian State Library.
- [RSL record 01003689091](https://search.rsl.ru/ru/record/01003689091)
  independently records the same edition, calls out the parallel German title
  page and parallel Russian/German text, gives call number `FB M 53/102`, and
  currently marks the document freely accessible.
- The [current RuNEB user agreement](https://rusneb.ru/user-agreement/) governs
  use of the service. Open viewing and an official download route are access
  facts, not an object-specific reusable-content license.

### Exact source-visible statements

PDF page 1 contains parallel German and Russian title pages. The Russian side
reads, in relevant part, `ДЕВЯТЬ ОТРЫВКОВЪ / въ переводѣ С. П. Нани` and
`С.-ПЕТЕРБУРГЪ / Типографія М. М. Стасюлевича, Вас. Остр., 5 лин., 28. /
1899.` The German side states `NEUN FRAGMENTE / ins Russische übertragen VON
S. P. NANI` and names the same printing house and year.

PDF page 2 exposes a censor statement dated 18 October 1898 and a dedication
to Vera Fedorovna Komissarzhevskaya. The censor date is not the publication
date. The dedication is not evidence for translator identity, provision, or
textual responsibility beyond its literal presence.

Nani's preface says the publication is intended to aid reading of the original
and therefore places the original beside the translation. This supports the
parallel-text design, but not the correctness of either embedded text layer or
any translation-equivalence relation.

## 2. Established scholarship

The 2016 *Новое литературное обозрение* conference report,
[«Ницше в России и мире»](https://nlobooks.ru/magazines/novoe_literaturnoe_obozrenie/138_nlo_2_2016/article/11895/),
summarizes research that distinguishes philosophical and literary strategies
in Russian Zarathustra translation. It describes Nani's publication as nine
fragments selected from different parts of the book and notes an overlap with
later Erastov selections that may indicate mediation. This is useful lineage
pressure, not exact-text collation, proof of derivation, or authority for
identity/equivalence edges.

## Rights and publication conclusion

The exact PDF remains in ignored owner-local storage. No resource-specific
license or exact-file redistribution permission was found. The Item is
therefore `copyright_undetermined`, `not_authorized` for redistribution, and
`local_research_only` for derivatives. A future ToS site may expose separately
reviewed public-safe metadata and provenance, but not these bytes, page images,
source text, embeddings, alignments, or translations under this assessment.

No access request is needed for the present local research because the exact
official object was acquired. Any future publication or protected derivative
requires a new exact-object, jurisdiction, and permission review.

## Foundation consequence

This edition is unusually valuable laboratory soil because one physical
manifestation visibly places German and Russian in parallel and explicitly
frames the translation as an aid to the original. ToS nevertheless keeps the
foundational layers separate:

`Work → Russian Expression → 1899 Edition → exact local Item`.

The printed `translated by` responsibility and printer manufacture statement
can be observed. The following remain withheld: a German Expression for the
parallel text, source-text acceptance, transcription, collation, full identity
for Nani, derivation, equivalence, completeness, translation-quality judgment,
semantics, human acceptance, and canon promotion.
