# Foundation Goal Completion Audit

Status: active requirement-by-requirement audit; the corpus mechanics are
substantial, but the full foundation goal is not complete

Audit snapshot: 2026-07-26

## Purpose

This audit reconciles the fifteen-phase Tree of Sophia foundation program with
the evidence that currently exists in the Tree of Sophia source owner,
`abyss-stack`, and the private host laboratory.

It does not turn the presence of a schema, report, validator, or experiment
receipt into proof of textual or philosophical completion. It distinguishes:

- **proven** — the required artifact and evidence exist at the required scope;
- **prepared** — the owner contract or interface exists, but its content
  authority has not been exercised;
- **partial** — bounded execution exists, but the full quality or human gate
  remains open;
- **blocked** — an explicit prerequisite prevents honest execution;
- **refresh required** — the artifact exists, but time-sensitive evidence must
  be rechecked before the next use.

The owner snapshots used for this audit are:

- Tree of Sophia `54be6f2` (`Build the Tree of Sophia corpus foundation`);
- `abyss-stack` `59e65abe` (`Add Tree of Sophia human OCR review workbench`);
- private, host-owned OCR candidate-review evidence produced on 2026-07-26.

The private review artifact declares itself local-only and non-publishable.
This audit therefore records only its public-safe consequence: one real
reviewer completed one method-blind candidate-review pass, while no diplomatic
gold, accepted text, or public derivative was created.

## Resolved and open contract pressures

### 1. Solo+AI is not two independent human reviewers

The legacy fifteen-page gold packet encoded two human passes without an
adequate solo+AI distinction. The operating reality is one human working with
AI. `manual-gold-assurance.schema.json` and `gold-assurance.v2.json` now add
the required distinction without rewriting the frozen v1 status. A second
pass by the same person can be a delayed, pass-1-hidden **solo recheck**, but
it is described only as intra-annotator stability.

The v2 assurance ladder records explicit postures:

- single-human source-visible review;
- same-human delayed recheck;
- independent multi-human review;
- language-competence-blocked.

The current packet sets a local 24-hour separation floor, hides pass 1,
candidates, and AI assistance during the recheck, and prohibits
inter-reviewer or independent-multi-human claims. This closes the contract
pressure, not the still-empty human evidence.

### 2. The completed OCR candidate pass is not diplomatic gold

The 2026-07-26 workbench pass reviewed thirty candidate outputs over ten
Russian source pages. It supplies real human fidelity, completeness, structure,
error-class, and decision evidence. It does not supply an independent
transcription, German-language review, CER/WER denominator, accepted source
text, or gold.

Its display assignment was imbalanced. Human active-time comparisons are
therefore confounded and cannot rank correction cost by method. The next
candidate packet must use a predeclared block-balanced assignment.

### 3. Private evidence needs a governed handoff

The post-reveal analysis is explicitly local-only and non-publishable. It
cannot be copied into this public research packet merely because its aggregate
tables are useful. A separate owner-reviewed derivative contract must define:

- destination and audience;
- fields allowed to survive;
- private identifiers and paths that must be removed;
- rights and correlation-risk review;
- human approval;
- creation versus publication effects;
- return handles to the private raw evidence.

Until that exists, Tree of Sophia may record the existence and boundary of the
private pass, but not import its detailed analysis.

### 4. German source review needs language competence

The current human reviewer can source-check Russian pages but cannot reliably
judge German orthography or grammar unaided. German units therefore remain
`language-competence-blocked` unless a competent human reviewer participates
or a separately reviewed assisted protocol is adopted. Machine agreement is
not a substitute for that competence declaration.
