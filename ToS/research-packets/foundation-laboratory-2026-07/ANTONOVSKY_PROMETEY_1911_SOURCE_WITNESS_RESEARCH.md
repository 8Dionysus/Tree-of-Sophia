# Antonovsky–Prometey 1911 Source-Witness Research

Date of evidence refresh: 2026-08-01
Scope: one exact Russian translation Expression, fourth Edition, RSL/RuNEB
digital Item, responsibility and provision statements, fixity, and rights
boundary
Authority posture: model-made bibliographic and technical research; no human
bibliographic, textual, translation, or rights review

## Question

Can the exact 1911 Antonovsky fourth edition become a source-returnable local
research witness without treating open access as redistribution permission,
folding it into the 1913/1996/2007 texts, promoting embedded OCR, or inventing
publisher, printer, date, and edition relations from a filename?

## Ordered method

The pass preserved the project research order:

1. exact institutional records, official access terms, and the exact digital
   object;
2. established historical reference and peer-reviewed scholarship;
3. the freshest relevant institutional scholarship and current edition
   surfaces;
4. general web results last, only as leads or negative controls.

One official 62,952,283-byte PDF was acquired through the exposed RuNEB
download route after a host storage preflight. No access control, crawler
restriction, or mass-download boundary was bypassed. The payload is stored in
the ignored canonical source tree and never in Git.

## 1. Official records, terms, and exact manifestation

The [RuNEB catalog record](https://rusneb.ru/catalog/000199_000009_003693383/)
identifies *Так говорил Заратустра: Книга для всех и ни для кого*, translation
by Ю. М. Антоновский, fourth edition, Saint Petersburg, Prometey, 1911, VIII +
292 pages. It exposes free viewing and a PDF route and names the Russian State
Library as source. The corresponding
[RSL record](https://search.rsl.ru/ru/record/01003693383) resolves MARC record
`01003693383`, language pair Russian/German, the full translator authority
Юлий Михайлович Антоновский (1857–1913), fourth-edition statement, imprint,
year, and extent.

Direct visual inspection separates three source surfaces:

- PDF page 1: translator credit, fourth-edition wording, Prometey, Saint
  Petersburg, address, and 1911;
- PDF page 2: the separate Energiya printer line and address;
- PDF page 4: Antonovsky's own edition-history statement.

The preface is decisive for the corpus model. Antonovsky distinguishes the
1900, 1903, 1907, and current 1911 editions and says the earlier editions were
new reworkings rather than simple reprints. A translator label therefore does
not identify one invariant Russian text. The 1911 state requires a distinct
Expression even before line-level collation exists.

## 2. Established reference and scholarship

The 1911 *New Encyclopedic Dictionary*
[entry for Antonovsky](https://ru.wikisource.org/wiki/%D0%9D%D0%AD%D0%A1/%D0%90%D0%BD%D1%82%D0%BE%D0%BD%D0%BE%D0%B2%D1%81%D0%BA%D0%B8%D0%B9,_%D0%AE%D0%BB%D0%B8%D0%B9_%D0%9C%D0%B8%D1%85%D0%B0%D0%B9%D0%BB%D0%BE%D0%B2%D0%B8%D1%87)
is a contemporary historical reference: volume 3, columns 74–75, identifies
Antonovsky as translator and reports the fourth Saint Petersburg edition of
1911. Its current response SHA-256 was
`34f63a39591edfb988162b5ca52d320bd5853c710745a1c1196ef7e0adb2a3e8`.

A peer-reviewed 2020 Institute of Philosophy RAS
[study](https://pj.iphras.ru/issue/download/307/144) identifies Antonovsky
(1857–1913) and his Nietzsche translations. Pertsev's 2015
[study of Russian Zarathustra translations](https://doi.org/10.17072/2078-7898/2015-3-24-31)
shows why translation strategy, rhythm, and meaning must be compared at the
edition/witness level rather than inferred from a translator name. Boiko's
2007 [account](https://www.ng.ru/koncep/2007-09-13/10_zaratustra.html) says
the scientific-edition text used the historical Antonovsky translation, names
the 1911 fourth edition as its basis, and describes later editorial
intervention. That is lineage evidence, not textual equivalence.

## 3. Fresh relevant evidence

Ermakova's 2025 Higher School of Economics
[record](https://publications.hse.ru/en/articles/1061726305) describes a
comparison of an Ivanov draft with Antonovsky, Borzakovsky, and Golosovker and
foregrounds variants, rhythm, archaism, and style. The current response
SHA-256 was
`c25b2a26ab091ba815e24af452e33c9c63fd43f81ae0b5d1f10fd4bea4f414a0`.
It reinforces the need for variant-aware, edition-specific comparison without
supplying identity or rights for this file.

A current 2026 Azbuka
[edition page](https://azbooka.ru/books/tak-govoril-zaratustra) expands the
translator as `Юрий Антоновский`. That conflicts with the exact RSL authority
record, DNB reconciliation already admitted by ToS, and the contemporary
reference. It is retained only as a freshness negative control: current
commercial metadata can be newer and still less reliable.

## 4. General web last

Only after the official, established, and fresh routes were exhausted, exact
title/translator searches were run on the general web. They mainly returned
the RuNEB record, antique and marketplace listings, a Nietzsche.ru
transcription, a LitRes public-domain presentation, and later reprints.

None proves identity with the exact RSL/RuNEB scan or grants redistribution
rights for it. Some surfaces expand the translator incorrectly or represent a
distinct transcription/content layer. They are rejected as Item identity,
fixity, rights, or equivalence authorities.

## Publisher and printer identities

The title page supports a provisional publisher Organization,
`tos.organization.prometey-publishing-saint-petersburg`. A contemporary RSL
[record](https://search.rsl.ru/ru/record/01003765070) independently catalogs
the Prometey publishing imprint. It does not settle legal-entity or successor
identity.

The title-verso line supports a separate provisional printer Organization,
`tos.organization.energiya-typolithography-saint-petersburg`. Independent RSL
[record 01004091435](https://search.rsl.ru/ru/record/01004091435) and
[record 01007532394](https://search.rsl.ru/ru/record/01007532394) corroborate
the contemporary Energiya typolithography form. Neither makes it the
publisher. Because the printer line itself is undated, the Edition's 1911
title-page year is not promoted to a manufacture completion date.

## Admission decision

Admit exactly:

- one verified 1911 Russian translation Expression, one verified fourth
  Edition, and one exact fixity-registered RSL/RuNEB digital Item;
- one source-visible `translated_by` responsibility claim to the already
  reconciled stable Antonovsky Agent ref;
- one publication and one separate manufacture provision claim;
- two provisional Organizations and the existing Saint Petersburg Place;
- three proposed whole-page source-return anchors;
- an ignored local payload, tracked manifest, fixity, metadata snapshot,
  forensic report, conservative rights record, and provenance;
- an updated translation-reference entry that records local acquisition but
  keeps source text and human acceptance closed.

Do not admit:

- equivalence among the 1900, 1903, 1907, 1911, 1913, 1996, 2007, or later
  Antonovsky states;
- the embedded OCR as reviewed text, a diplomatic transcription, accepted
  Russian, translation quality, etymology, semantics, signs, concepts, or
  canon;
- an exact manufacture date, release day, print-run count, legal-entity
  equivalence, or current-address continuity;
- a reusable-content license, redistribution permission, or future-site
  payload upload;
- human review or acceptance inferred from validation.

No access request is needed for bounded local research because the official
route is directly available. If future public redistribution of the exact
file or a protected derivative becomes necessary, rights-holder and
jurisdiction research must reopen before any message is drafted or sent.
