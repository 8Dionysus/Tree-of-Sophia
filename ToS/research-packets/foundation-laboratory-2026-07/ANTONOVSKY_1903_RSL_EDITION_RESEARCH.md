This closes a provisional Edition identity for the existing Antonovsky 1903

## 1. Classical and official documentation

### 1.1 Exact RSL record

The [RSL public record](https://search.rsl.ru/ru/record/01003693381) and its
current MARC display identify the title, Antonovsky responsibility, edition
statement, place, typography string, year, extent, language, and four holdings.
The record expands `Ю. М. Антоновского` to `Юлий Михайлович Антоновский,
1857-1913` with translator role, consistent with the already reconciled ToS
Agent. That catalog expansion supports Edition identity; it is not a new
source-page responsibility claim.

The RSL data also records MARC transaction value `20061002170739.2` and the
source string `(NLR)rc\1717108`. The latter is retained as a reported external
identifier, not promoted to a direct current National Library of Russia URL:
no public NLR record was independently resolved in this pass.

### 1.2 Holdings are not ToS Items

The exact RSL record reports four shelfmarks. They identify institution-held
copies, but ToS has acquired none of them. An Item in the current corpus law
requires custody of an exact payload plus manifest, fixity, provenance,
rights, and resource inventory. Creating an empty Item from a remote shelfmark
would make the identity ladder look complete while removing its evidence
floor. The four shelfmarks therefore remain discovery and request identifiers.

### 1.3 Current lawful-copy route

The record exposes a specific [fragment-order route](https://search.rsl.ru/ru/fragment-eorder/rsl01003693381).
The RSL [fragment-order FAQ](https://search.rsl.ru/ru/faq/36) describes page-
range requests and electronic delivery after payment. The current RSL
[document-copying page](https://www.rsl.ru/international-services/document-copying)
states that remote orders can be initiated from the catalog or service portal
and that copying depends on law, preservation condition, and library review.
The current [library-use rules](https://www.rsl.ru/vizit-v-biblioteku/rgb-pravila-polzovaniya)
and [copying rules PDF](https://www.rsl.ru/photo/%21_ORS/1-O-BIBLIOTEKE/7-documenty/4readers/3_2_rules-copir.pdf)
remain the institutional control surface.

The prepared request is tiered. It first asks whether the exact holdings are
copyable and what terms, price, and format apply; then asks for title, verso,
preliminary, contents, and terminal pages; only then asks whether a complete
research reproduction is lawful and practicable. Local OCR, indexing,
embeddings, quotation, and metadata publication are requested separately.
Source redistribution, derivative publication, and server processing are not
requested.

The site-wide open-access help text is not record-specific evidence. Because
the exact record supplies no read link or digital object, this pass does not
label the book digitized, open, public-domain, or distributable.

## 2. Established scholarship

Pertsev's 2015 peer-reviewed [study of Russian *Zarathustra*
translations](https://doi.org/10.17072/2078-7898/2015-3-24-31) remains the
methodological control: translator, title, and edition sequence cannot replace
comparison of exact witnesses when philosophical meaning, style, rhythm, and
translation strategy are at issue.

Antonovsky's exact 1911 preface remains the primary lineage statement. It
names the 1900, 1903, and 1907 predecessors and says each later predecessor
was a new reworking of the one before it. The RSL record supplies the missing
bibliographic endpoint for 1903; it does not add or strengthen a textual edge.

## 3. Fresh and current evidence

Ermakova's 2025 [direct comparison of Russian *Zarathustra*
translations](https://doi.org/10.17588/2076-9210.2025.2.161-169) compares
Ivanov, Antonovsky, Borzakovsky, and Golosovker through stylistic, rhythmic,
and variant evidence. It reinforces the need to preserve exact historical
witnesses rather than collapse an Antonovsky name into one invariant text.

No inspectable 2026 publication found in the ordered search changes the exact
1903 bibliographic identity. A 2026 English translation and other current
Nietzsche work are relevant to the wider translation laboratory but not to
this Russian Edition endpoint. Freshness therefore affects the live RSL
request route and negative availability check, not the historical assertion.

Exact searches of Internet Archive and Open Library found no matching 1903
Russian Antonovsky digital witness. Google Books returned a transient quota
failure. DNB and the Lithuanian National Library routes used for adjacent
Antonovsky states supplied no exact 1903 source object. These negatives are
time-sensitive and must be refreshed before acquisition or publication work.

## 4. General web last

Only after official and scholarly routes, a search-indexed [Litfund physical-
copy description](https://www.litfund.ru/auction/445/52/) corroborated the
second corrected edition, Saint Petersburg 1903 imprint, extent, and a
17.5 × 12 cm copy in a period half-leather binding. The page was not stably
retrievable during the final refresh, so it remains secondary corroboration,
not authority. Marketplace listings are weaker still and are not used for
identity, rights, or provision claims.

## Rights and publication boundary

Historical age does not settle the rights or institutional terms of a supplied
reproduction. Any received source file stays local, access-controlled, and
gitignored unless a separate object- and layer-specific permission or
public-domain/open-license route is established. Public ToS may expose
permitted bibliographic metadata and provenance without exposing source bytes.

The request is a draft, not an action. It sends no message, submits no order,
authorizes no payment, and accepts no terms. Any future correspondence,
invoice, permission, or attachment belongs in the ignored private lane.
