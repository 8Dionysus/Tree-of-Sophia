## Governing conclusion

Tree of Sophia should not begin by declaring a universal immutable inventory
of philosophical entities. Its durable foundation is an **evidence spine**:

```text
work
  -> expression / language state
    -> edition or manifestation
      -> physical or digital item
        -> immutable acquisition event and digest
          -> addressable passage or region
            -> observable occurrence
              -> versioned proposal or claim
                -> human review decision
                  -> replaceable search and graph projections
```

The bytes, acquisition record, digest, persistent identity, and review event
can be stable. A word occurrence can be stable when its witness and address
are stable. Lemmatization, sign boundaries, etymology, semantic sense,
concepts, abstractions, relations, and cross-work equivalence remain
versioned interpretations even when they become highly trusted.

This is a stronger foundation because it preserves the route back to what was
actually seen while allowing understanding to deepen.

## Laboratory truth boundary

- A validator may prove that required fields, files, and references exist.
- A benchmark may measure a bounded task on a declared sample.
- Neither proves that OCR is faithful, a translation carries the sentence, a
  semantic boundary is sound, or a graph relation is philosophically true.
- Manual comparison against visible source regions and a deliberately small
  gold set is the promotion authority for this laboratory.
- Failed, ambiguous, and rejected outputs are retained as evidence rather
  than removed to make a metric greener.
