publication credited to `С. П. Нани`, and which conclusions apply to the

## 1. Classical and official documentation

### 1.1 Exact national-library object and operator classification

The [exact RuNEB record](https://rusneb.ru/catalog/000199_000009_003689091/)
currently exposes `Скачать pdf` for the RSL electronic copy of the 1899
publication. It identifies `Девять отрывков в пер. [и с предисл.] С. П.
Нани`, the parallel Russian/German languages, and the 1899 Saint Petersburg
edition.

The current [RuNEB FAQ](https://rusneb.ru/faq/) states all three controlling
operator propositions:

1. works in free access are works that have entered the public domain;
2. outside participating library premises, only public-domain works are
   available;
3. only works that have entered the public domain may be downloaded, while
   downloading copyright-protected editions is impossible.

The exact record is freely readable outside a reading room and exposes the PDF
download. The bounded inference is therefore not merely “old book” or “button
present”: the current national-library operator classifies this downloadable
publication as public domain in Russia.

The [RuNEB user agreement](https://rusneb.ru/user-agreement/) separately
governs access to the service. It allows access to objects subject to free use
under Russian law and use for personal, informational, scientific,
educational, and cultural purposes under applicable law. It prohibits mass
automated downloading, access-control bypass, and harmful use. It is not an
object-specific open-content license and leaves the user responsible for
lawful downstream use.

These are different evidence types:

- the exact record proves the selected object and its current download state;
- the FAQ explains what RuNEB says that download state means;
- the user agreement controls interaction with the service;
- none is a Creative Commons-style license for the exact PDF file.

### 1.2 Current Russian legal baseline

The latest consolidated [Russian Civil Code record in WIPO
Lex](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/23256) is amended
through 3 August 2025. WIPO identifies the 2025 change as an amendment to
Article 1350; it does not change the copyright rules used below. The current
Russian text is the authority surface; the linked English text is an earlier
reference version.

[Article 1281](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_64629/675b81f0bc9b8ecd68ea332ecfd9d1562541fb98/)
sets the ordinary life-plus-seventy term and special rules for anonymous or
pseudonymous works, posthumous publication, posthumous rehabilitation, and
authors who worked during or participated in the Great Patriotic War.

[Article 1282](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_64629/366db9d9c7215d66ada95405f1654967f2ae5e4b/)
provides that, after exclusive rights expire, a work enters the public domain
and may be used by anyone without consent or payment, while authorship, the
author's name, and integrity remain protected.

ToS does not independently calculate the Nani term from the 1899 date. The
translator's full identity, death date, possible rehabilitation, and wartime
facts remain unresolved. The positive translation/preface posture rests on
the exact current RuNEB operator classification, not on age alone.

The neighboring right of a publisher does not supply a generic new right in
this already published book. Current [Article
1337](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_64629/91ef51382ce928552168449874ff58dc7e1ecd67/)
concerns the lawful first disclosure of a previously undisclosed work in the
public domain. The Nani publication was disclosed in 1899. This does not
resolve whether a particular modern digitization contains independently
creative matter.

### 1.3 Creative threshold for the digital reproduction

The official [Supreme Court Plenum Resolution No. 10 of 23 April
2019](https://www.vsrf.ru/files/27771/) explains that the use of technical
means does not prevent copyright protection when human creative activity is
present, but results created by technical means without a creative human
contribution are not copyright objects.

This supports a narrow distinction, not an automatic scan clearance. A purely
faithful mechanical reproduction may fail the creative threshold; a
digitization containing original choices or added creative matter may not.
The exact RSL production process is not documented in the captured record.
The PDF also contains handwritten notes of unresolved authorship and date and
an embedded text layer whose production is not evidenced. The File-level
posture therefore remains undetermined.

### 1.4 Jurisdiction boundary

The official [Berne Convention text](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/text/283698)
states in Article 5(2) that, apart from treaty provisions, the extent of
protection and means of redress are governed by the law of the country where
protection is claimed. A Russian operator classification is strong evidence
for the Russian layer; it is not a worldwide clearance. The future ToS site's
serving jurisdiction and audience have not been selected or reviewed.

## 2. Established scholarship and practice

The Moscow State University law-journal article [“Electronic libraries of
scientific and educational organizations: legal problems of creation and
activity”](https://cyberleninka.ru/article/n/elektronnye-biblioteki-nauchno-obrazovatelnyh-organizatsiy-pravovye-problemy-sozdaniya-i-deyatelnosti/pdf)
explains the established Russian distinction: libraries need authorization to
digitize protected works, while works in the public domain may be converted to
electronic form and their copies distributed under Article 1282. This supports
the positive consequence of an actual public-domain determination but does
not identify which modern additions belong to the historical publication.

WIPO's established overview [“Copyright and Museums in the Digital
Age”](https://www.wipo.int/ru/web/wipo-magazine/articles/copyright-and-museums-in-the-digital-age-39611)
describes the cross-jurisdiction distinction between an original digital
reproduction and a simple reproduction lacking individuality or originality.
It reinforces the need to inspect the exact reproduction layer rather than
assuming that every scan creates a new right or that no scan ever can.

These sources are interpretive context. The current statute, Supreme Court
guidance, exact RuNEB object, and current operator policy remain the stronger
evidence surfaces.

## Consequence for ToS

This slice exposes a real contract requirement. `rights.json` must preserve
layer-specific evidence while keeping one fail-closed Item admission posture.
The optional `layer_assessments` structure records scope, basis, jurisdiction,
rights-holder knowledge, permissions, redistribution, derivative and server
processing posture, term-calculation state, uncertainty, date, review status,
and rationale for each actual layer.

A later transcription, normalized reading text, OCR corpus, translation,
alignment, embedding, or site export must receive its own rights record. It
may cite the positive Russian public-domain assessment for the historical
translation, but it may not inherit authorization from the exact PDF merely
because that PDF supplied the evidence.

The operator's local-source rule remains stronger than what copyright alone
might permit: the acquired PDF itself is not to be published by ToS. Public
metadata and provenance may remain tracked. A future public text or derivative
requires an exact layer selection, intended-use decision, serving-jurisdiction
review, source-integrity check, and explicit server import decision.
