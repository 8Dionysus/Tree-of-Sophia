## Question

What may Tree of Sophia say about the exact operator-provided PDF of volume 4
of Friedrich Nietzsche's complete works, *Так говорил Заратустра*, published
by Культурная Революция in Moscow in 2007?

The answer cannot be inherited from Nietzsche's death, Antonovsky's historical
translation, the Edition year, or the fact that the file is locally available.
The exact object contains at least eight analytically distinct layers:

1. Nietzsche's underlying German Work;
2. the historical Antonovsky translation substrate first published in 1898;
3. the separately claimed 2007 checking and new editing of that translation;
4. Colli and Montinari's underlying critical commentary;
5. the 2007 Russian translation of that commentary;
6. the 2007 Edition design;
7. the publisher's Edition-level contribution or arrangement; and
8. the exact later vector-outline PDF package.

## Result

Nietzsche's underlying Work remains `public_domain_reviewed` in the bounded
Russian and United States routes already established for the exact 1911 and
1913 Antonovsky witnesses. The Russian reviewed-jurisdiction endpoint is 1950.
For the United States foreign-work route, Germany is the original Work's
first-publication source country under 17 U.S.C. §104A(h)(8)(C)(i), and the
German life-plus-seventy term ended in 1970, before possible restoration.
This positive Work result does not select or clear a particular critical
German text.

Antonovsky's historical translation contribution is also
`public_domain_reviewed` in the same bounded RU/US route. Antonovsky died in
1913; the former Russian fifty-year term ended in 1963, before the 1993
transition and possible United States restoration. The source-visible 2007
statement that the Edition is based on his translation first published in
1898 is enough to recognize a historical substrate. It is **not** evidence
that the exact 2007 text equals the 1898, 1900, 1903, 1907, 1911, or 1913
Expression. The positive historical result therefore does not clear the exact
2007 Russian Expression.

The source-visible 2007 page supplies the decisive separate boundary. It says
that the historical translation was checked and newly edited, credits
E. V. Oznobkina for that work, and carries an explicit 2007 notice for the
translation editing. Russian Civil Code Article 1260 treats a translation and
other derivative contributions independently from the pre-existing material;
Article 1281 applies the ordinary life-plus-seventy term. Current United States
law likewise limits derivative-work protection to the new contribution while
protecting qualifying post-2002 foreign publications under §§102–104. The
2007 editing is therefore treated fail-closed as `in_copyright` in RU/US. The
exact creative delta, ownership chain, death date, and final term endpoint are
not established; no wider ownership claim is made.

The same page credits A. G. Zhavoronkov and carries a 2007 notice for the
Russian translation of the commentary. That translation is separately
`in_copyright` in the reviewed RU/US route. Colli and Montinari's underlying
commentary remains a different layer. This pass did not establish its exact
source Edition, publication date, ownership chain, or term in the jurisdictions
needed for this Item, so it remains `copyright_undetermined`; the positive
status of Nietzsche's Work cannot be transferred to it.

The Edition also carries a 2007 design notice naming I. Bernshtein. That
contribution is treated as `in_copyright` in RU/US without deciding the
copyrightability of every typographic choice. The publisher's separate 2007
notice is real evidence, but its exact scope across selection, arrangement,
editorial work, and other contributions is not stated. That layer remains
`copyright_undetermined` rather than being converted into a blanket ownership
claim for every page.

The exact 162,778,307-byte PDF is an aggregate `in_copyright` object because
it reproduces the protected 2007 editing, commentary translation, and design.
Its embedded 2021 CorelDRAW/Corel PDF Engine metadata does not establish the
identity, authority, originality, or license of the digital creator. Even if
the vector conversion itself added no copyright, that would not remove the
rights in the content it reproduces. The earlier acquisition route and any
reuse permission remain unknown.

The top-level record therefore becomes `in_copyright`, not
`copyright_not_evaluated`, while staying `local_only`, redistribution
`not_authorized`, derivatives `local_research_only`, and human review
`unreviewed`. This is a better-supported closed boundary, not a transfer or
publication permission.

## Classical and official documentation

### Russia

The current consolidated Russian Civil Code surface was checked on
2026-08-11. It identifies the text as amended through 23 July 2025, with the
relevant changes effective 4 January 2026. No later demonstrated amendment
changing this layer analysis was found.

- [Article 1260](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_64629/26eaf5de7ca59025f4388fe2980d3dd03dd5e775/)
  treats translations and other derivative works as independently protected
  contributions while preserving the rights in the material on which they are
  based. It also confirms that one translation does not prohibit an independent
  translation of the same original.
- [Article 1281](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_64629/675b81f0bc9b8ecd68ea332ecfd9d1562541fb98/)
  supplies the ordinary life-plus-seventy term and preserves the transition
  rule for works whose former fifty-year term had not expired by 1 January
  1993.
- [Article 1282](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_64629/366db9d9c7215d66ada95405f1654967f2ae5e4b/)
  permits use after expiry while preserving authorship, name, and integrity.
- [Supreme Court Plenum No. 10 of 23 April 2019](https://www.vsrf.ru/files/27771/)
  separates human creative work from a purely technical result and confirms
  that unprotected facts and methods do not erase protected expressive parts.

The notices are evidence of claimed 2007 contributions, not proof that the
publisher owns every layer. The record therefore names only the visibly
credited contributions and keeps ownership and term details bounded.

### United States

The current Copyright Office [Title 17
edition](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/) includes amendments enacted
through 18 December 2025. In [Chapter
1](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html), §103 limits derivative and
compilation protection to new material and does not enlarge protection in the
pre-existing material. Section 104 protects qualifying published works whose
authors or first publication connect them to a treaty party. The Copyright
Office's current [Limitation of Claim
guidance](https://www.copyright.gov/eco/help-limitation.html) expressly treats
translations and sufficiently original editorial revisions as possible
derivative contributions while excluding pre-existing material from the new
claim.

The historical Work and Antonovsky substrate retain their independently
reviewed §104A results. The 2007 contributions do not inherit those expired
terms and do not need a restoration theory: they are recent foreign
contributions assessed under the current ordinary protection route.

## Established scholarship and practice

Thomas Margoni's established study, [*The digitisation of cultural heritage:
originality, derivative works and (non) original
photographs*](https://www.ivir.nl/publicaties/download/1507.pdf), explains why
digitisation must be assessed for both the rights in the source object and any
new originality in the digital representation. Its central method applies
here even though the operator PDF is a vector-outline reconstruction rather
than a documented institutional scan: uncertain digital originality cannot
clear still-protected source layers.

The Copyright Office [Compendium, Third
Edition](https://www.copyright.gov/comp3/docs/compendium.pdf) and the Russian
Supreme Court guidance both require human creative activity for a new
copyrightable contribution. They support a bounded negative proposition only:
purely automatic technical conversion would add no new authored layer. They do
not prove that this PDF was purely automatic, establish its license, validate
its text, or remove the rights in the reproduced 2007 contributions.

## Consequence for ToS

This Item is the strongest current demonstration that a public-domain Work and
historical translation do not make a modern edited Edition or local PDF
publishable. ToS may use the exact file for bounded local research, OCR
experiments, comparison, and provenance-aware indexing under the operator's
local policy. It may track public-safe bibliographic facts, fixity, layer
status, and source-return addresses.

ToS may not publish or transfer the file, page images, OCR, transcription,
translation editing, commentary, design, snippets, embeddings, alignments, or
source-bearing annotations under this record. A future public route must use
an independently permitted source or receive written permission covering the
specific layers and intended uses, then pass fresh fixity, quality, rights,
human-review, and operator-approval gates. Rights review does not accept the
text, assess translation quality, establish German competence, create semantic
authority, or promote anything into canon.
