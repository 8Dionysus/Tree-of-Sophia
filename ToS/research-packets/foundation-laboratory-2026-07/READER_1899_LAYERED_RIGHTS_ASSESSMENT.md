## Question and exact scope

What can ToS say now about the rights of the 1899 Moscow edition issued by
the editorial office of the journal *Reader*, and which conclusions apply to
Nietzsche's underlying work, the uncredited Russian translation, the
periodical/offprint presentation, the RNL/RuNEB photographs, and the
non-sequential digital fragment returned as three PDFs?

The answer cannot be one scalar and cannot be inherited from Nani. The exact
RuNEB record is freely readable outside a reading room and currently exposes
an unauthenticated ZIP download. RuNEB states that only works that have
entered the public domain may be downloaded. That is positive current Russian
operator evidence for the underlying Nietzsche work and the published
Russian translation embodied in this exact publication.

The translation nevertheless remains uncredited and its 1898–1899 lineage is
uncollated. A contemporary 1901 source reports that the separately issued
book reprinted Antonovsky material without his permission or name. Current
Russian law expressly preserves the independent copyright of a translator
even where a derivative work was used unlawfully. Historical unauthorized
publication therefore cannot be treated as proof that no translation right
ever existed. ToS does not calculate the anonymous-work term from 1899, does
not identify Antonovsky as this Expression's translator, and does not infer
lawful publication. Its positive Russian translation-layer result rests on
the current exact-object operator classification, with the historical conflict
and missing attribution retained.

The exact digital Item is a separate problem. It is not the cataloged complete
236-page book but three image-only PDFs containing 22 selected surfaces. No
reusable-file license, scan-production history, selection-responsibility
statement, or worldwide clearance was found. The aggregate Item/File remains
local-only and `copyright_undetermined` even though two narrower historical
layers have positive Russian public-domain evidence.

### 1.3 Current Russian legal baseline

The latest [WIPO Lex Civil Code record](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/legislation/details/23256)
is the 2025 consolidation amended through 3 August 2025. Its recorded 2025
change concerns Article 1350, not the copyright provisions applied below.
The current Russian text remains the controlling authority surface.

[Article 1259](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_64629/9ab75500370084e37fe1f32eb8a05db489ebca99/)
separates copyrightable expression from ideas, facts, and exclusively
informational reports. This supports tracking public bibliographic facts and
identifiers but does not make a catalog database, page image, or creative
selection automatically free.

[Article 1260](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_64629/26eaf5de7ca59025f4388fe2980d3dd03dd5e775/)
recognizes a translator's copyright in the translation as an independent
object. Since the 2023 amendment it also says expressly that unlawful use of a
derivative or composite work does not prevent protection of the translator,
compiler, and underlying authors. Its paragraph 7 separately recognizes the
publisher's right to use a periodical publication while preserving the rights
of authors whose works it contains. Therefore:

- the reported absence of Antonovsky's permission does not erase translation
  rights;
- a journal/offprint publication layer cannot be collapsed into the
  translation layer;
- identifying the historical editorial office does not identify a current
  rights holder or calculate a current term.

[Article 1265](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_64629/01cf40c9e42efacdb8dff7cdd410e2542bbbfdf4/)
protects authorship and the right to a name and treats the named publisher as
the author's representative for an anonymous publication until the author
discloses identity. That procedural presumption is not a translator identity
claim and does not turn a historical publisher label into present ownership.

[Article 1281](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_64629/675b81f0bc9b8ecd68ea332ecfd9d1562541fb98/)
sets the ordinary life-plus-seventy term and a seventy-year rule, counted from
the year after lawful disclosure, for anonymous or pseudonymous works unless
the author's identity becomes certain during that period. ToS does not apply
the anonymous calculation here because the exact translator, lawful-disclosure
history, possible identification, death, rehabilitation, and wartime facts
are not all resolved.

[Article 1282](https://www.consultant.ru/document/cons_doc_LAW_64629/366db9d9c7215d66ada95405f1654967f2ae5e4b/)
provides that an expired work enters the public domain and may be used without
consent or payment while authorship, name, and integrity remain protected.
The positive historical-layer decision relies on the exact current RuNEB
classification, not on an incomplete age-only calculation.

### 1.4 Creative threshold and digital-fragment boundary

The official [Supreme Court Plenum Resolution No. 10 of 23 April
2019](https://www.vsrf.ru/files/27771/) explains that using technical means
does not prevent copyright when human creativity is present, while a result
created by technical means without creative human activity is not a copyright
object.

That rule does not classify these PDFs by appearance alone. The record does
not document the RNL capture workflow, human choices, post-processing, or
responsibility for selecting the 22 non-sequential surfaces. The exact return
also differs materially from the cataloged complete Edition. ToS therefore
keeps two separate unresolved digital layers:

- the photographic/digital scan production;
- the digital fragment selection and three-part assembly.

The PDFs have no usable embedded text. No embedded-text layer is invented in
the rights record, and no OCR layer exists yet.

### 1.5 Jurisdiction boundary

The official [Berne Convention text](https://www.wipo.int/wipolex/en/text/283698)
states in Article 5(2) that, apart from treaty provisions, the extent of
protection and means of redress follow the law of the country where protection
is claimed. The Russian operator classification is strong evidence for the
Russian layer; it is not worldwide clearance. A future ToS site's serving
jurisdiction and audience remain unselected and unreviewed.

## 2. Established scholarship and practice

The Moscow State University law-journal article
[“Electronic libraries of scientific and educational organizations: legal
problems of creation and activity”](https://cyberleninka.ru/article/n/elektronnye-biblioteki-nauchno-obrazovatelnyh-organizatsiy-pravovye-problemy-sozdaniya-i-deyatelnosti/pdf)
explains the established Russian distinction between protected works that
require a legal basis for digitization and public-domain works that may be
converted and distributed under Article 1282. It supports the consequence of
an actual public-domain decision, not a shortcut from age or availability.

A. S. Vorozhevich's 2020 study
[“Electronic libraries and copyright: can rights in digital copies be
exhausted?”](https://base.garant.ru/77298473/) treats the legality of digital
library creation and operation as a separate problem from possession of or
access to a paper copy. This is the relevant established control against
reading an official download as a blanket downstream redistribution grant.

WIPO's established overview
[“Copyright and Museums in the Digital Age”](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/wipo-magazine/articles/copyright-and-museums-in-the-digital-age-39611)
distinguishes original digital reproductions from simple reproductions lacking
individuality and emphasizes jurisdiction-specific treatment of orphan works
and online access. It supports inspecting the exact scan and added layers
rather than asserting that every scan creates a new right or that none can.

These sources are interpretive controls. The current statute, Supreme Court
guidance, exact source pages, exact RuNEB object, and current operator policy
remain stronger evidence.

## Consequence for ToS

The Reader contrast validates why layer-specific rights must coexist with a
fail-closed Item gate. A positive operator finding about historical content
does not authorize publication of provider photographs, non-sequential
selection, page images, OCR, transcription, alignment, embeddings, or a
future ToS translation.

A later transcription or normalized reading text must receive its own rights
and integrity record. It may cite the positive Russian operator assessment of
the published translation, but it must preserve the unresolved translator
identity and must not inherit authorization from the exact PDFs. A later
collation may establish a relation to Antonovsky; until then, responsibility
and equivalence remain withheld.

The operator's local-source rule remains stronger than a narrow copyright
conclusion: these three acquired PDFs are not to be published by ToS. Public
metadata and provenance may remain tracked. Any future public text or
derivative requires exact layer selection, intended-use and serving-
jurisdiction decisions, source-integrity review, and an explicit server-import
decision.
