# Naumann 1893 Internet Archive PDF and EPUB Layered Rights Assessment

Status: model-authored rights research; exact upstream objects and local
fixity reconciled; no legal advice, human rights review, source-text
acceptance, operator transfer approval, or publication decision

Research snapshot: 2026-08-02; United States foreign-work route corrected and
exact live provider/law surfaces rechecked 2026-08-10

## Question

What may Tree of Sophia say about the exact Internet Archive image PDF and
automatic EPUB derived from the 1893 Naumann edition of *Also sprach
Zarathustra*?

The answer must not collapse these distinct layers:

1. Nietzsche's underlying Work;
2. Peter Gast/Heinrich Köselitz's preface;
3. the historical Edition presentation, portrait, and letter facsimile;
4. the modern image scan;
5. automatically recognized text and page-image resources;
6. Internet Archive's later notice, package, navigation, styles, and metadata;
7. the operator's local custody and the future site's admission policy.

## Result

The exact image PDF has a positive public-domain basis in the reviewed German
and United States routes. The Work, preface, historical presentation, portrait,
and letter facsimile are outside the reviewed terms. Current German
institutional guidance says that a simple mass scan of book pages creates no
new protected layer; the current governing United States Copyright Office
Compendium likewise excludes mere scanning or digitization from copyrightable
authorship. The exact Internet Archive Item also carries Public Domain Mark
1.0. Its PDF can therefore move from `copyright_undetermined` to
`public_domain_reviewed` in DE/US.

The exact automatic EPUB cannot receive the same aggregate result. Its
historical and automatic-OCR content layers are public-domain material, but
`EPUB/notice.html` is a later, born-digital Internet Archive text created with
the 2023 package. The notice is institutional explanatory prose, is in the
reading spine, and has no exact license. The Item-level PDM was supplied in
2015 by an individual uploader and cannot silently clear a later Internet
Archive contribution. Package selection, navigation, style, and metadata also
lack an exact reusable license. The aggregate EPUB therefore moves to
`conflicting_evidence`, not to `public_domain_reviewed` or `licensed`.

Both local payloads remain `local_only`, `not_authorized`, and
`local_research_only`. A rights-positive upstream finding does not override
the operator's rule that local research files are never uploaded to the site.
It also does not satisfy the server protocol's human rights-review and explicit
operator-approval gates.

The aggregate outcomes are unchanged by the 2026-08-10 correction, but the
independent United States reasoning is now explicit. This German publication
cannot be cleared merely by applying the domestic pre-1931 shorthand. Under
17 U.S.C. §104A, a foreign work whose protection had already expired in its
source country before the possible 1996 restoration date fails the restored-
work source-country condition. The reviewed German terms for Nietzsche's Work,
Köselitz's preface, and the conservative historical Edition, portrait, and
letter routes all ended before 1996. The scan and automatic-OCR conclusions
still require the separate no-new-authorship analysis; §104A does not itself
clear a modern digital contribution.

## Classical and official documentation

### Exact provider surfaces and PDM

The current [Internet Archive metadata
API](https://archive.org/metadata/NietzscheAlsoSprachZarathustra1893) is the
exact object surface. It carries
[Public Domain Mark 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/mark/1.0/).
Creative Commons describes PDM as a tag for material believed to be free of
known copyright restrictions, not a license or waiver. CC does not recommend
it for hybrid or jurisdiction-limited status, and the deed disclaims warranty
while preserving the possibility of other rights and jurisdictional
differences.

The live Internet Archive [Terms of
Use](https://archive.org/about/terms) surface was refreshed on 2026-08-02. Its
served text is dated 2014, but it remains the current provider contract. It
places legal compliance and risk on the user, gives no warranty of
non-infringement, and says a declared CC or other license is usable only to the
extent that the user trusts the declaration and declarer, which is rarely
Internet Archive. Here the PDM predates the later EPUB notice and came from an
individual uploader account. ToS therefore treats it as strong evidence for
the old-book object, not as an Internet Archive license over every later file.

### Germany

The current German Copyright Act supplies the term boundaries used here:

- [UrhG §64](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__64.html) provides the
  ordinary life-plus-seventy term and
  [§69](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__69.html) makes the term end at
  the end of the relevant calendar year. Nietzsche died in 1900, so the Work
  term ended 1970-12-31.
- Peter Gast, Heinrich Köselitz, died in 1918; the current
  [Deutsche Biographie identity](https://www.deutsche-biographie.de/pnd127679677.html)
  supports that date. His preface term ended 1988-12-31.
- [§66](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__66.html) provides the
  anonymous/pseudonymous publication route. Even if unknown historical
  presentation or portrait contributors were evaluated conservatively through
  that route, a 1893 publication term ended 1963-12-31.
- [§70](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__70.html) provides twenty-five
  years for qualifying scientific editions,
  [§71](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__71.html) covers specified
  posthumous publications, and
  [§72](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__72.html) gives photographs
  and similar products fifty years. None supplies an active 2026 term for the
  source-visible 1893 presentation, portrait, or Nietzsche letter facsimile.
- [§68](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__68.html) concerns
  reproductions of public-domain visual works. It does not justify a blanket
  EU conclusion for every scanned book page.
- [§87a](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__87a.html) keeps substantial
  database investment distinct from copyright in individual factual fields.

The source-visible `Uebersetzungsrecht vorbehalten` statement records an 1893
reservation. It is preserved as historical evidence. It is neither erased nor
treated as a present-day restriction after the reviewed terms expired.

The EU Copyright in the Digital Single Market
[Directive 2019/790, Article 14 and Recital 53](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2019/790/oj)
supports the public-domain status of faithful reproductions, but its operative
scope is works of visual art. The PDF conclusion for ordinary book-page mass
scans instead relies on the German institutional guidance below plus the exact
PDM, not on an overbroad reading of Article 14.

### United States

The Copyright Office's April 2026
[Circular 15A](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ15a.pdf) places all works
published in the United States before 1931 in the public domain. This Edition
was first published in Germany, however, so that domestic cutoff is context,
not an independent foreign-work conclusion.

[17 U.S.C. §104A](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap1.html#104a) defines
the restored-work route and excludes a work already in the public domain in
its source country through expiration of term. The Copyright Office's current
[URAA/GATT overview](https://www.copyright.gov/gatt.html) identifies January 1,
1996 as the restoration date for works from then-eligible Berne/WTO countries.
Germany is the source country for this first-published German Edition. By then:

- Nietzsche's ordinary German Work and letter term had ended in 1970;
- Köselitz's preface term had ended in 1988;
- even the deliberately conservative anonymous historical-presentation and
  portrait route had ended in 1963, while any applicable photographic or
  scientific-edition term stated above ended no later.

Each reviewed historical layer therefore fails §104A's source-country-
protection condition. This source-country analysis—not the domestic pre-1931
shorthand—is the independent United States basis. It does not determine
whether the modern scan or OCR contains new authorship.

The Copyright Office's [Compendium, Third
Edition](https://www.copyright.gov/comp3/) remains the governing administrative
manual. Chapter 300 §313.4(A) explains that mere copies created through
photocopying, scanning, or digitizing a literary work lack copyrightable
authorship. Its human-authorship discussion also distinguishes machine output
from expression conceived and executed by a human. This supports the result
that the exact image PDF and automatic OCR do not acquire a new U.S. copyright
merely through the conversion process. It does not clear later human-written
notice prose or other independent contributions.

## Established scholarship, cases, and institutional practice

The established U.S. line is consistent with the current Compendium:

- [*Bridgeman Art Library v. Corel*](https://www.law.cornell.edu/copyright/cases/36_FSupp2d_191.htm)
  found no originality in exact photographic copies of public-domain images.
- [*Meshwerks v. Toyota*](https://law.justia.com/cases/federal/appellate-courts/ca10/06-4222/06-4222-2011-03-14.html)
  evaluates whether a reproduction contains independently original expression
  rather than rewarding labor alone.
- The CJEU's [*Painer* judgment](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:62010CJ0145)
  ties originality to free and creative choices. It is useful for separating a
  faithful mechanical reproduction from a creatively authored photograph.

Wallace and Euler's 2020 study,
["Revisiting Access to Cultural Heritage in the Public Domain"](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40319-020-00961-8),
shows why copyright, contract, platform controls, and institutional policy must
remain separate rather than being reduced to a single `open` flag.

Two current German institutional sources are more directly applicable to this
book scan than Article 14 alone. The Staatsbibliothek zu Berlin's 2025
[image-rights training](https://blog.sbb.berlin/wp-content/uploads/LiD25_Bildrechte.pdf)
states that a simple mass scan of book pages receives no protection. Deutsche
Digitale Bibliothek's current
[digital-reproduction guidance](https://pro.deutsche-digitale-bibliothek.de/daten-liefern/teilnahmekriterien/rechtliches/das-urheberrecht-digitalen-abbildungen)
likewise separates copyright in the book from a non-original scanning process.
These are guidance, not judgments, but they are current, German, book-specific,
and align with the exact Item's PDM and the U.S. mere-copy rule.

## Fresh and currently relevant checks

The current snapshot adds several signals that were not safely replaceable by
older assumptions:

1. Internet Archive's exact metadata still exposes the PDM through
   `metadata.licenseurl` in August 2026 and still identifies the PDF as original
   and the EPUB as a derivative of ABBYY output.
2. The current details page still exposes the PDM, but the 2026-08-10 terms
   route yielded only a JavaScript shell. The substantive 2026-08-02 terms
   capture remains historical evidence and is not represented as freshly
   reconfirmed; this access limitation reinforces the closed human/server
   gates without changing either aggregate Item decision.
3. Current 17 U.S.C. §104A and the Copyright Office URAA/GATT overview retain
   the source-country-protection condition and 1996 restoration route. The
   April 2026 Circular 15A domestic cutoff is not substituted for that route.
4. Creative Commons' 2025
   [cultural-heritage guidance](https://creativecommons.org/2025/07/09/recommended-licenses-and-tools-for-cultural-heritage-content/)
   explicitly separates public-domain originals, faithful reproductions,
   born-digital institutional content, and metadata. It recommends PDM/CC0 for
   faithful public-domain reproductions, open licensing or CC0 for
   institutional content, and CC0 for metadata; it does not recommend placing
   one indiscriminate license over a mixed package.
5. Europeana's current [rights-labelling guide](https://pro.europeana.eu/files/Europeana_Professional/Europeana_Network/Europeana_Network_Task_Forces/Final_reports/RightsLabellingofDigitalCulturalHeritage.pdf)
   requires checking other legal and public-private-partnership restrictions
   before selecting PDM. A rights statement is a communication about the
   object, not proof that every layer shares one legal origin.
6. WIPO's April 2026 [cultural-heritage access
   toolkit](https://www.wipo.int/meetings/en/doc_details.jsp?doc_id=658407) is a
   current workflow reference for collection access. It is not binding law and
   does not clear these Items, but it reinforces object-, source-, purpose-,
   and jurisdiction-specific analysis.

## Server consequence

The image-PDF plan may record `public-domain-reviewed` evidence in DE/US, but
remains `metadata-only`, `blocked-rights`, and operator-unapproved. The EPUB
plan remains `rights-unknown` at the server boundary because its aggregate
record has conflicting evidence. Neither plan authorizes payload transfer,
source processing, source text, page images, snippets, embeddings, alignments,
translations, or source-bearing annotations.

A future public PDF candidate must be independently reacquired from the exact
then-current upstream route, receive new fixity and provenance, recheck the
PDM, terms, capture furniture, and object composition, then pass human rights
review and explicit operator transfer approval. It must not be copied from the
operator's local ToS payload.

The useful future EPUB route is to generate a new accessible ToS package from
a rights-positive, quality-accepted source. That new package must have its own
provenance, licenses for ToS-authored contributions, accessibility and text
quality evaluation, and server admission. Removing `notice.html` from the
current local EPUB would create an untracked derivative and would not by itself
resolve package, provenance, quality, or operator-transfer requirements.

## What this does not prove

This assessment does not prove:

- German orthographic, grammatical, textual, or reading-order correctness;
- exact agreement between the OCR and the 529 page images;
- a critical, author-final, or complete Nietzsche text;
- that PDM is a license, warranty, or worldwide legal conclusion;
- reuse rights in unreviewed jurisdictions;
- rights in Google/Cornell logos, capture furniture, trademarks, marginalia,
  privacy, personality, moral rights, or contractual access controls;
- an unrestricted right to copy every Internet Archive metadata field or
  database;
- translation quality, etymology, semantics, signs, graph truth, or canon;
- permission to publish either operator-local file.

The durable result is a provenance-bearing fork: a rights-positive historical
and faithful-scan route for the PDF, and an explicitly mixed, blocked exact
EPUB whose useful historical content can guide a separately built future
edition without smuggling later institutional layers into the public domain.
