# Lived-witness capture form

Use this form conversationally, one block at a time. The author may skip,
withhold, revise, pause, or stop. Do not turn it into a long page of required
manual fields.

## 1. Begin

Ask:

> Do you want to begin a local-only record now, and should I preserve your next
> answer verbatim unless you explicitly approve edits?

Record the answer and capture mode. If the answer is not an explicit yes, stop.

## 2. What this record touches

Ask one open question:

> Is this about the whole *Zarathustra*, a particular part or passage, a time
> you read/listened/recited it, or something in your life that met the book?

Do not require a passage when the true scope is the whole work or a life
interval.

## 3. The testimony

Invite the author:

> Say it in the form that is true to you. I will preserve the words first and
> ask only necessary clarifications afterward.

Keep the raw answer. Do not summarize it into the packet body.

## 4. Optional context

Only after the first answer, ask whether the author wants to add:

- an exact, approximate, relative, unknown, or withheld time;
- their own words for the life context;
- a source passage or edition anchor;
- the mode of encounter: reading, rereading, listening, reciting, memorizing,
  or living with the work;
- uncertainty, contradiction, or a later counter-reading.

These are offers, not mandatory categories.

## 5. Exact-body confirmation

Show the complete candidate body and a visible list of any transformations.
Ask:

> Is this exactly the testimony you want attributed to you in this version?

Without explicit confirmation, leave the packet as `draft`.

## 6. Third parties

If other people may be identifiable, ask whether the author wants names or
details retained, redacted in a derivative, or kept entirely private. Do not
edit the original body silently.

## 7. Use beyond local storage

Default answer for every item below is `not-granted`. Ask only when the author
requests a wider use, and ask separately for each:

- tracked metadata in Git;
- quotation;
- lexical index;
- semantic index;
- graph projection;
- public release;
- model training;
- external service processing.

End by restating what remains local and what, if anything, was explicitly
granted. No permission is implied by another. Copyright remains with the
author unless the author separately records a scoped license or dedication.
