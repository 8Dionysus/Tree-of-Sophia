# AGENTS.md

This card applies to the golden kernel and bounded routes in `ToS/zarathustra/`.

## Role

This branch carries the first source-to-review learning cycle and its public
entry. [GOLDEN_GROWTH_KERNEL.md](GOLDEN_GROWTH_KERNEL.md) owns the architecture;
`public-entry/TINY_ENTRY_ROUTE.md` and `prologue-1/TRILINGUAL_ENTRY.md` own the
tiny-entry orientation route. Source witness text belongs in `ToS/source-witnesses/`, canon in
`ToS/canon/`, and voluntary lived testimony follows the lived-witness card.

## Boundary Routes

- Keep the public entry bounded to one source route, capsule, source authority
  surface and concept hop. It returns to source and review owners.
- Separate primary text, lived testimony, textual observation and interpretive
  proposal. Kernel guidance is subordinate to the actual witness and canon.
- Transfer the method to other branches; derive their ontology, predicates and
  readings from their own sources.

## Kernel sequence

Resolve the exact witness, language role and stable address; put provisional
observations/proposals in candidate intake; record accept, revise, reject,
defer, ambiguity and counter-reading outcomes in review. Kernel canon
promotion requires human review. Rebuild each derived export or public companion after authored input moves.
Test unseen material for vocabulary rejection instead of forcing a fit.

Every gold slice exposes anchors, layer, human/agent authorship, review state,
uncertainty and lineage. If the contract cannot express the source faithfully,
retain candidate/review posture and return the pressure to doctrine/contracts.
Source-visible textual assessment follows Knowledge Assessment; it does not
remove the kernel's canon or lived-witness consent boundaries.

## Validation

Select `source_home`, `public_entry` or the affected lived-witness/export route
in [ToS/VALIDATION.md](../VALIDATION.md). Use the review checklist for source,
interpretation, consent, method transfer and human-review judgments.
