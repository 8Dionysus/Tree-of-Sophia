# Zarathustra Golden Growth Kernel

*Thus Spoke Zarathustra* is the first golden growth kernel of Tree of Sophia.

It is not a convenient demo corpus and not a universal ontology for all
philosophy. It is the first route that should eventually make the whole path
from source witness to reviewed, graph-readable knowledge legible enough that
humans and agents can learn how ToS grows.

## Why this seed

This seed is carried rather than merely selected.

Dionysus has lived with *Thus Spoke Zarathustra* for five years through
repeated reading, listening, recitation, and memorization, and has carried it
through love, war, death, suffering, and joy.

That lived provenance explains why this work bears the first full growth
cycle. It gives the route depth, continuity, and accountable human attention.
It does not replace Nietzsche's text, bibliographic evidence, translation
provenance, scholarship, or review.

## Two coupled growth fronts

Tree of Sophia grows through two different but interdependent fronts:

1. **World-philosophy corpus soil** — the ordered source and bibliographic
   foundation of written philosophy across works, witnesses, editions,
   languages, time, place, traditions, and transmission.
2. **Zarathustra golden kernel** — the first dense, end-to-end worked route
   through which ToS learns how a source becomes observations, candidates,
   interpretations, concepts, relations, reviewed canon, and derived views.

The corpus soil gives the tree breadth and somewhere to root. The golden
kernel gives it a first complete path of growth. Neither substitutes for the
other.

## What is stable enough to build on

The foundation is not a frozen universal inventory of entities that can be
tagged correctly once and never revisited.

The durable base is an evidence spine:

- identity and provenance of a work, witness, edition, translation, or
  manuscript;
- a stable address inside that witness;
- the exact text or an explicit source reference;
- observable occurrences, repetitions, forms, and annotation actions tied to
  that address;
- temporal, spatial, linguistic, and bibliographic claims with visible
  evidence status;
- versioned review history showing who proposed, accepted, revised, rejected,
  or left a reading unresolved.

Concept boundaries, semantic entities, motif identity, relations, and
interpretations are not immutable in the same way. They remain versioned,
contestable, and able to deepen without severing the evidence beneath them.

The stable foundation therefore protects change. It does not deny it.

## The full growth cycle

The golden kernel should make this movement inspectable:

1. identify the source witness and its provenance;
2. establish a stable addressable text spine;
3. record source-near observations such as recurrence, wording, form, image,
   contrast, and translation tension;
4. propose semantic objects without disguising interpretation as observation;
5. relate objects through context, lineage, tension, transformation, support,
   and other reviewed relation kinds;
6. preserve acceptance, rejection, ambiguity, counter-reading, and reviewer
   rationale;
7. promote only reviewed objects and relations into authored canon;
8. derive graph, search, KAG, visualization, and evaluation views downstream
   without transferring authority to them.

This is a growth cycle, not a one-way extraction pipeline. Later evidence may
return an object to an earlier layer, split it, merge it, rename it, or leave
it unresolved while preserving its lineage.

## What makes a gold example

A gold example is more than an accepted label.

It should preserve:

- exact source anchors and witness scope;
- the layer at which each assertion belongs;
- the distinction between observation and interpretation;
- accepted, rejected, and deliberately unresolved alternatives;
- reviewer rationale and meaningful counter-readings;
- confidence, uncertainty, and version lineage;
- the boundary between human judgment and agent proposal;
- the route from the authored object back to the source.

Agents should learn the path of judgment, including refusal and uncertainty,
not merely imitate a final answer.

## Transfer rule

What transfers from Zarathustra is the method:

- how to return to source;
- how to distinguish evidence from interpretation;
- how to propose and test semantic boundaries;
- how to preserve ambiguity and disagreement;
- how to review promotion into stronger layers;
- how to emit useful graph and retrieval projections without making them
  authoritative.

Nietzsche's concepts, predicates, calibration axis, and branch shape do not
automatically transfer to another work or tradition. A new source may resist
the learned vocabulary, expose a missing distinction, or require the contract
to grow. That resistance is evidence about the architecture, not noise to be
forced away.

## Lived-witness boundary

Lived witness is an authored first-person provenance layer. It may explain
attention, selection, salience, and the continuity of a reading.

It must remain distinct from:

- primary source witness;
- textual observation;
- published translation or edition evidence;
- scholarly claim;
- interpretive proposal;
- canonical ToS judgment.

Lived witness may guide review and make the human root visible. It cannot by
itself settle what Nietzsche wrote, what a passage must mean, or what all
philosophy should become.

## Preparation boundary

This note prepares the repository for the kernel. It does not claim that the
whole book has already been gold-annotated or that corpus soil is already
defined.

Before wider planting, the next owner work should define:

- the corpus-soil contract for work, witness, edition, language, time, place,
  address, and evidence status;
- the annotation-unit and layer contract for the golden route;
- a reviewable gold-packet shape with positive, negative, ambiguous, and
  counter-reading examples;
- evaluation questions that test source return, layer discipline, uncertainty,
  and transfer to unseen material;
- the boundary between ToS-authored gold material and downstream training,
  retrieval, graph, or runtime machinery.

No mass corpus ingestion, bulk semantic labeling, model training, or silent
import of private reading notes is authorized by this architecture note.

## Owner route

- Source witnesses remain under `ToS/source-witnesses/`.
- Knowledge and annotation law remains under `ToS/doctrine/` and
  `ToS/contracts/`.
- Provisional extraction remains under `ToS/candidate-intake/`.
- Authored nodes and relations remain under `ToS/canon/`.
- Review evidence remains under `ToS/review-ledger/`.
- World-philosophy branch growth remains under `ToS/philosophy/`.
- Repeatable growth operations remain under `mechanics/`.
- Generated graph and KAG views remain under `ToS/derived-exports/` or their
  stronger downstream owners.
- Evaluation machinery routes to `aoa-evals` when it exceeds bounded ToS-local
  eval pressure.
