Reviewed: 2026-09-09. This public-safe technical derivative supplements the
[earlier citation/observation lifecycle](2026-09-09-real-native-quality-assessment-review.md).
Exact confidential sources, proposals, grants, issuer audits and immutable

neither their contents nor their return locators.

## Three distinct content decisions

A non-maker agent separately assessed one exact English TextLayer for
`text-layer:semantic-analysis`, one passage-bound interpretation Claim for
local research, and one complete Russian source-copy statement Form. The
Form copies a Russian research description about the English witness: it is
not a new translation, source quotation, shortened caption or standalone
summary. Exact original return, complete source records, qualifications,
counter-reading and current rights were inspected for the content decisions.

The source layer's suitability does not establish the interpretation. The
Claim's admission does not admit its Form. The Form's admission permits its
qualified presentation, not endorsement of its parent. Local research does
not grant translation fidelity, original-language equivalence, empirical
truth, rights clearance, canon or publication.

Across this semantic-purpose slice, six substantive proposals were fully
reviewed and committed: an initial separate decision on each target, then a
fresh separate renewal on each after actual grant expiry. All six were
`admit-with-limits`; no additional substantive error was found in these six
complete issuer audits. This is not an error-rate estimate. The earlier
incidental language-identification error, caught before append in the citation
slice, remains part of the quality record and limits wider qualification.

## Bounded competence and human involvement

A twelve-case bilingual calibration covered attribution, negation, belief
versus assertion, expected versus necessary effects, passage versus token
scope, compressed glosses and unsupported fidelity or independent-evidence
claims. All twelve labels matched the fixed key; full rationale/source review
found no unresolved material error. The key was not changed. The assessor's
recorded calls did not read the withheld key, but this was cooperative local
separation, not hostile same-account isolation or an attested execution trace.

The source had already been repeatedly encountered and overlapped calibration.
Maker, assessor and issuer separation does not create a second source origin
or independent-model group. Actual native execution was independently checked
as `gpt-6-astra` with `high` effort before each admitted continuation, rather
than accepting the output's own model claim. Hidden inference configuration
was unavailable; bitwise reproducibility, blind evaluation, general bilingual
competence and population accuracy are not claimed.

No new human signature or approval was required for these six decisions or
their consumption. The operator's prior mandate and rights restrictions stayed
governing. Every proposal received complete source-visible agent issuer audit;
this does not demonstrate zero-cost supervision, high throughput or the absence
of legitimate future human decisions. Arbitrary translation and summary
generation were outside the qualified scope.

## Actual expiry, renewal and delivery

The first grants expired at their real clock boundary; their documents and
earlier events were not edited. The old owner rejected payload access. A new,
explicitly narrower reviewer/source-read grant preserved those expired records
and initially restored none of the three admissions. Renewed judgments were
required against the newly current source-quality basis.

The final ordinary command returned the exact full statement, complete raw
parent, current quality basis with all its limits and a separate current
parent-assessment companion. `standalone_reading` is false. Parent status,
limits and journal observation are not replaced by the parent's historical
raw `review_status`; Form status is not used as parent status. The complete
local result was approximately 35 KiB, below the bounded materialization
ceiling; no context or restriction was truncated.

Exact repeats of the renewed Claim and Form append commands returned their
original events and unchanged journal revisions. Before/after current views
matched around the final read; source wording, Form identity and observed
journal heads were unchanged by consumption. Different-purpose citation
events were retained and not renewed through this semantic scope.

The old source-only `ready/admission:null` result was used only as historical
evidence for the unchanged wording. Its expired creation delegation was not
renewed. Strict preparatory refusals for that expired writer and a mismatched
read-request field were retained and corrected without weakening product

## Executable consumer and verification

The owning implementation is in
[the assessment command](../../mechanics/growth-cycle/parts/branch-growth-cycle/scripts/assessment_journal.py),
[human-form materialization](../../mechanics/growth-cycle/parts/branch-growth-cycle/scripts/human_forms.py)
and the source-owned field catalog. Its contract is
[Human forms](../doctrine/HUMAN_FORMS.md) and
[the native quality route](../../mechanics/growth-cycle/parts/branch-growth-cycle/docs/NATIVE_TEXT_LAYER_ASSESSMENT.md).

The explicit assessed route now consumes existing `source-copy` Forms without
rewriting them into freeform or inserting a generated basis into authored
bindings. Current quality context comes from the trusted owner. Previously
authored bindings still resolve exactly; a stale binding is not silently
replaced. Source-only readiness remains `admission: null`, and template
authority is not extended.

For v5 Claim-forms, parent, Form and quality histories share a bounded lock and
snapshot boundary, including final head checks. The mandatory
`subject_assessment` companion retains current parent admission and historical
withdrawals separately. Missing parent read scope refuses the operation;
positive parent status cannot admit an unreviewed Form. A qualified reading
does not hide an unreviewed, disputed, rejected or withdrawn parent. Older
strict schema consumers must update or reject this companion, not strip it.

The final shared regression passed 163 tests plus 908 subtests in 502.63
seconds. Focused parent/quality checks passed 8 tests plus 10 subtests; public
form compatibility passed 15 tests. These use synthetic mechanics fixtures,
not extra historical evidence. They include current quality and parent
changes, missing/revoked scope, non-admitted forms, preserved source-copy
language/context, freeform compatibility and final parent-head drift.

Single renewed-command observations were: Layer append 3.43 s, Claim
append/repeat 2.69/2.80 s, Form append/repeat 2.27/1.92 s and actual Form
materialization 2.21 s. Peak process RSS was about 49 MiB. These exclude model
inference and full issuer review; they are neither cold-cache nor p95 nor
corpus-scale throughput measurements.
