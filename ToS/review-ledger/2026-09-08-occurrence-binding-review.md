## Source and operation boundaries

The [Occurrence contract](../contracts/occurrence-description-record.schema.json)
and [registry explanation](../doctrine/semantic-interchange/README.md#exact-bound-occurrence-descriptions)
describe a particular use, with its own subject identity and correctable
account. Its immutable complete native binding returns to the exact TextUnit,
segmentation, ordered Anchors and frozen textual layer. The native objects,
their granularity, proposed boundaries and historical reviews are not rewritten
or replaced. A sentence description does not make its unit a word token.

Entity registry 24 uses the existing semantic metadata adapter. Mandatory
occurrence/context accounts, scope and continuity criterion are exposed as
typed properties; name/hover forms retain all of them and the native binding.
Relation registry 23 gives separate qualified Claims to a LexicalForm, Lexeme
and contextual Sense, with concrete endpoints, evidence, statement, basis and
attestation scope. No assignment is automatic, transitive or globally unique.
The optional source-Claim polarity is positive, negative or unknown; omission
is unspecified. Ordinary descriptive revision cannot reverse this
identity-bearing field, and the flag does not rewrite a source statement.

Common source creation verifies the declared exact public UTF-8 representation.
Catalog, forms and descriptive revision check metadata and dependency closure
without opening text. A nonpublic native binding is refused even when the
description has no quotation: an endpoint or short-span digest can disclose
private text. Existing DTA, eKGWB and operator-held IA restrictions remain.
No retained real text was copied, reclassified or cleared for publication.

Assessment through the [source-owner command](../../mechanics/growth-cycle/parts/branch-growth-cycle/README.md#descriptions-bound-to-native-text)
requires an explicitly selected exact read of the same complete binding.
This includes an Occurrence used by a selected Claim or source-bound human
form. Metadata-only, unrelated-unit, same-unit/different-packet and fabricated
inline selections cannot substitute. Missing exact reading makes current
admission unusable, including on metadata inspection of earlier positive
history; it does not erase that history. Direct append and freeform
materialization enforce the condition independently of discovery.

Protected native dependency snapshots enter creation, form/revision, Claim
grounding and assessment currentness. The assessment writer checks under lock
before evaluation/replay and after writing an immutable blob before publishing
the head. Late failure may leave an unpublished orphan blob. These are bounded
checks; the issuer still owns stable multi-file inputs, not a filesystem
transaction or protection from hostile same-account code.

## Verification

not real linguistic or rights evidence. They exercise common create, forms,
graph roundtrip, assessment, correction and exact replay; immutable binding
and scope, unknown fields, competing and negative assignments, wrong endpoint

checks native identity drift/collision, then creates a freshly prepared Claim
without admission. Helper's final five-test run passed in 47.560 seconds;
root inspected the complete file and separately reran both new suites.
