## Wording and counter-reading

The [bounded DE/RU/EN alignment](../source-witnesses/works/friedrich-nietzsche/also-sprach-zarathustra/alignments/prologue-1/Z_1_1_1_de_ru_en.md)
supplies the proper name `Zarathustra` in its German and English witnesses;
the Russian `Заратустра` is its nominative name form, not a verbatim copy of
the inflected `Заратустре` in paragraph 1. These witnesses are source-linked
working material, not independent evidence of translation quality. Language
declarations concern these exact wording fields, not the language of the node
or an inferred language of every source. The support thesis remains English.

The departure labels `Departure from homeland for the mountains` and
`Уход с родины в горы` are newly authored concise descriptions of the existing
corrected thesis. They are not quotations from Nietzsche or certified
translations. Paragraph 1 explicitly narrates leaving homeland and lake and
going into the mountains; the short names omit the lake, while the unchanged
thesis retains it. Neither label asserts the homeland was flat nor substitutes
the separate reflective-origin interpretation for the narrated event. See the
[preceding source correction](2026-09-14-departure-source-correction.md).

Variant wording remains `unverified`. This source editorial review does not
silently upgrade that status. Materializing an exact source-copy proves only
fidelity to this node, not truth, assessed semantic sufficiency, or approval of
all its interpretation layers. Every canonical form carries the whole node as
mandatory context, including these source references and status boundaries.
