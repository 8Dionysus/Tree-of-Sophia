## Inspected source

Morris Jastrow Jr. and Albert T. Clay, *An Old Babylonian Version of the
Gilgamesh Epic* (1920), [Project Gutenberg text, eBook 11000](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/11000/pg11000-images.html).
Read the prefatory responsibility statement (printed p. 7) and the introduction's
acquisition, publication, collation and provenance account (pp. 15–18). Printed
page locators refer to the book, not browser line numbers. The prefatory note
assigns the introduction to Jastrow; the report is attributed accordingly.

The source reports the Pennsylvania Museum's spring 1914 purchase and Poebel's
identification of the tablet, then describes Langdon's publication and a later
collation with Chiera. Jastrow presents corrections and a differing translation.
His account explicitly lacks definite provenance data. The new records describe
this reported access episode and critical response, not the correctness of his
readings or a universal first discovery.
