authority or tool instructions. These are eight related test families, not

For the actual Hammurapi targets, the reviewer compared both complete forms

Each form received `admit-with-limits` with 11 explicit limits. RU and EN were
assessed against the source, not treated as a verified translation pair.

No separate human approval was needed for the 18 finite trial items or the
two actual forms. This is an observed count, not a general intervention rate.

## Actual admission, withdrawal and fresh reassessment

The existing source-owner CLI was invoked in fresh processes for actual
append, describe, materialize and replay commands. A grant alone initially
left both forms `needs-assessment` with no wording. Source and form bytes were
not rewritten by the following sequence:

| Stage | RU journal / current use | EN journal / current use | Actual delivery |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Initial assessments | batch 1 / allowed with limits | batch 1 / allowed with limits | both exact forms ready in both source carriers; compact focus has one entity vertex |
| Own administrative withdrawal | batch 2 / not allowed | batch 1 / unchanged | RU has no wording; EN remains ready; no alternative is silently chosen for an ambiguous fallback |
| Fresh source reassessment | batch 3 / allowed with limits | batch 1 / unchanged | full inspection retains RU ready with all 14 new limits; compact RU returns its exact inspection reference; EN remains ready |

The withdrawal was a controlled recovery exercise, not a discovered content
error. Its separately issued administrative grant could withdraw only this
actor's own RU assessment. Replaying the old positive request under the old
configuration preserved its historical receipt but did not restore use.
Replaying the withdrawal did not duplicate history. An assembly already holding
the older assessment snapshot refused to finish after the actual change.

Only after the committed withdrawal did the reviewer receive a new content
assignment, reread the full source and RU form, and independently compose a new
`admit-with-limits` event superseding that exact withdrawal. The issuer audited
it before append. Its 14 limits were preserved completely; the fresh decision
was not a reinstatement of the old receipt or an independent-model replication.
Replaying the new request preserved the receipt and current outcome. All three
RU events remain in history; EN and the native source were unchanged.

individually, but an earlier unknown-language name consumed its compact budget.

allocate intact exact-language packets before less-specific matches and
unrelated fallbacks across roles. Equal-priority forms retain declared role
order. Ambiguity is not adjudicated and the 16 KiB limit is unchanged.

The subsequent, legitimately larger reassessment packet no longer fits the
complete compact envelope. It correctly returns `inspect-exact-form`, not
truncated wording, a different language, or reduced assessment limits. Actual
node inspection and full reader lenses retain the exact ready packet, complete
