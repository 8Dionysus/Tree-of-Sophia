## Independent findings and corrections

- V4 initially hashed parsed owner configuration but not its raw bytes. A
  whitespace-only edit between describe and append could therefore survive
  expected-snapshot comparison. V4 now binds both; legacy semantics stay intact.
- Private form validation initially consulted runtime-global schemas without
  binding the selected context's form grammar. A fresh required field there
  was ignored. Selecting and hashing that grammar fixed validation, but review
  then demonstrated a second root in the renderer: a valid context-specific
  form extension was assessed yet refused during display. The completed seam
  now supplies one fresh protected schema snapshot through history, engine,
  journal and renderer, including after a previous renderer call.
- The initial language guard looked only at private selected bodies. A private
  form could bind a public German source while its scope and competence omitted
  German. Language requirements now follow the exact combined public/private
  selected sources; adding a language only to scope still does not enlarge the
  independent authority or competence record.
- Applying that language closure to native units as well initially coupled
  independent unit scopes: two individually valid units in different languages
  could no longer be described together. Native targets now retain their
  separately verified own-language scope; source/form binding closure stays
  separate. The joint-selection regression uses exact synthetic native records.

During verification, an initially incorrect schema-domain check was corrected
to the existing `treeofsophia.local` identities; no schema IDs were renamed.
A schema-negative test was narrowed to expect the actual `ValidationError`.
Neither correction weakens the source contract.
