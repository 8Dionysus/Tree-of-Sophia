name/caption/hover/statement Forms received separate source-visible agent
judgments under the existing [assessment law](../doctrine/KNOWLEDGE_ASSESSMENT.md)
and policy v3. All returned `admit-with-limits` for the delegated low-risk

source context before native append. Native append, unchanged replay and
current admission checks then completed, with zero additional per-record human
approvals. Source records and their historical embedded review fields stayed
unchanged. A Form's quality decision is not the parent Claim's decision.

The cohort is finite and completely audited, with one conservatively grouped
documentary origin and a correlated model family. It is not an independent
quorum, general language calibration, population accuracy estimate or proof
that every future summary is correct. Source-sensitive limitations include:

- editorial attribution is not autograph authentication, dispatch, delivery
  or reading; a person is not the printing firm;
- the commentary's book attribution and commissioning report differ from the
  letter's own wording; a commentary citing that letter is not an independent
  second witness;
- document, inventory leaf and printed page retain distinct identities;
- catalogue date/origin/destination are attributed fields, not established
  historical movement or event dates; provisional places and unspecified
  calendar metadata remain provisional or unknown;
- the supplied catalogue-body representation did not independently confirm
  its surrounding heading/navigation; the decisions retain that limit;
- shortened wording is usable only with its complete mandatory Claim context.

These are reviewed judgments with restricted consequences, not a rewrite of
historical truth, rights, source provenance or canon. The local journal remains
the authority for the recorded acts. Its historical receipts do not establish
that a grant is still current when this note is read.
