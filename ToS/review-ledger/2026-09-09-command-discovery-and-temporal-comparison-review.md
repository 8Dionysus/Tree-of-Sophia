## Boundary checklist

- **Yes — source return and separate layers.** Full Claim identity, version,
  predicate, source context, uncertainty, polarity and qualification remain
  inspectable. Discovery is implementation availability, not authorized-now.
  Neither operation changes source or confers admission.
- **Yes — time and plurality.** Only explicit exact historical-time date
  envelopes with compatible declared calendar and year numbering compare.
  Before/after, equal, contains/contained-by and overlap describe inclusive
  envelopes, not simultaneity, duration, causality, identity or historical
  truth. Negated and disputed propositions are not affirmed by arithmetic.
- **Yes — exactness and bounded reads.** Missing or ambiguous selected IDs
  fail; aliases are not resolved. Source/content revisions must match. Warm
  comparison uses at most four indexed node reads; D1 also verifies its
  surrounding publication revision. This does not make cold preparation free.
- **Yes — malformed input stays visible without false success.** Unknown
  temporal meaning yields an explicit unsupported/undetermined result.
  Invalid structural containers fail the read with HTTP 503; the reader does
  not scrub a corrupted carrier into a schema-valid successful operand.
- **Yes — recovery and history.** Discovery reuses the connected handler
  grammar and binds it into new preparation dependencies. Old committed
  receipts keep their historical meaning and exact retained bytes; stale
  preparation cannot borrow the new implementation's authority.
- **Not applicable.** No new philosophical profile, counterpart, canon,
