## Owned change and identity

The registry's explicit `structured-reference-value-v1` reader interprets only
the fixed `/object/members` slot. The first profile is
`occurrence_motif_proposal`: one qualified Claim over two through eight exact
Occurrence records. Proposed signification, grouping basis, scope, contrast and
limitations belong to the whole proposal. Its formulation is marked as research
paraphrase; an exact witness quotation keeps its separate anchored contract.
The focal subject stays a member. Removing that focal requires a separately
formulated successor, not silent retargeting of the Claim.

The Claim ID owns candidate identity. Its literal projection is neither a Sign
nor an independently owned collection; equal values in separate Claims do not
merge those candidates. Array order is serialization order, not chronology.
All participants are typed source dependencies. Unknown extensions remain
inert and preserved; the older structured-value reader is unchanged. Rationale
and alternatives are recorded in
`docs/decisions/TOS-D-0056-claim-scoped-reference-values.md`.

Public create/revision v4 and private command v2 independently require the
exact object value and each member in its object role, including the focal.
Older grants cannot acquire this interpretation or wording-only correction by
a registry change. Private selector allowlists may cover present/proposed
versions, but all permissions are preflighted and each frozen reader consumes
only that version's exact declared closure. Unused selectors are not evidence.
Current and retained-request source/native closure is rechecked on replay,
including after a later correction removed one of the old request's members.
Original receipts, predecessor bytes and human-form histories are retained.

Assessment uses the existing public/private source reader and policy engine.
Every selected Occurrence and exact native unit/layer is required evidence;
forms additionally require the Claim. Admission follows those exact versions,
not a participant count or an old receipt. No policy or competence grant was
issued by this change.

## Projection and human reading

`has_value_member` edges and `value_member_node_ids` carry structural returns
within the Claim context, not accepted independent membership assertions.
Python and Worker compact paths require every declared member node and exactly
its member edge; details and mandatory reading context retain the full set.
Missing or malformed context prevents folding. A focused or shared participant
does not lose its own neighborhood.

Independent review found a separate normalization gap: removing both the
member marker and all member edges could make exported Motif content look
like an ordinary pair. Canonical source-backed rebuild already refused that
export, but the standalone access route loads exported JSON directly. The
normalizer now checks the explicit reference profile against the retained
source Claim, projected object/literal, typed members, complete trace and
structural edge origins. It rejects inconsistent carriers without source IO,
whole-repository rebuild, heuristic recovery or reference scanning in prose.
Thirteen independent JSON-carrier mutations exposed eleven missing refusals
before the fix; all thirteen are refused after it.

The separate UI owner has prepared member-context consumption without scene,
camera or artistic changes. Its local fixture/browser and frontend checks are
not joint HTTP acceptance of this root snapshot; that integration remains a
separate next check.
