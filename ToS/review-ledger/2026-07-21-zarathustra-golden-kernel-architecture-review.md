# 2026-07-21 Zarathustra Golden Kernel Architecture Review

## What changed

- named *Thus Spoke Zarathustra* as the first golden growth kernel rather than
  only a bounded public-entry route;
- coupled that kernel to a separate world-philosophy corpus-soil front;
- defined stable evidence and review lineage as the foundation beneath
  revisable semantic entities and interpretations;
- preserved the five-year Dionysus lived route as first-person provenance while
  keeping it distinct from source, bibliography, scholarship, and canon;
- made accepted, rejected, ambiguous, and counter-reading outcomes part of the
  future gold-packet standard;
- routed implementation pressure through witness, doctrine/contracts,
  candidate, review, canon, and derived owners without claiming that the packet
  or corpus-soil contracts already exist.

## Most at-risk checklist items

- turning Zarathustra into a universal ontology for philosophy;
- confusing lived depth with textual or scholarly authority;
- calling accepted labels "gold" while losing refusal and uncertainty;
- treating the existing tiny-entry capsule as a completed whole-book kernel;
- freezing semantic entities in the name of a stable foundation;
- letting graph, KAG, embedding, or model surfaces become hidden owners.

## Review result

- `yes` the kernel transfers a source-and-review method, not Nietzsche-specific
  answers or vocabulary;
- `yes` the evidence spine remains stable while semantic objects stay versioned
  and contestable;
- `yes` lived witness is explicit but cannot settle source meaning or canon
  promotion;
- `yes` human review retains ownership of accepted, rejected, revised,
  deferred, ambiguous, and counter-reading outcomes;
- `yes` the public tiny-entry remains a bounded entrance rather than the whole
  golden kernel;
- `yes` corpus soil remains future owner work and is not falsely presented as
  implemented;
- `yes` downstream graph, retrieval, evaluation, and runtime surfaces remain
  projections below ToS-authored authority.

## What remains unresolved

- the exact corpus-soil record and source-address contract;
- the annotation unit and layer vocabulary for a full Zarathustra gold packet;
- the machine-readable shape for negative, ambiguous, and counter-reading
  examples;
- the review and version-lineage receipt contract;
- the first unseen-material and cross-work transfer eval;
- how much of the founder's private reading history should ever become a public
  authored witness beyond the bounded provenance statement recorded here.

## Boundary note

This landing prepares the owner surfaces and review law. It does not begin mass
annotation, corpus ingestion, model training, or private-note import.

The next pass belongs first to corpus-soil and gold-packet contract design.
