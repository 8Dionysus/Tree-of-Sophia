## Counter-reading and limits

The paragraph does not determine whether the homeland itself was mountainous.
The correction therefore neither asserts a flat homeland nor disputes its
possible geography; it removes the unsupported geographic modifier and states
the expressly narrated direction. It does not identify a real-world birthplace
or historical journey.

The separately marked [reflective-origin synthesis](../canon/synthesis/friedrich-nietzsche/thus-spoke-zarathustra/prologue-1/departure-from-reflective-origin/node.json)
already owns that interpretation. It remains unchanged, byte SHA-256
`ba3e258dacbb7a625f018fb44712a5ef33a5e17c6912f93f36b7fba0ac38b2ec`.
Its continued presence is not new acceptance of the interpretation. Source
witnesses, intake wording, relation IDs, endpoints, interpretation-layer lists
and all past reviews remain unchanged. No edition or translation status is
upgraded, and no rights or publication permission is inferred.
