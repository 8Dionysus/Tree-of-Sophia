# Synthetic repository artifact boundary

This assessment concerns explicitly declared capacity-fixture repository
resources. Each resource is a newly produced deterministic byte object,
distinct from its Artifact witness JSON, catalog entry, discovery plan and
rights metadata. Its identity binds the actual workload declaration, ordinal
and preserved resource bytes. It does not identify an archaeological object,
an ancient inscription, a museum holding or an upstream author's work.

The bounded research question is how source-owned identity survives delivery
through a generated catalog and packed storage. A byte object, its descriptive
witness and a projection are different things even when one software operation
produces them together. This distinction is the philosophical planting of the
fixture: a usable representation does not confer textual, interpretive or
canonical authority on its referent. It establishes no historical analogy.

The explicit synthetic Artifact v1 template uses `other` and repository
identity. Physical measurements and archaeological circumstances remain
unknown or inapplicable; no fabricated excavation or museum custody is
permitted. Resource preservation is verified by the actual producer and
candidate source owner. An uncaptured v1 catalog fingerprint does not assert
a native v2 creation capture. Discovery input may describe a planned local
repository route; it must not fabricate a network query, download, timing or
captured external response.

As a model-authored assessment, this note permits only a descriptive
metadata-only posture within the private synthetic experiment. Copyright is
not evaluated, no jurisdiction or holder is adjudicated, and no payload
redistribution, third-party license or rights transfer is granted. Each
generated rights record must bind its actual new Artifact ID and retain this
qualification. Historical source rights and permissions remain separate.

The three source JSON prototypes are templates, not admitted Artifact
witnesses or execution receipts. Their declared fields must be instantiated
coherently for the actual new resource, rights, discovery and provenance
members. The maintained family predicates, global identity/reference checks,
complete source EOF and admission remain necessary. No result is claimed here
for the benchmark, 100K admission, 1B records, concurrent agents or native v2
mutation cost.
