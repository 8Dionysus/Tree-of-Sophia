The selected set is partial relative to the inquiry. The deliberate reading
order is poem → portion (8) → Burnet's book → Burnet's quoting passage → Jenseits.
It moves between whole and part, earlier referent and later presentation, then
to another existing route. This is a research itinerary, not chronology,
causation, textual sequence, priority or completeness. It is expressed as a
total order only among these five selected subjects; unrelated objects are
neither excluded from future research nor asserted absent.
