## Compatibility and continuation

Existing Python/Worker readers still consume their built-in constants; this
change does not claim that they switched to the descriptor. QueryVocabulary
version 1 faithfully captures their observed constants but its use in Rust
requires a native validator, a compatible adapter implementation, an eighth
source fixture, and a complete full knowledge graph/catalog producer. The
current Python knowledge builder also synthesizes repository topology and
semantic-interchange joins. Those remain explicitly registered derived
adapters, never authored source assertions. A source cut or registry change
must invalidate the selected derived binding, even if this authored descriptor
does not change. The compiler and query owners carry that remaining work;
rights and publication remain with their owners. Local mechanical checks and
any compatibility difference must be appended to execution evidence before
integration or publication claims.
