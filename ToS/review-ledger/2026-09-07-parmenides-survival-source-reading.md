Palmer attributes the poem to Parmenides and describes its incomplete survival
through quotations by later writers. He distinguishes those fragments from
reports/paraphrases and notes uneven preservation across the poem. The
transmitted title *On Nature* is described as probably unauthentic. The new
record therefore uses a descriptive name, not an asserted original title.
