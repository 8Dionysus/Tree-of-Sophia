## Layer and authority review

- **Source traceability: yes.** Construction retains the exact acquired File,
  container member, structural selector, input fixity, extraction policy,
  configuration and transformation event. A first TextUnit can be constructed
  without inventing a predecessor TextUnit; later refinement keeps its existing
  route. Original bytes and prior layers are not overwritten.
- **Layer distinctions: yes.** The first profile is bounded structural XHTML
  extraction, not OCR, correction, diplomatic transcription, original-language
  fidelity or translation verification. Its raw layer remains an unreviewed
  machine transcription. Exact reproduction is a mechanical fact, not a
  fabricated human review or semantic verdict.
- **Rights and privacy: yes.** Current exact source-read and derivative-write
  permissions are distinct from historical creation configuration. Item and
  layer rights are checked before payload reads. Original payload and private
  metadata use their declared separate roots. The public assessment adapter
  rejects private v4/v5 configurations before private I/O; ordinary discovery
  does not issue grants. No private record is promoted into a public catalog.
- **Assessment scope: yes.** The v5 owner derives the comparison and current
  quality basis from exact selected source records and the journal. It does
  not trust an inline positive quality record. A dependent subject must bind
  every required layer for its actual purpose and cite the current exact basis.
  Authority, competence, execution and source visibility remain necessary;
  policy v3 grants no universal textual-fidelity or publication verdict.
- **Revision and plurality: yes.** Historical decisions retain the assertion
  layer, risk, languages, maker and requested use committed with their batch.
  A later configuration cannot repurpose that history or supersede across its
  scope. Withdrawal, revocation and changed quality evidence stop current use;
  restoring quality does not automatically resurrect an old dependent review.
- **Negative decisions: yes.** Available comparison evidence permits a qualified
  rejection or withdrawal even when positive use is disallowed. Metadata-only
  access does not permit any source-visible decision. This distinction applies
  to the entire required layer closure, not just the target record.
- **Human forms: yes within the tested scope.** The exact whole quality basis
  is mandatory context. A displayed form carries the current limitations;
  missing, withdrawn or changed admission prevents display without deleting
  the authored form or its history.
- **Owner boundary: yes.** Deterministic extraction, source judgment, scoped
  admission, public projection and deployment remain separate. Ordered locks
  coordinate the selected journal heads during a bounded operation; they do
  not promise arbitrary cross-subject or cross-root atomic transactions.

Counterpart mapping, compost, lived witness, canon promotion and tiny-entry
