# AGENTS.md

This card applies to derived read models under `ToS/derived-exports/`.

## Role

Exports return consumers to authored ToS sources. Change the source or native
builder first, then regenerate into an explicitly selected data artifact.
Standalone delivery belongs to `access/`; consumer indexing and orchestration
belong to the consumer.

## Boundary Routes

- Corpus and lexical indexes retain source bindings. A lexical plan controls
  source-withholding fields; recoverable form hashes do not grant publication
  or confidentiality clearance.
- Philosophy atlas and graph-view catalogs expose branch review material and
  source-owned lenses. Materialize one graph node/edge set; each view carries
  stable membership IDs rather than duplicate records.
- The bibliographic Claim graph stays separate from the atlas graph. Keep the
  Claim as relation center, literal objects as literals, and edges linked to
  evidence, maker, provenance and review. Work/Expression/Edition/Item routes
  remain reified Claims; embodiment does not establish textual equivalence.
- Read-only corpus queries require an explicit selector and exact source
  parity. They fail on overflow instead of silently truncating and create no
  review, relation, runtime or service authority.

## Validation

Select the relevant export, generated-parity or public-entry route from
[ToS/VALIDATION.md](../VALIDATION.md). [RELEASING](../../docs/RELEASING.md)
separates software verification from corpus/export publication. Downstream
trust and admission remain with each consumer.
