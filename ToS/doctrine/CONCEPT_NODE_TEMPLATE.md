# Concept Node Template

A concept node gives a recurring semantic thread a stable identity across source-linked readings.

## Core fields

The scaffold uses the shared [node contract](NODE_CONTRACT.md):

- `node_id`
- `node_type = concept`
- `source_anchor`
- `key_terms`
- `distilled_thesis`
- `relations`
- `interpretation_layers`

## Use the scaffold when

- The concept needs a stable authored handle.
- Several source-linked readings illuminate the same semantic thread.
- The thesis describes that thread and records the scope of its interpretation.

## Source and language

The source node centres on a work or passage; the concept node centres on the
semantic thread traced through it. A `commentary-on` relation may connect the
concept to a shared multilingual source node.

Concept identity is language-neutral. Language witnesses, lexemes and variant
names retain their own provenance and relations to the concept.

## Worked scaffold

The concept and source examples form a pair in the trilingual Zarathustra
route. Further concept growth follows source reading and scoped review.
