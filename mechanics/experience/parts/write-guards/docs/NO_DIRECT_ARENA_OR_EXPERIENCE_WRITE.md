# NO DIRECT ARENA OR EXPERIENCE WRITE

## Purpose

Blocks direct arena, assistant, Codex, or cross-repo experience writes into ToS
canon. Candidate review flow and owner gates are maintained in the
[candidate-review contract](../../candidate-review/docs/TOS_CANDIDATE_DOSSIER_REVIEW.md).

## Local guard

This guard covers the direct-write attempt itself. Cross-repo or assistant
proposals stay reviewable until the owning source and canon routes accept them;
this surface grants no write authority.

## Notes

The guard does not replace source-owner review, bounded evaluation, or the
canon admission route.
