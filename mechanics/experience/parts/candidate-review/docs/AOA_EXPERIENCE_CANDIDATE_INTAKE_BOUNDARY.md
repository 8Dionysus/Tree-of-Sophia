# AOA EXPERIENCE CANDIDATE INTAKE BOUNDARY

## Intake boundary

Defines what a ToS candidate dossier may contain and what it must not do. The
shared flow, evidence requirements, owner routes, promotion gates, and review
invariants live in [TOS_CANDIDATE_DOSSIER_REVIEW](TOS_CANDIDATE_DOSSIER_REVIEW.md).

This intake surface accepts a dossier for review; it does not itself approve
promotion, change an owner route, or activate runtime state.
