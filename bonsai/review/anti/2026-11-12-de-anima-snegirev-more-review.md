# Aristotle, De anima: further Snegirev places

This follows the [II.1 review](2026-11-12-de-anima-snegirev-review.md) of the
[1885 Snegirev edition](https://archive.org/details/aristotle_psychology),
adding five III.4 places on pp. 81–82 and II.1 412b4–6 on pp. 40–41. The
same public-domain translation, source-visible transcription method, page
images and source-bound limits apply. The III.4 source is printed continuously
across the two pages; page breaks and footnote cues are not part of the
transcription.

III.4 429a10–22 was aligned as a continuous Greek and Russian span using
`alignWitnesses` in `bonsai/lib/align.mjs`, and the five stand places were
extracted from the paired whole sentences. The first two Greek sentences and
their two Russian counterparts form one alignment group, so a place
intersecting either receives both sentences. The underlying First1K TEI has
`ε τε` where another existing stand passage gives `εἴτε`; for exact location
matching that one variant was mapped to the TEI spelling. No Greek witness
text was changed and no Greek textual correction is claimed. The retained
candidate transcription, alignment, and extracted matches have SHA-256
`da65383c66ad9f57b38acbe32dd163ef78517166175861ebc498c3026e17b108`,
`b0c7810f862ae0febc0f003238a6b2c3e35168fe46ad6b2df900b9a6056bf22f`, and
`8b02b1d1e31ed897bca2f11485e3752c0530d66230c1448466ae85f3fbdfd202`.

For 412b4–6, the initial wider alignment was displaced, as the preceding
review records. A second bounded alignment of the continuous plant/root,
soul-definition and wax passage in both languages produced matching sentence
groups, which were checked against the source; the complete mapped Russian
sentences are now supplied. Its retained alignment and matches have SHA-256
`6553ef112b2be8a6395d2ada320d47b16a317d6ce66d2b6b4eab0162dc39c545` and
`9b1526521ab00e301e2eff310c5982bac3c40f670274f92bbb112b6491949ff6`.
The edition's errata say the p. 41 spelling «бытіе» should read «бытія»;
the quotation retains the printed form and records the correction.

These are bounded stand translations, not complete-book alignment or a
source-owner, rights-owner or canon admission. At that stage the remaining I.1, II.2 and
II.3 places still required their own source-visible checks, recorded below.

The remaining three stand places were then transcribed and checked against
pp. 13–14 (I.1 402a6–7), p. 43 (II.2 413b11–13), and p. 45 (II.3
414a29–31). Three separately numbered section pairs went through
`alignWitnesses`; every pair and its two sentence groups aligned 1:1 and was
checked against the printed Russian and existing Greek. Their retained
transcription and alignment receipts have SHA-256
`dd98ba0e79ce5b1b5d6b35ac3b6527dd2ba4f969375e9dcc72d86ff843f758fa` and
`c640c0c070c654a7bd232197d04d54cdf8563494c66c5c9ed2eadd3029a10ea3`.
The printed Russian list of capacities in II.3 omits αἰσθητικόν (sensation),
which the Greek includes. The omission is noted and the historical witness is
retained as printed. This completes the Russian counterparts of the 21 places
currently held in this stand; it does not establish a complete or reviewed
translation of the full work.
