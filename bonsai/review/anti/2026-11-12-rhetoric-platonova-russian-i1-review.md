# Rhetoric I.1: Russian Platonova witness, 12 November 2026

Scope: the 1894 translation by N. N. Platonova (Shamonina), printed pages 1–7,
PDF pages 14–20 of the Internet Archive scan. The separately retained scan has
SHA-256 `547c70691e55ceb6879de09d86570853ed44d6a48df701fe34bbf9bf046df18e`.
The independent manual transcription omits the printed chapter synopsis,
footnotes, typographical accents, page-end hyphenation and line wraps; it keeps
historical spelling and bracketed additions in the main text. It is a partial
witness, not an assertion of a complete Russian Rhetoric. The modern digital
scan itself is not republished. Platonova's identity, 1861–1928 dates, and
translation are documented by https://aristoteles.philosophy.nsc.ru/node/216.

The stand cites the opening Greek unit `tos.text-unit.sid-687a8faa55b74f62963435475ae0781f`
within offsets 0–613. I compared its two cited groups with the 1894 scan's
opening on printed pages 1–2 and with the alignment produced by
`bonsai/lib/corpus-align.mjs`: offsets 0–308 correspond to the two Russian
sentences from «Риторика — искусство…» through «…обвинять»; offsets 309–613
to the next two sentences, from «Въ этихъ случаяхъ…» through «…согласится
каждый». The line-wrap split between the first two printed pages has been
joined. Both cited ranges correspond in sense and order; no modern reprint
wording was substituted. The rest of the chapter was transcribed directly but
is not claimed to have been independently proofread sentence by sentence.

The scope binds this version to this one Greek chapter only. The algorithm also
forms pairs for the rest of that chapter; those pairs have not all been
reviewed and must be checked against source and translation before any new
citations to those parts are admitted. Subsequent chapters have no Russian
mapping under this partial witness. The full historical work remains a
separate acquisition and transcription task.
