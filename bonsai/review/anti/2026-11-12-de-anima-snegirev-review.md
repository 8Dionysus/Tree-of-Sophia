# Aristotle, De anima II.1: Snegirev's Russian translation

Twelve cited places are supplied from V. A. Snegirev's *Изследованіе о душѣ*,
Kazan, 1885, pp. 40–42. The [Internet Archive copy](https://archive.org/details/aristotle_psychology)
is a scan of that printing; the title page names Snegirev and the date. The
translator died in 1889. The independently transcribed nineteenth-century
translation is public domain on the stated Russian, US and EU term bases. The
source item marks itself public domain; the scan is not redistributed here.
Bibliographic research reports an omission in III.12; it is outside these
II.1 passages, and no completeness of the translation is asserted.

The selected Russian source text was transcribed from and compared against
printed pages 40–42. Pre-reform spelling, wording and punctuation are kept;
line breaks within words and page breaks are joined. The printed section
numbers and footnote cues are omitted. The scan includes a printed errata list
on the page after p. 98; the recorded errata on pp. 40–42 do not change these
selected passages. The scan PDF SHA-256 is
`93a95ce99f34e721ba7b4fa398cc79e6d89a682019337e004ed26281d458b58a`.

For the alignment, the continuous Greek II.1 span from the already selected
Bekker/First1K witness was aligned with the corresponding continuous Russian
span using `alignWitnesses` in `bonsai/lib/align.mjs`; the pairs were reviewed
against both texts and the pages. The cited Greek sentences were located
exactly in the aligned source span, and the target text consists of the
corresponding whole Russian sentences. A Russian sentence may render several
Greek sentences, so quotations can include adjacent material. This is a
bounded comparison, not a full-book or critical-edition collation. The retained
candidate transcription, alignment and extracted matches have SHA-256 values
respectively `54003769dd9b359242e5266feb0d32d0b11e54d8d8f2df378fec0ae6d4f393d2`,
`fc744487d1cfe15e2a9da3ba3e001de3673eaf4f98722c801b66ce030469ce32` and
`db21bb54c35ab5deeba18177aa0406dc7a5b02be610c526f087a4f731a8a9112`.

The aligner's pairs around Bekker 412b4–6 were displaced: the Russian sentence
about roots was grouped with the preceding Greek sentence, and the following
Russian sentence was skipped. That place remains without Russian text until
its alignment can be established; these incorrect pairs are not published as
its counterpart. All other places outside the reviewed twelve remain separate
work. This is stand material and makes no canon or complete corpus claim.
