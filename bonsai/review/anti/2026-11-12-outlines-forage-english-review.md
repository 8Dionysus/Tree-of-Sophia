# Sextus Empiricus, Outlines: Forage English reading

The Forage October 2026 edition explicitly says its English was translated
from Greek with Claude and then edited, without review by a human specialist.
The publisher permits reuse with attribution under CC BY-SA 4.0. This edition
is separate from Patrick's 1899 Book I translation. No human specialist review
or independent full translation collation is claimed here.

The captured EPUB has 241, 259 and 281 sequential numbered sections in Books
I, II and III. Its two different section-marker classes were both counted;
there are no missing or duplicate section numbers. Four unnumbered prose
continuations are attached to their preceding sections, and all 73 publisher
notes are carried to their corresponding sections. Editorial chapter headings
and summaries remain in the retained EPUB rather than the reading body. EPUB,
web page, PDF, and the provider's terms are retained in local artifact custody;
the exact EPUB digest and capture URLs are recorded in the source metadata.

The extracted beginnings and endings of all three books were checked against
the EPUB; I.1 and I.8 were also compared to the stand's Greek and Patrick
passages, and II.13 to the Greek and Bury passages. These are bounded checks
of source coverage and meaning, not a complete philological review. The
extraction retains the publisher's angle-bracket editorial additions and note
texts; formatting of verse lines and references is a reading transformation.
