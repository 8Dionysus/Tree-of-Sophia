# Xenophon, Memorabilia: remaining Russian stand passages

This review extends the [III.8.4–7 review](2026-11-12-xenophon-ru-iii-8-review.md)
to the remaining nine Xenophon places cited by the stand. The source and
public-domain translation are the same 1879 Yanchevetsky edition. The scanned
images are research evidence and are not redistributed by this change.

Page and line breaks within words are joined; original spelling and wording
are kept, and margin section numerals are omitted. Exact sections were
compared to the Greek place and passed through `alignWitnesses` in
`bonsai/lib/align.mjs`, grouped by chapter. All nine section groups align 1:1;
this bounded alignment does not establish complete-book alignment. The locally
retained alignment receipt has SHA-256
`28363b7a6f42a9cc9f706f19270543af0af780b9a44d72c62c65fac2f14749ca`.

In I.6.14 Yanchevetsky omits the Greek clause about teaching whatever good
Socrates has, and renders the last statement about friends as being useful to
one another. These are features of this historical translation, not silent
corrections. The passage is an incomplete witness to that Greek section.

All Xenophon places currently held in the stand have Russian text after this
change. This does not make the full scanned book a reviewed or published
corpus text, establish completeness of the historical translation, or admit
anything to the Tree's authored source.
