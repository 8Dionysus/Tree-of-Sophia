# Russian direction: research records

These files are the Russian direction's research for the Aesthetics works:
where a Russian translation stands (edition, translator, editor, dates,
rights, scans and catalogue evidence) and, for some works, candidate passages
with their page locations. One file per work, named by the work's packet id;
its `status` says how far the research has come.

Nothing here is planted. A candidate passage reaches the map only through the
planting line: `planting/kit.py` cuts its words from a fetched transcription or
the scan's OCR, the page decides every difference, a second reader checks it,
and the line accepts the packet (`planting/AGENTS.md`). The two `*-transcription.txt`
files are the makers' own readings of page images, kept as research evidence;
they are not sources a witness may cut from.

Two witnesses made from this research stay outside the map for now:

- Buffon, *Discours sur le style* (1786, Ozeretskovsky): its passages were cut
  from a transcription typed from the NEB page images, which law 1 of the
  planting line does not admit; the scan's own OCR needs a provenance route the
  kit does not yet give (`planting/NEEDS-ru.md`, 2026-11-19).
- Schiller, *Über das Pathetische* (1902, Weinberg): the accepted witness was
  cut from a locally typed excerpt; its repair, cut from the Internet Archive
  OCR of the same volume, was not finished (its gate stays shut).

What the direction needs from the line is in `planting/NEEDS-ru.md`.
