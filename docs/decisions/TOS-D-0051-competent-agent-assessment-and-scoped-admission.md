# Competent agent assessment and scoped admission

## Index Metadata

- Decision ID: TOS-D-0051
- Original date: 2026-09-05
- Surface classes: doctrine, contracts, mechanics/growth
- ToS layers: doctrine, contracts, review-ledger
- Tree classes: knowledge foundation, assessment, admission
- Guard families: source-first authority, exact-version review, scoped authority
- Posture: accepted

## Context

The founding dialogue describes one connected world with indefinitely deepening
branches. The operator's foundation-v1 goal explicitly requires growth beyond
the rate of manual human assessment. Several current doctrine paragraphs and
legacy packet schemas restricted all substantive judgment to humans. Removing
only that enum would blur identity, competence, actual authority and history.

## Decision

Use source-visible assessment by competent, authorized humans or agents and
derive current scoped admission separately. A trusted issuer grants reusable
scope by actor, task/layer, language, consequence, method and purpose. A review
binds exact assertion, source, policy, grant and competence records; neither
its prose nor a deterministic validator grants authority. Research admission
can proceed without per-record human signatures or prior canon acceptance.

This supersedes the universal human-only rule in the active growth doctrine,
corpus foundation, knowledge model and review checklist. Historical v1/v2
human-review records retain their meaning. New assessment events address old
assertions through explicit adapters rather than fabricated human entries.
Personal consent, authorship, rights, publication and canon retain their actual
owner authority. The read-only `access` product does not become a write owner.

## Options Considered

- Keep all agent output pending until manual approval: imposes a human throughput
  ceiling and contradicts the operator's stated scope.
- Relax `reviewer_kind` in place and trust `accepted`: loses historical meaning,
  competence and authority boundaries.
- Exact assessment events plus separate scoped admission: chosen; substantive
  work can scale while disagreements, revocation and stronger-owner boundaries
  remain inspectable.

## Consequences and limits

Method-qualified reuse avoids a repeated expensive review for each safe repeat.
Independent evidence and reviewers use trusted provenance, not counts of calls
or mirrored sources. Invalid successors cannot suppress good judgments; valid
corrections and withdrawals do not erase history. Dependent admission must be
recomputed after source, policy, method, competence or authority change.

The initial pure policy engine and synthetic tests prove mechanics only. The
foundation is not complete until a trusted durable command adapter, actual
language/task evaluation, corpus migration, dependency invalidation and human
and agent consumption also work. No production grant, competence certification,
canon promotion or publication is created by this decision note.
