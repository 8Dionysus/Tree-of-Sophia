## Context

Tree of Sophia already had two visible growth fronts: a world-philosophy atlas
and a bounded *Thus Spoke Zarathustra* route. Existing route law correctly kept
Zarathustra stronger than a generic sample, but it mostly explained the branch
as a narrow public-entry and canon route.

That did not preserve why Zarathustra is golden or what future agents should
learn from it. Without a stronger decision, later work could reduce it to a
demo dataset, transfer Nietzsche's ontology into every tradition, or widen the
world corpus before ToS has one inspectable full growth cycle.

The route also has explicit lived provenance: Dionysus has carried the work for
five years through repeated reading, listening, recitation, and memorization,
and through love, war, death, suffering, and joy. That provenance matters to
the choice of seed, but it needs a boundary so it is not confused with source
or scholarly authority.

## Decision

Adopt *Thus Spoke Zarathustra* as the first golden growth kernel of Tree of
Sophia.

The kernel is the first high-resolution, end-to-end worked route from source
witness and stable address through source-near observation, semantic proposal,
relation and context, human review, authored canon, and downstream graph or
retrieval projection. Gold material must preserve rejected and unresolved
readings, reviewer rationale, uncertainty, and version lineage, not only final
labels.

Keep this kernel coupled to a separate world-philosophy corpus-soil front. The
soil owns the ordered foundation across works, witnesses, editions, languages,
time, place, traditions, and transmission. The kernel teaches the method by
which that foundation may become reviewed knowledge.

Transfer the method and review discipline, not Nietzsche's ontology. A new
work or tradition may resist the current vocabulary and require ToS contracts
to change. Preserve lived witness as an authored first-person provenance layer
that can explain attention and salience but cannot replace primary text,
bibliographic evidence, scholarship, or canonical review.

## Options Considered

- Keep Zarathustra as only the current tiny-entry example. This preserves the
  existing narrow seam but loses the full-cycle learning role that makes the
  branch golden.
- Treat Zarathustra as a universal calibration ontology for the entire tree.
  This would give agents a single reusable vocabulary, but would flatten other
  works and traditions before reading them on their own terms.
- Build the widest possible corpus first and derive the method afterward. This
  would increase coverage quickly, but would multiply weak and inconsistent
  markup before one complete review path is understood.
- Couple world-philosophy corpus soil with Zarathustra as the first golden
  growth kernel. This preserves both breadth and a dense path of accountable
  learning.

## Rationale

The selected route gives future agents examples of judgment rather than a bag
of labels. It makes source return, layer distinction, rejection, ambiguity,
and human review learnable before ToS scales them.

The stable foundation is the evidence spine: witness identity, provenance,
addressable text, source-near observations, evidence status, and review
lineage. Semantic entities and interpretations remain versioned and
contestable. This allows the tree to deepen without pretending that every
entity is immutable once marked.

Separating method transfer from ontology transfer protects plurality. Keeping
lived witness distinct from textual and scholarly evidence protects source
authority while retaining the human root that gives this first seed its depth.

## Consequences

Future Zarathustra work should be evaluated by how completely and honestly it
exposes the growth cycle, not by annotation volume.

Gold packets should eventually include positive, negative, ambiguous, and
counter-reading examples with source anchors and review rationale. Agent
proposals remain provisional until human review accepts or revises them.

The current bounded prologue route remains valid but does not represent a
completed whole-book kernel. This decision does not authorize mass annotation,
model training, bulk corpus ingestion, or import of private reading notes.

The next architectural pass should define corpus soil and annotation-unit
contracts before wider planting. Downstream graph, KAG, retrieval, evaluation,
and runtime systems may consume bounded outputs but do not own ToS meaning.
