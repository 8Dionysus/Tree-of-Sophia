## Context

The frozen first translation-laboratory plan treated `human_only`, `ai_only`,
and `ai_human` as globally comparator-blind lanes until all drafts froze. That
was a useful early isolation rule, but it conflicts with two actual exposure
records. TOS-D-0019 preserves five years of repeatedly reading, listening to,
speaking, and memorizing Zarathustra at Work level; it does not identify the
exact Expression, Item, or retained wording. Separately, the current project
preserves a source-visible Workbench observation of the exact Antonovsky 2007
Item and page 11, although that observation is not attested as completed human
review.

Hiding the comparator in a current interface cannot remove retained memory.
Likewise, withholding comparator text from a model prompt cannot prove that a
model never encountered the translation during training. Without a correction,
future records could call an exposure-aware human revision a blind human
translation or call a run fully independent when only its current context was
isolated.

## Decision

Make translation exposure an actor- and event-scoped provenance property.
Preserve the frozen v1 plan as historical evidence, but govern future solo+AI
experiments through an additive exposure-aware plan.

For the current operator, prohibit the label `blind-human-only`. Name the
mixed lane `exposure-aware-assisted-retranslation`, disclose the prior
Work-level lived history and current-project Antonovsky exposure separately,
and keep Russian literary judgment separate from German fidelity. An AI
candidate may be called only operationally
context-isolated when comparator text, retrieval, other drafts, and current
session content were excluded and recorded; unknown model-training exposure
remains explicit. Exact comparator reveal occurs per actor only after a
candidate freeze, and later changes enter a ledger.

## Options Considered

- Keep the global sealed-comparator flag and omit historical exposure. This
  would retain a simple protocol while making the human provenance false.
- Reveal the comparator globally because the human already knows it. This
  would unnecessarily contaminate candidate-producing model contexts and
  erase a still useful operational isolation boundary.
- Abandon independent comparisons entirely. This would avoid overstating
  blindness but lose reproducible AI-only evidence and post-freeze influence
  analysis.
- Scope exposure to each actor and event, preserve the historical plan, and
  distinguish context isolation from training or memory independence. This
  retains every defensible comparison without claiming an impossible one.

## Rationale

The golden kernel should teach honest provenance, including the limits of
independence. Retranslation research treats the predecessor's influence as a
condition to study rather than a fact to conceal. Translation-process work
also shows that unaided production, post-editing, and comparison are different
evidence-generating workflows.

The selected route makes the strongest valid claims at each boundary. It
preserves an operationally isolated AI producer, the human root and Russian
literary authority that make this kernel unusual, a later comparator-analysis
actor, and an exact change history. It refuses to convert target-language
fluency, model agreement, hidden interface content, or a green validator into
German competence or accepted translation.

## Consequences

Future candidate and comparison packets must bind a per-actor exposure
snapshot. AI-only means operational context isolation only. The current mixed
lane is never an independent human baseline.

The five-year lived route must remain Work-scoped unless the operator later
identifies its actual witness history. The exact Antonovsky 2007 Item exposure
is separately evidenced by the current project and must not be inflated into a
completed review, remembered wording claim, or account of the full five years.

Dionysus may judge Russian clarity, rhythm, voice, pronounceability, literary
force, lived-reading fit, and his own comparator influence. German reading,
semantics, etymology, and fidelity remain source-facing proposals until their
actual competence gate is met.

The exact recognized comparator remains hidden from a run-specific candidate
producer until freeze even though historical human exposure is known. A
post-freeze comparison does not retroactively alter the candidate's bounded
context history; any revision after reveal is separately attributable.

A truly comparator-naive source-and-target-competent human would be an
optional external future condition. It is not scheduled under the current
solo+AI model. This decision creates no human task, model run, accepted text,
semantic promotion, graph fact, canon effect, publication grant, or server
transfer.
