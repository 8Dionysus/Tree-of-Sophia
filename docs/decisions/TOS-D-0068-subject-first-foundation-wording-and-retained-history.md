# Subject-first foundation wording with exact historical continuity

## Index Metadata

- Decision ID: TOS-D-0068
- Original date: 2026-09-22
- Surface classes: doctrine/ontology, corpus/contract, mechanics/source-revision, scripts/validation
- ToS layers: doctrine, contracts, source-witnesses, mechanics
- Tree classes: knowledge foundation, semantic interchange, human forms, provenance
- Guard families: source-first authority, exact-byte provenance, scoped delegation, human wording
- Posture: accepted within the operator-delegated foundation cleanup

## Context

The operator requested a complete personal review and cleanup of recurring
negative identity formulas in the ToS foundation, with UI work excluded.
The pattern appeared in doctrine, instructions, templates, registry definitions,
source descriptions and generated prose. Several schemas and validators
required the exact explanatory sentence, so editing a record alone would
either fail validation or allow its producer to recreate the boilerplate.

Source records, Claims and forms already have typed identity, version,
assessment, rights and operation contracts. These contracts can carry their
own conditions while descriptions explain the subject. Source quotations,
substantive disagreement, uncertainty and specific evidence limits still
belong to the material being described.

## Decision

Describe a subject through its content, purpose, properties, relations and
source-grounded examples. The authored rule lives in
`ToS/doctrine/NODE_CONTRACT.md`; local templates and authoring routes apply it.
Keep qualifications specific to the reading or use they constrain. Shared
processing and permission rules live in their owning contracts.

Treat explanatory `authority_boundary` and `claim_limit` prose as authored
nonempty text. Preserve typed statuses, permission effects, enums, exact
identities, evidence bindings and their mechanical validation. Producers emit
an explicit account of their output's scope. Optional explanatory exclusion
lists can remain empty where the typed contract already states the condition.

Correct current source descriptions and Claims through their existing owner
commands. Preserve exact predecessors, source-copy form bindings, qualified
scope, source attribution and assessment status. A delegated public
`tos_local_profile_revision_owner_v2` may correct `semantic_scope` wording
while preserving its referent and continuity criterion; its exact field grant
is required. Earlier public grants and private revision grants keep their
existing scope.

## Historical inputs and compatibility

Extend TOS-D-0052's retained-input route in two explicit ways:

- Resolve a recorded public metadata path and raw digest through the owning
  `MetadataVersionReader` and its committed revision chain. The reader verifies
  exact source bytes, identity, lineage and publication stability.
- Preserve required frozen public builder inputs at
  `ToS/research-packets/retained-builder-inputs/<script-stem>/<sha256>.py`.
  Resolution requires the original active `scripts/<name>.py` path, exact
  digest, safe path components and the 1 MiB bound. Retained code is read as
  provenance evidence. Execution continues to use current program code.

The earlier schema-history route keeps its active-path, digest and `$id`
checks. Current records still undergo current schema and source validation.
Historical events keep their original inputs, outputs, makers and timestamps.

Resource inventory generation advances to version 2 for the new descriptive
wording. Exact version-1 creation replay retains its original producer text;
the admitted generator version selects the reconstruction. This preserves
old receipts while allowing new inventories to use the current description.

## Alternatives and rationale

- Editing only current records leaves the instruction, template and producer
  sources of repetition active. The chosen change follows those dependencies.
- Removing negative words mechanically risks altering source arguments,
  scientific distinctions and actual permission checks. Each changed passage
  receives an individual reading and replacement.
- Replacing old provenance digests would misdescribe the bytes consumed by
  earlier operations. Exact retained inputs and versioned revisions preserve
  the evidence needed to interpret both old and current sources.
- Keeping exact boilerplate as schema constants couples wording to admission.
  Typed fields retain mechanical conditions; descriptive fields express the
  particular subject and operation.

## Consequences

Current descriptions become more specific and independently editable.
Historical snapshots and dated evidence retain their wording for faithful
reconstruction. Genuine source negation and technical refusal conditions
remain part of the source and program contracts.

Human or agent source-visible assessment, rights, canon and publication keep
their existing owners. This cleanup issues wording revisions and mechanical
compatibility changes. Software landing, corpus admission, data compilation
and consumer activation follow the separate routes in TOS-D-0062.
